Kaspersky's ICS CERT investigated a Russian software developer's network after the firm asked for help in June 2025. It found a months-long compromise of the company's development infrastructure and its software code-signing server. Kaspersky attributes the activity to BO Team, also tracked as Black Owl and Hoody Hyena.

The headline says the intrusion exploited Log4Shell in VMware vCenter. The source text supplied is truncated, so it gives no detail on the initial access chain, the tooling used, or what was signed with the compromised server.
