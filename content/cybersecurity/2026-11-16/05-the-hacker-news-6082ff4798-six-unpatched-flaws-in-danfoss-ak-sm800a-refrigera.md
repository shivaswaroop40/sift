Dutch consultancy Midnight Blue says six flaws in Danfoss AK-SM800A refrigeration controllers could let a remote attacker take over a device without logging in. No patch exists. The researchers say the newest firmware, version 5.4.0, does not fix the flaws.

Government cybersecurity agencies in Belgium, Ireland and Germany have told operators to act this week. The source text is cut off at that point, so the specific guidance is not available here.
