Researchers have disclosed a software supply chain attack in which Q-Dir and DesktopOK installation files served from the official SoftwareOK website were tampered with. The headline of the report says the trojanised files delivered remote access malware.

According to the researchers, malicious files were produced both when users downloaded the programs from the official download page and during the update process of copies already installed. The source text is truncated, so further detail on the payload and infection chain is not available here.
