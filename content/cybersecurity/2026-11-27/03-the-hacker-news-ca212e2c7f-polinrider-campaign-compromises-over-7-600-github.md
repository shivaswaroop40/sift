The North Korea-linked PolinRider campaign has compromised more than 7,600 GitHub repositories. In some cases the attackers planted a Python loader that can deliver next-stage payloads. Evidence suggests the activity may date back to February 2025.

Apiiro researcher Shmulik Cohen said the affected accounts belong to real developers rather than throwaway profiles. His figures put 6,541 of the 7,609 repositories, or 86%, in that group. The source text cuts off before the rest of the breakdown.
