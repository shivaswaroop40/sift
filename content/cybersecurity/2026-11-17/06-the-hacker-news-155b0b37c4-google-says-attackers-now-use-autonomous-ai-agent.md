Google reported on Monday that threat actors have moved beyond manual prompting of AI models. They are now deploying autonomous multi-agent offensive frameworks, named as ARTEX, CAIRN, CyberStrikeAI and PentAGI, to carry out multi-stage intrusions at greater speed.

The source text is truncated after saying attackers are also using large language models. The headline points to agents building and running exploits from software patches, but the supplied text gives no detail on how that works.
