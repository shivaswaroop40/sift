An attacker copied about 170 of CrowdSec's private GitHub repositories on May 22 using the account of an employee who had just left the company, CrowdSec said on September 18.

The French security firm had left his GitHub access active after his departure. CrowdSec says his laptop was compromised in the May TanStack supply chain attack, in which malicious versions of TanStack's npm packages stole credentials from developer machines.

The account access combined with credential theft gave the attacker a way into internal code. CrowdSec disclosed the incident roughly four months after it occurred.
