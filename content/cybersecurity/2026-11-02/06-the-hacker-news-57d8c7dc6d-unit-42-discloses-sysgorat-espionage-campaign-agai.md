Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 has disclosed a cyber espionage campaign against Linux infrastructure linked to government entities and state-affiliated infrastructure. The activity was observed between October 2025 and April 2026 and is tracked as CL-UNK-1211, where CL denotes cluster and UNK denotes unknown. The campaign is reported to involve SysGoRAT malware and scripts suspected of being AI-generated.

Teams running Linux for government or state-affiliated bodies are the direct target, not collateral victims of opportunistic crime. Suspected AI-generated scripts, if confirmed, would point to cheap and quickly tailored tooling. The seven-month observation window, October 2025 to April 2026, gives a defined period for retrospective log review.

The detail available is thin. It names the malware, the target set, and the observation window, but does not describe SysGoRAT's capabilities, its persistence, or how initial access was gained. The AI-generated script attribution remains a suspicion rather than a confirmed finding. The full Unit 42 report is the reference for any detection work.
