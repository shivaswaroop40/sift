TA419 has established fake professional relationships with AI policy specialists at US think tanks, universities, and legal organisations, posing as officials to build trust before exploitation.

This matters because AI policy experts have deep access to sensitive government thinking on emerging technologies. Compromising their systems or extracting their communications could expose classified policy positions and inform Chinese technical strategy.
