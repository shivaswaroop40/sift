Bell Canada has confirmed that an "agentic threat actor" breached its systems on 17 November 2026 and exfiltrated enterprise customer data. The attacker accessed a pre-production environment of Maximo, which Bell uses to order and provision telecom circuits. Bell says it contained the incident and that customer services were not affected.

The stolen data may include business contact details, ticket titles and some IP addresses tied to network elements or systems, plus limited context. Bell says credentials, highly sensitive personal information, PDFs and attachments were not in the affected dataset. It has not said how many customers were hit or how much data was taken.

Bell's use of "agentic" suggests AI-powered tools were involved, though the company has not explained how. Attribution is unknown. Bell is sharing indicators of compromise with enterprise customers and the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, but has not published them or replied to a request to do so.

Customers have been warned to watch for social engineering by people impersonating Bell.
