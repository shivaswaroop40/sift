CISA added four vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue on Friday, citing evidence of active exploitation. They affect Apache Roller, Cisco Expressway, SAP NetWeaver and Broadcom VMware vCenter.

The only entry visible in the source text is CVE-2026-129448, rated CVSS 9.8. It is described as a deserialization of untrusted data vulnerability. The text cuts off before naming the affected product, and it gives no detail on the other three flaws.
