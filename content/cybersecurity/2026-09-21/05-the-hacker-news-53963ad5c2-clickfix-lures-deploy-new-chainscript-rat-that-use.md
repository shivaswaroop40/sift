Threat actors are using ClickFix-style lures to deliver a previously undocumented RAT called ChainScript, which researchers at Blackpoint Adversary Pursuit Group have observed posing as Spotify, Zoom Workplace and Microsoft Teams installers.

The malware has appeared under multiple build names, including ComponentTask33, UpdateDigital, HostShared and OrchidViolet66, suggesting active rebranding to evade detection.

Blackpoint reports that ChainScript uses the Polygon blockchain to rotate its command and control infrastructure, a technique that makes takedowns harder for defenders.

For security teams, the combination of social engineering lures, familiar application branding and blockchain-based C2 rotation raises the bar for blocking infrastructure and identifying malicious activity.
