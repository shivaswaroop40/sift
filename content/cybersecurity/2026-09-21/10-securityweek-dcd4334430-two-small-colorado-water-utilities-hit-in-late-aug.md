Hackers targeted operational technology systems at two private Colorado water utilities in late August, each serving fewer than 200 people. A spokesperson for Governor Jared Polis said the attackers changed equipment settings, disabled remote access and alarms, and altered pumping cycles. Service and public safety were not affected, and the disruptions were brief.

The governor's office described the attacker as "foreign actors" and pointed to ongoing efforts by an Iranian-backed group against US water systems flagged by CISA. It is not confirmed that the Colorado incidents are part of the campaign that hit water facilities in at least a dozen states in July, with confirmed targets in Minnesota, Michigan, Georgia, South Dakota, New Jersey, Wisconsin, and Alabama.

CISA said it is aware of 100 internet-exposed water systems targeted in July attacks and urged the sector to secure OT. Details on the Colorado incidents remain limited, with neither the affected utilities nor forensic indicators publicly named.
