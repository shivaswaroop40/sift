A threat actor operating for roughly a year has deployed Warlock ransomware against large organisations in Spain and Portugal. The group demonstrates characteristics of both cybercrime gangs and state-associated advanced persistent threats, suggesting unclear motivations or possible state sponsorship.

For defenders, Warlock represents an unpredictable threat that does not follow typical ransomware gang targeting patterns. Understanding its tactics and indicators of compromise is essential for organisations in Europe and those with Iberian operations or supply chains.
