Monta monta.app contains four vulnerabilities affecting all versions. Missing authentication on critical functions and improper access control allow attackers to gain unauthorised administrative control or disrupt charging services. CVSS v3 rating is 9.4.

Charging infrastructure is part of critical operational technology. Compromise could allow attackers to disable charging networks or modify settings affecting availability to customers and grid stability. The impact scales with network size.
