CISA added CVE-2026-104286, a path traversal vulnerability in Fortinet FortiMail, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog based on evidence of active exploitation. Path traversal flaws are a frequent attack vector for malicious actors.

Federal agencies must prioritize rapid remediation of KEV vulnerabilities on publicly exposed assets under BOD 26-04. CISA expects these agencies to check whether attackers compromised systems before patches were applied.

CISA encourages all organisations to adopt risk-based vulnerability management and prioritise KEV remediation, regardless of regulatory status. New vulnerabilities enter the catalog when evidence of exploitation and clear mitigation guidance exist.
