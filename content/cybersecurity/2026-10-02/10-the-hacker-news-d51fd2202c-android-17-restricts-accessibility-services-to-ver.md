Google is limiting access to Android accessibility services to verified applications when Advanced Protection is enabled. The move blocks apps from requesting accessibility permissions unless they are classified as legitimate Accessibility Tools.

Malicious apps have exploited accessibility APIs as a primary vector for malware and financial fraud. This restriction removes a major attack surface by preventing unauthorised apps from gaining the broad system permissions that accessibility services provide.
