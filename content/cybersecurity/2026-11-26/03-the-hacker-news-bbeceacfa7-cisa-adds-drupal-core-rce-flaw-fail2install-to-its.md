CISA has added CVE-2026-132165, known as Fail2Install, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue. The flaw is a remote code execution bug in Drupal core with a CVSS score of 9.8. Federal agencies must apply fixes by 27 November 2026.

A KEV listing means exploitation has been observed in the wild. Anyone running Drupal core should treat this as an active threat rather than a theoretical risk. The federal deadline is a useful benchmark for your own patching timeline.

The source text is truncated after the start of the CISA description. It does not give the affected versions, the root cause, the exploit mechanism or the patched releases, so those details need checking against the original article and Drupal's advisory.
