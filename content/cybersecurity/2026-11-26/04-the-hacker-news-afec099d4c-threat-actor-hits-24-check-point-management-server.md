An unknown threat actor has compromised Check Point Security Management servers in a campaign that appears to use AI-driven offensive tooling. GreyNoise tracks the activity as GreyNoise Threat 13, or GT13. The headline figure is 24 servers, and the affected organisations number 22 across the world.

GreyNoise assesses that the targets include organisations in financial services, energy and government. The source text is cut off after that list, so further sectors, the entry method and the evidence for AI use are not available here.

Security Management servers control firewall policy for many gateways. A compromised one gives an attacker a view of, and potentially influence over, network defences. Teams running these servers should treat this as a prompt to check exposure and logs. The claim of AI involvement is described as suspected, and the detail behind it is not in the excerpt.
