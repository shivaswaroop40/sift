XBOW researchers chained three previously unknown Chrome vulnerabilities into a working full-chain attack using OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol model. The exploit runs arbitrary code on Windows when a user loads a malicious webpage. Development took six days using AI agents to automate the attack building process.

The speed and ease of construction matters to defenders because the workflow demonstrates how commodity AI models can now accelerate exploit development for high-impact bugs. The attack targets the browser rendering engine and sandbox, two critical layers most users assume protect them.

Google has confirmed the vulnerabilities, issued patches, and awarded XBOW the maximum bug bounty payout, though the exact amount was redacted in available reporting.
