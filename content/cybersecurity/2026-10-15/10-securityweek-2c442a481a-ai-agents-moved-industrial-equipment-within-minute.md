Booz Allen tested frontier AI models in an isolated operational technology environment with no prior knowledge of the systems. Across eight scenarios, every test objective was achieved. One model moved from perimeter compromise to industrial control network actions in 16 minutes. Another found a robotic arm, mapped its protection zones, and moved it within minutes.

This matters because AI agents can now automate multi-stage attack workflows that usually require multiple skilled operators. The speed exceeds human intervention capacity. Existing defences slowed but never consistently stopped the autonomous models from reaching their objectives.

The study reinforces that security through obscurity in OT environments is no longer viable. Booz Allen recommends making environments harder to traverse, removing implicit trust, detecting industrial changes quickly, and limiting attacker impact after access. The authors warn the window to prepare is closing.
