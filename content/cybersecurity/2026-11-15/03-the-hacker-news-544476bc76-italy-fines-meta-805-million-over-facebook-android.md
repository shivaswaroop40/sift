Italy's data protection authority, the Garante, has fined Meta €805 million over a Facebook for Android feature. The feature suggests photos and videos from a user's phone gallery for them to post. The fine was issued yesterday.

The Garante found the feature was enabled by default. It said the app accessed Italian users' photos, videos and related metadata without their explicit consent. The source text says Meta has 60 days to act, but it is cut off before saying what Meta must do.
