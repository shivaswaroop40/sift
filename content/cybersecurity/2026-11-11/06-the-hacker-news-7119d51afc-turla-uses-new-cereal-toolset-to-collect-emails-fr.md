ESET has reported that the Russia-aligned group Turla, also known as Snake, is using a previously undocumented toolset called Cereal. The report, shared with The Hacker News before publication, describes it as sophisticated and modular. It is used to break into victims' Microsoft Exchange servers and quietly collect emails.

The source text is truncated after this point. It does not give the infection method, the victims, the dates, the module names or any indicators of compromise. Anything beyond the Exchange targeting and email collection aim is not covered here.
