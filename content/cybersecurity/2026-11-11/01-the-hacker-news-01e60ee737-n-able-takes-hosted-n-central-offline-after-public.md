N-able has taken its hosted N-central environments offline and is telling on-premises customers to shut down their servers until further notice. The action follows public disclosure of more than 30 vulnerabilities, collectively tracked as Able2Shell. The headline of the original report refers to 33 zero-days released with proof-of-concept exploits.

N-able described the move as a precaution while it works to secure the platform. The source text is truncated after that statement, so it gives no detail on the individual flaws, severity scores, patch status or any confirmed exploitation.
