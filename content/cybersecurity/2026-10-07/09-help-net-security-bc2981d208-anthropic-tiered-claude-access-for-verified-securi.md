Anthropic expanded its Cyber Verification Program into three tiers. Defense Access permits malware analysis and vulnerability testing for security teams, nonprofits, universities, and government bodies. Red Team Access adds penetration testing for authorized teams and firms. Specialized Access, reviewed with US government, covers high-risk infrastructure like power grids and telecom networks.

The tiering matters because it creates pathways for defenders to use advanced Claude capabilities with fewer blocks, while maintaining controls against misuse. Real-time blocks remain for actions causing physical harm or widespread disruption. Anthropic benchmarked Claude Opus 5.5 against CyScenarioBench: without access, all 50 trials were blocked; under Red Team Access, none encountered blocks and 34 of 50 completed.

Between April and July 2026, Project Glasswing partners used Claude to find at least 129,000 verified vulnerabilities, with over 33,000 rated critical or high severity. Anthropic found another 5,500 through open-source scanning. The program requires data retention for misuse monitoring, though Enterprise Frontier Safeguards will allow zero data retention options.
