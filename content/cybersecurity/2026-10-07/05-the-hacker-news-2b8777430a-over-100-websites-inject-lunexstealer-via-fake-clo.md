CERT-UA identified more than 100 compromised websites delivering LunexStealer, an information-stealing malware, through malicious JavaScript injection. The activity was attributed to threat cluster UAC-0277 and observed in September 2026. Attackers used fake Cloudflare verification checks to distribute the stealer.

The technique is significant because legitimate-looking Cloudflare challenges lower user suspicion and increase infection success rates. Compromised websites act as distribution vectors, potentially affecting many users who trust the underlying sites.
