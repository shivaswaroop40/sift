Threat actors deployed backdoors on Linux telecom and network appliances in South Korea and Taiwan. The malware disguises itself as email security software and legitimate system processes to avoid triggering alerts during execution and network communication.

Detection evasion through process impersonation is effective because security teams expect certain binaries to run and certain services to make outbound connections. A backdoor named after legitimate software blends into normal traffic, making it harder to spot during log review or network monitoring.
