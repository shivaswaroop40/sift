CISA added CVE-2026-76504 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue after reports of active exploitation. The flaw affects Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager with a CVSS score of 9.8. An unauthenticated remote attacker can exploit it to gain access to affected systems.

Organisations running Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager must treat this as a priority remediation target. The critical severity combined with confirmed active exploitation and unauthenticated attack vector means hostile actors are likely scanning for and compromising exposed instances.
