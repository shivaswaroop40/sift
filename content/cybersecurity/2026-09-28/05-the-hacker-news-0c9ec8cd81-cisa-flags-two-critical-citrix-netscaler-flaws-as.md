CISA has added two critical vulnerabilities in Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after reports of active exploitation. One flaw, CVE-2026-88771, carries a CVSS score of 9.5 and stems from improper input validation that could allow unauthenticated attackers to take action against the device.

Both flaws affect widely deployed Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway appliances, which sit at the network edge and are commonly used for remote access and application delivery. An unauthenticated remote code execution or memory disclosure flaw on these devices gives attackers a direct path into enterprise networks without needing credentials.

Affected organisations should patch immediately or remove vulnerable instances from production. CISA KEV listing typically imposes a remediation deadline for federal agencies and is treated as a priority signal across the wider security community.
