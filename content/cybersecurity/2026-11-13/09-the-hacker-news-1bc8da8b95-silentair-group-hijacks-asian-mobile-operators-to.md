Enea, a Stockholm-based telecom and cybersecurity software company, has named a new spying group called SilentAir. The group used compromised equipment inside Asian mobile operators to intercept the calls and texts of selected people, mostly in Taiwan.

Enea found attack sources in at least six Asian countries. It assessed with high confidence that SilentAir is a state-level actor. The source text is truncated, so it gives no detail on the technique, the equipment involved, or the operators affected.
