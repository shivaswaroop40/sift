Security firm watchTowr reported on 26 September that two unpatched remote code execution zero-day vulnerabilities in Citrix NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway are being actively exploited in the wild.

Citrix has not confirmed the flaws and has not published a patch. Some administrators have pulled appliances offline rather than wait for a fix, indicating the seriousness of the reported exploitation.

The source text is truncated, so technical details such as CVE identifiers, affected versions, and the exploitation mechanism are not available. Practitioners should treat the report as preliminary until Citrix issues an advisory.
