Attackers are actively exploiting a critical SSRF vulnerability in SonicWall SMA 1000 appliances. The flaw allows server-side request forgery attacks against affected devices currently in use.

This matters because SMA appliances often sit at network edges, providing remote access for enterprises. Successful exploitation could allow attackers to reach internal systems behind the appliance or gather sensitive information.

SonicWall customers have faced repeated critical flaws in these devices. The active exploitation means patching should be treated as urgent priority.
