On 11 October, attackers gained remote access to Danfoss refrigeration controllers at hundreds of French supermarkets and raised temperatures to 50°C, forcing stores to discard stock. The controllers are used by maintenance firms to manage cooling systems across entire store locations.

Danfoss controllers are internet-accessible devices designed for remote management by authorised technicians. Compromise of these systems gives attackers direct influence over critical food-storage infrastructure, with immediate financial and operational impact.

The incident affects multiple retail chains and demonstrates the vulnerability of industrial systems that prioritise remote access for maintenance efficiency over network isolation.
