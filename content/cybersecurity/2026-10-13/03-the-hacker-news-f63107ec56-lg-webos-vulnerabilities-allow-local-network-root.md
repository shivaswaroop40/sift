Three chained vulnerabilities in LG webOS enable an attacker on the local network to gain root access to affected TVs. No PIN or user interaction is required. The chain was demonstrated on the 32LQ636BPSA and 27TQ600SW models running webOS 7.5.7-25.

An unauthenticated local attacker can compromise a TV completely, potentially accessing connected accounts, viewing camera feeds if available, or using the device as a pivot point into the home network. LG has released patches as of October 12, but vulnerable devices on older firmware versions remain at risk.
