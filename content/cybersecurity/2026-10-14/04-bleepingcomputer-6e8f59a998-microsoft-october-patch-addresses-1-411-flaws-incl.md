Microsoft has released security updates fixing 1,411 vulnerabilities in this month's Patch Tuesday. Three of these are zero-days: one actively exploited in the wild and two that are publicly disclosed but not yet widely exploited.

This volume represents a significant patching load for platform engineers managing Windows and Microsoft application deployments. The actively exploited zero-day poses immediate risk to unpatched systems in production.

The three zero-days demand prioritised testing and rollout ahead of routine patch schedules. Organisations should begin vulnerability scanning and impact assessment immediately to identify affected assets.
