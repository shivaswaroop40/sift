An attacker chained three zero-day flaws in Ivanti Connect Secure VPN appliances to execute commands as root without authentication, Volexity disclosed on October 13. Ivanti has patched two of the three vulnerabilities; the third remains unpatched.

This matters because Connect Secure is widely deployed in enterprise networks as a remote access gateway. An unauthenticated root compromise allows attackers to move laterally, exfiltrate data, and maintain persistence across the VPN boundary.

Ivanti instructed customers on affected versions to take immediate action, but organisations running unpatched instances remain exposed until the third fix arrives.
