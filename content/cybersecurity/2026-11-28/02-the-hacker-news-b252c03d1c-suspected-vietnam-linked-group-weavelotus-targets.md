SOCRadar has attributed a previously undocumented activity set, codenamed WeaveLotus, to a Vietnam-sponsored hacking group. The group is said to target military, government and legislative organisations in Southeast Asia. Its aim is to gather intelligence on South China Sea defence and military agreements.

The activity is assessed to include five intrusions in Thailand. The source text is truncated after that point, so details of other countries, tooling, initial access and timing are not available here.
