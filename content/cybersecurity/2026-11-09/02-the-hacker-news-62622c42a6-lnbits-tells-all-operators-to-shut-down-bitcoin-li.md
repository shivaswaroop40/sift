The LNbits project has told everyone running its free, open-source Bitcoin Lightning wallet system to shut down their servers now because of a wave of attacks. Several security fixes are due to be released, and operators have been asked to be careful until they are out. One operator said new accounts used an exploit to withdraw 2,285,545 satoshis, about 0.023 bitcoin, from his server.

Anyone operating LNbits faces active theft rather than a theoretical flaw. The reported loss came through newly created accounts, which points at the account creation and withdrawal flow as the attack path. Because the fixes are not out yet, the project's stated mitigation is simply to stop running the service until the patches land.

The report does not name the vulnerability, describe the fixes, or say how many operators have been hit. The scope of the campaign is therefore unknown. With no technical detail published, there is nothing to build a partial mitigation around beyond taking servers offline and watching for the patch release.
