Vulnerabilities in middleware used by SWIFT banking and government systems allow remote code execution. The flaws affect ultra-sensitive infrastructure that processes financial transactions and government communications.

These vulnerabilities matter because they sit at the junction between security hardware and software. An attacker who achieves RCE can potentially bypass hardware-based MFA protections that would normally block lateral movement or credential theft.

Patches are available and organisations should apply them immediately to production SWIFT environments, given the critical nature of the systems and the direct path to code execution.
