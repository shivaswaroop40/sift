CISA added CVE-2026-102489 and CVE-2026-102490 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after confirming active exploitation. Both affect Zammad GmbH's platform: one enables session fixation, the other allows improper privilege escalation.

These vulnerabilities matter because they grant total control of affected systems post-exploitation. Federal agencies must now prioritize patching them under Binding Operational Directive 26-04, which requires rapid remediation of high-risk KEV catalog entries on publicly exposed assets.

CISA recommends all organisations adopt risk-based vulnerability management and check whether systems were compromised before patching. The agency continues adding vulnerabilities to the catalog when evidence of active exploitation emerges.
