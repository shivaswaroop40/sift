TA419, a China-nexus group, has conducted phishing campaigns against AI experts at U.S. think tanks, universities, and legal organisations. The attacks impersonated prominent economists, AI policymakers, and staff from Anthropic to compromise credentials.

TA419's targeting of AI policy specialists indicates state-level interest in understanding U.S. AI governance and strategy. Compromised accounts at policy institutions could grant access to unreleased research, policy drafts, and internal deliberations on AI regulation.

The group uses Microsoft Account-in-the-Middle (AitM) phishing, a technique that intercepts authentication to bypass multi-factor authentication protections.
