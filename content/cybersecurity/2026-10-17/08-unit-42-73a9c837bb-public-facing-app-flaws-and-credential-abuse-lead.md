Unit 42 tracked five primary initial access vectors in September 2026. Exploitation of public-facing applications ranked first, with attackers targeting exposed web applications, databases, and perimeter services. Valid credentials and credential abuse ranked second, with adversaries using harvested or sprayed authentication material often combined with edge vulnerabilities.

These findings matter because they show attackers still rely on external infrastructure compromise and identity theft as reliable entry points. Your perimeter defences must treat public-facing application security as a critical control.

Phishing and social engineering ranked third, targeting public sector and defence environments. Supply chain compromise and drive-by compromises rounded out the top five, each posing systemic risk across interconnected systems.
