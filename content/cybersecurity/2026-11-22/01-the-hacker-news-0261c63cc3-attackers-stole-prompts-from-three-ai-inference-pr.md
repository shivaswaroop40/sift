Dragos said attackers took data from inside three AI inference providers over several months. None of the three providers knew about it until researchers told them. The headline says OpenRouter dropped the three providers as a result.

Some of the stolen requests were flagged as Zero Data Retention, the setting meant to ensure a prompt is never stored. OpenRouter is a gateway that routes customers' AI requests to a pool of providers.

The source text is truncated after that point. It does not say how the attackers got in, which providers were affected, what data was taken, or how many customers were exposed.
