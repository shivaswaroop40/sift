CISA added two vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog on 25 September 2026 based on evidence of active exploitation. The entries are CVE-2026-65660, a code injection flaw in Microsoft SharePoint, and CVE-2026-67279, an improper enforcement of behavioural workflow issue in MikroTik RouterOS.

Federal Civilian Executive Branch agencies must remediate KEV-listed flaws on internet-facing assets under Binding Operational Directive 26-04, which also sets expectations for checking whether systems were compromised before a patch was applied. CISA encourages private organisations to follow the same risk-based prioritisation.

Neither CISA's alert nor the catalog excerpts include exploit details, impact metrics, or patch version information in the public text. Practitioners should consult vendor advisories for Microsoft SharePoint and MikroTik RouterOS to confirm affected versions, fixed builds, and any required workarounds before scheduling remediation.

Organisations aware of an actively exploited flaw not yet listed can submit it through CISA's KEV Nomination Form, provided they have a CVE ID, evidence of exploitation, and clear mitigation guidance.
