Kiteworks has emailed customers urging them to shut down the platform during a six-hour window on Saturday, after receiving what it called credible threat intelligence from federal intelligence authorities about an attempt to target customer systems. The CISO Frank Balonis said the advice is preventative and that the firm is unaware of any compromise.

No CVE, patch or technical details have been published. A Kiteworks support official told Heise the email was prompted by a potential zero-day, and the company said all known flaws are addressed in release 9.5.1. The FBI declined to comment and CISA did not respond.

Kiteworks was previously Accellion, whose file transfer appliance was hit by the Russian-linked Clop group using a zero-day in 2020 to steal data from dozens of organisations including Kroger and Bombardier. Researcher Jake Knott of watchTowr called the request to unplug production systems over a weekend unusual and concerning.
