Three research teams remotely exploited fully patched Google Pixel 10 phones at the Pwn2Own Ireland contest on October 8. Ikotas Labs won the top prize of $300,000 for their working exploit, also earning overall contest winner status.

For platform defenders, this demonstrates that current patches do not prevent all remote code execution paths on modern flagship devices. Each successful exploit represents a gap between public patch release and actual closure of attack surface.

Pwn2Own participants submit their findings to vendors after demonstration, enabling the vendor to develop further mitigations. The contest rules mandate fully patched systems to reflect real deployment conditions rather than theoretical vulnerabilities.
