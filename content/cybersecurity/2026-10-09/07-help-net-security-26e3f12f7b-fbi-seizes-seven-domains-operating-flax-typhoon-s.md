The FBI disrupted infrastructure used by Flax Typhoon, a Chinese state-sponsored group working through contractor Integrity Technology Group. Seven domains were seized that hosted Microscan, a network scanning tool, and FishHub, a phishing and malware delivery platform. The tools targeted critical infrastructure, airports, universities and power companies across the US, Taiwan, Japan and Poland.

Microscan identified vulnerabilities for later exploitation. FishHub delivered malware that either granted remote access or exfiltrated files. This represents the second disruption of Integrity Tech's operations in two years, following the September 2024 takedown of a Mirai botnet controlling over 200,000 devices.

Organisations should disable unused services and ports, apply patches promptly, enforce multi-factor authentication across all services, and sanitise web application inputs to prevent injection attacks. The advisory names specific attack vectors including password spraying on Exchange servers and cross-site scripting.
