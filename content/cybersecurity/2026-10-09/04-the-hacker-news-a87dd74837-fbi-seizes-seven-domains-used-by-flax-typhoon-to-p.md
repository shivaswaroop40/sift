The FBI and Department of Justice have seized seven domains used by Flax Typhoon, a China-linked advanced persistent threat group, to scan and infiltrate US critical infrastructure systems. The operation blocked access to platforms the group relied on for reconnaissance and initial compromise.

This matters because Flax Typhoon targets operational technology in electricity, water, and communications sectors. Disrupting their infrastructure tooling reduces their ability to conduct network reconnaissance and establish persistence in defended environments.
