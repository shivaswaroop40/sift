Chinese government-linked threat actors supported by Integrity Technology Group are using automated scanning tools, large-scale botnets, and manual exploitation techniques to target organisations worldwide, including US critical infrastructure sectors, for data theft.

Defence teams face a hybrid attack model that combines speed of automated reconnaissance with the precision of hands-on operators, making detection harder and increasing dwell time opportunities.
