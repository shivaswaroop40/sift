The FBI has issued a flash alert on Artex, an open-source AI penetration testing framework that turns any large language model into an agent-driven intrusion tool. It converts plain-language goals into machine commands. The FBI says some intrusions went from initial targeting to privileged access in under three hours.

Artex was published on GitHub in August and later removed after the developer said it had been abused. The FBI says forks, archived copies and a commercial version keep it available. Censys found over 1,600 exposed Artex consoles, with real deployments likely far higher.

It works with models including Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, GLM, Grok and Kimi. The FBI says Chinese models dominate attacker-controlled instances, with DeepSeek Flash models favoured for cost and a lack of observable guardrails. Targets span finance, government, energy, healthcare, defence and legal sectors.
