A 3AM ransomware affiliate breached a European school. Evidence from the incident suggests an AI model may have generated and revised commands during the intrusion. Researchers who reviewed the evidence stopped short of claiming full automation, so the model's exact role remains unconfirmed.

The claim matters because command generation and revision mid-intrusion would make attacker behaviour less predictable. Detection that relies on matching known scripts or fixed command patterns would struggle against it. The evidence is thin, though. Treat this as an early signal rather than a proven shift in ransomware operations.
