Binance said on Tuesday that the record theft of $6.43 billion in assets from the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange was the result of a sophisticated, multi-stage campaign. The campaign combined a tampered Homebrew installation with a recruitment-themed social engineering attack targeting two of its engineers. The company said it was not a single isolated breach or a simple phishing incident.

Both entry vectors aim at the people and tools that run production, not at end users. Homebrew is the standard package manager on macOS, and a tampered installation means the attackers subverted routine developer tooling that is trusted by default. Recruitment-themed approaches reach privileged engineers directly. Each vector lands on a machine with production access, behind the perimeter.

One element awaits detail. The report's headline also cites abuse of AI tools, but the available text does not explain how AI fitted into the campaign. The route from two compromised engineers to a $6.43 billion loss is likewise not yet described. Defenders will want the middle of that chain most.
