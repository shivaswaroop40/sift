CISA, the FBI, the Department of Health and Human Services, the USDA and the Department of Transportation have issued a joint advisory. They report malicious cyber activity targeting industrial refrigeration and cold chain monitoring systems globally. The affected sectors include Healthcare and Public Health, Chemical, and Food and Agriculture. The agencies urge organisations to harden these systems.

For defenders, this confirms active targeting of operational technology in the cold chain. The named sectors, including healthcare, chemicals, and food and agriculture, depend on reliable refrigeration, where loss of temperature control has direct operational impact. Engineers in these sectors should read the agencies' hardening recommendations and check whether their refrigeration and monitoring deployments are in scope.
