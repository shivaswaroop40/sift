Zohar Pinhasi, owner of MonsterCloud, has been charged with wire fraud for billing victims approximately $19M while secretly paying ransomware operators to decrypt their data, rather than using legitimate recovery tools as claimed.

This matters because it reveals a business model that actively incentivised ransomware attacks. By paying attackers directly, Pinhasi funded future operations whilst presenting himself as a legitimate recovery service. Victims believed they were funding data recovery, not criminal networks.
