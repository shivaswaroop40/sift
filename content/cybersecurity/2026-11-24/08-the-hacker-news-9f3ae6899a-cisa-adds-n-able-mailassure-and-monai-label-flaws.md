CISA added two critical vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Monday, citing evidence of active exploitation. They affect N-able MailAssure and MONAI Label. One is CVE-2026-134624, scored 9.3 on CVSS, a server-side request forgery flaw in MailAssure's LDAP component.

The source text is truncated after that point. It does not give the CVE for MONAI Label, the affected versions, patch status, or details of how attackers are exploiting either flaw. The headline refers to AI agents exploiting a MONAI Label zero-day, but the supplied text does not confirm this.
