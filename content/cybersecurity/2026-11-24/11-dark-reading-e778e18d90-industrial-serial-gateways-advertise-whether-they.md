Dark Reading reports that industrial serial gateways, which give network access to operational technology devices, often reveal whether authentication is enabled. Anyone who queries them can tell. The source states that many of these devices are not protected by authentication.

The source text provided is only a short standfirst, so it gives no device counts, vendors, protocols or exposure figures. It does not say how the devices disclose their status or how many are reachable from the internet.
