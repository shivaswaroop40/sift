Broadcom has released patches for 23 vulnerabilities across VMware ESX, vCenter, Workstation, Fusion and Avi Load Balancer. One is critical. CVE-2026-123903 is rated 9.8 out of 10.0 on CVSS and could be used to bypass authentication. It sits in the VMware Directory Service, known as vmdir.
