A cyber-espionage campaign using PapRAT malware is hitting organisations in South Asia. Dark Reading attributes the activity to a known India-linked threat group and reports that attacks in the region are growing. The operators use three distinct attack chains, each built on legitimate Windows functionality.

Attacks that lean on built-in Windows features are hard to distinguish from normal administrative activity, so signature-based defences tend to miss them. Three separate chains also mean several entry paths to cover. Teams with exposure in South Asia should check whether their telemetry can flag unusual use of native Windows tooling.
