N-able has taken its N-central remote monitoring and management platform offline after the discovery of 33 flaws. The company also released a hotfix as one of its recommended mitigations. Public exploits are said to exist for alleged zero-days that would let an attacker take over the platform.

N-central is an RMM tool, so a compromise can give an attacker a route into the many customer environments it manages. Managed service providers and their clients are the obvious exposure. Public exploit code shortens the time between disclosure and attack.

The source text is only a short summary. It does not name the affected versions, say which of the 33 flaws are exploited, or detail the other mitigations. Those points need checking against N-able's own advisory before you act.
