A suspected Russian-speaking attacker used AI to develop exploits for two recently disclosed PaperCut NG/MF vulnerabilities and broke into more than 440 instances. Independent reports from Blackpoint Cyber and GreyNoise tie the activity to IP address 45.142.193[.]132. The original reporting describes the operation as using hundreds of AI agents.

For defenders, this is an early example of AI being used at scale to turn known vulnerabilities into working exploits. The scale of compromise suggests the attacker automated reconnaissance and exploitation, so patching PaperCut servers quickly is the main mitigation. The IP address can be used for hunting, but attribution to a specific actor remains uncertain.
