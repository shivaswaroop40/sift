From 1 October, Hyatt replaced its flat 500-point-per-night new-hotel bonus with a double-points offer. You must book a specific promotional rate to qualify, and only earn bonus points on base points earned, capped at 25,000 per stay.

For independent travellers booking new properties, the maths can cut both ways. A Portland airport hotel makes the premium rate worthwhile at 1.6 cents per point. The luxury Park Hyatt in Mexico requires paying 2.6 cents per point—above market value unless you need flexible cancellation.

The offer excludes members redeeming points or using free night certificates, removing a key reason to check Hyatt's new-hotel list.
