Southwest Airlines has opened summer 2027 bookings and added 23 new or returning routes alongside frequency increases across its network. Three airports dominate the expansion: Austin gains seven new tropical destinations including Costa Rica and Nassau; Nashville grows to 220 daily departures with Caribbean and Mountain West additions; San Diego reaches 50 destinations with new Hawaii links.

For independent travellers, this matters because Southwest is reshaping where you can go without connections. Austin and San Diego are becoming increasingly competitive against Delta, which means more nonstop options to leisure destinations. Nashville is transitioning toward a connecting hub, offering better itinerary combinations for longer journeys.

Southwest's expansion reflects strategic competition: Austin requires growth to defend against Delta's rapid buildup; Nashville's investments in a 30,200 square-foot lounge signal hub-like operations; San Diego leverages its position as both a leisure market and Hawaii gateway. These three airports show Southwest's network strategy moving beyond traditional point-to-point service.
