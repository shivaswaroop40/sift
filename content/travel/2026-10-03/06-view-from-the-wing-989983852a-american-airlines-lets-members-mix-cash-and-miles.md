American Airlines is rolling out a cash and miles booking option for AAdvantage members on domestic flights. You select a ticket price in dollars, then use a slider to apply miles during checkout to reduce the cash portion. The airline has not disclosed how much value each mile receives in this redemption.

This matters because miles are your most flexible reward currency. If American values them too low, cash and miles bookings could be a poor use of your balance. The airline declined to reveal its calculation method or provide the baseline price in its own example.

Based on American's Instant Upgrade pricing, which values miles around one cent each, cash and miles redemptions will likely deliver similar poor returns. The feature rolls out over coming weeks on the website and app, initially for domestic bookings excluding Alaska and Hawaii.
