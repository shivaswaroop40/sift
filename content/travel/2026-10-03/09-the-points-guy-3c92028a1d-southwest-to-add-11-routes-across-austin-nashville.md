Southwest Airlines announced an 11-route expansion starting June 2027, with nine routes the airline has never flown before. New service includes Caribbean and Central American destinations from Austin, island routes from Nashville, and Deep South connections from San Diego. The expansion runs through early August.

For independent travellers, this matters because it opens new gateway cities and Caribbean access. Austin will see 143 daily departures at peak, Nashville 220. Both airports are also getting Southwest's first dedicated lounges, changing the loyalty value proposition.

Most routes run seasonally during peak summer. Austin receives seven new or returning routes, making it Southwest's largest-ever summer schedule there, despite recent competition from Delta.
