Qantas' Project Sunrise aircraft, the Airbus A350-1000ULR, carries a unique 20,000-litre rear centre fuel tank integrated into the fuselage. This addition raises total fuel capacity from 156,000 to almost 180,000 litres, enabling nonstop routes of nearly 10,000 nautical miles from Australia to London or New York by late 2028.

The tank forces significant trade-offs. Seating drops to 240 passengers, 24 percent below standard A350-1000 layouts. Maximum takeoff weight rises sharply, requiring reinforced landing gear, modified winglets, software upgrades to fuel systems, and more powerful Rolls-Royce Trent XWB-97 engines rated at 97,000 pounds of thrust.

No other airline has ordered this tank configuration, making certification complex. Airbus and Qantas have spent years on ground and flight testing. First aircraft arrive early 2027, with commercial flights targeted for October 2027.
