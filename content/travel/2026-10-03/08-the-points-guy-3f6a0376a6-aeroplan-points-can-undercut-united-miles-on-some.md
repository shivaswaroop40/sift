Air Canada's Aeroplan programme and United MileagePlus both use dynamic pricing for United-operated awards. You can often book the same United flights through Aeroplan for fewer points than United charges, particularly on short-haul flights during peak travel and some transatlantic business-class routes.

This matters because Aeroplan accepts transfers from American Express Membership Rewards and Capital One miles, which do not transfer directly to MileagePlus. The comparison tool Seats.aero lets you check prices in both programmes before committing points.

Aeroplan's North America short-haul starts at 6,000 points each way in economy for flights up to 500 miles. Transatlantic business-class to the Atlantic zone begins at 60,000 points for up to 4,000 miles. Booking online through Aeroplan costs a CA$39 partner fee per ticket.
