Point.me has launched a tool that finds award flights, transfers points between accounts and completes bookings automatically. The system integrates with banks and loyalty programmes offering transfer partners, handling signup, points movement and redemption without user intervention.

This matters because points transfer arbitrage has long required manual legwork across multiple websites and accounts. The automation makes transfer strategies viable for ordinary members, not just optimisers, and puts point.me in a different category from basic award search tools.

Programme support and adoption remain limited. Some issuers may resist the tool because points transfers typically generate higher costs than redemptions through partner retailers like Amazon.
