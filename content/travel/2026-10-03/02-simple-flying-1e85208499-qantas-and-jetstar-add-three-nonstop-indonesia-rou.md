Australia and Indonesia have updated their bilateral air services agreement for the first time in 15 years, unlocking 5,000 additional weekly seats. Qantas will launch Perth to Jakarta on 737-800s from December 2026 and Brisbane to Bali from August 2027, both daily. Jetstar will start daily Bali service from the new Western Sydney airport from March 2027.

These routes matter because Bali is already visited by 1.6 million Australians annually, and demand consistently outpaces capacity on existing services. The new flights offer independent travellers cheaper alternatives to premium carriers and spread access beyond the five existing capital-city routes to Bali.

Melbourne to Jakarta will shift from A330-200 to A321XLR aircraft to go daily instead of four times weekly, compressing the timeline to March-October 2027. Qantas will use 4,500 of the 5,000 permitted seats, with full capacity expected by November 2027.
