British Airways has ended three long-haul routes from London Heathrow in 2026: Abu Dhabi (suspended from March), Jeddah (suspended from April) and Orlando (a short summer trial that has not returned to the schedule). Bahrain is suspended until March 2027 due to the Iran war, rather than removed.

The cuts hit routes that compete with Gulf carriers or carry relatively few premium travellers. Jeddah was described as much lower-yielding than Riyadh, where BA plans to run two daily flights next summer alongside Riyadh Air and Saudia. Abu Dhabi is now paused until winter 2027/2028 at the earliest.

For travellers, LHR-JED is now down to Saudia's daily or twice-daily service. Five daily flights to Riyadh are scheduled from Heathrow next summer. A one-off BA A350 service to Orlando on 2 November is on sale, with no inbound leg published.

BA still operates around 80 long-haul departures a day at Heathrow, about a third of the airport's total. The carrier has not confirmed whether Orlando will repeat in 2027 or whether Jeddah and Abu Dhabi will resume at all.
