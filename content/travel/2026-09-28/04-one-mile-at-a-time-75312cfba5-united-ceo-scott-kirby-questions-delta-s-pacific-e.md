United CEO Scott Kirby has publicly questioned Delta's growing trans-Pacific ambitions, as Delta adds routes from Los Angeles including Hong Kong and Manila.

Delta executives have stated a goal of overtaking United on Pacific long haul flying, and are positioning LAX as a long haul hub to close the gap.

Kirby's comments carry weight because United holds a deep network on Pacific routes from its hubs in San Francisco, Los Angeles and Tokyo, giving it scale advantages Delta lacks.

For travellers, the rivalry matters if it produces more flight options or lower fares on US-Asia routes, though Delta's LAX build-up is still early and its competitive position remains unproven.
