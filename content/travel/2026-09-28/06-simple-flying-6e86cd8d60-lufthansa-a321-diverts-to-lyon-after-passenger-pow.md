A Lufthansa Airbus A321-200, registration D-AISB, diverted Frankfurt to Lisbon flight LH1170 to Lyon on 26 September after a passenger's power bank overheated and caught fire in a seat. The crew declared an air emergency and landed at 9:55 pm local time, where the device was placed in a lithium-safe bag.

The diversion matters because Lufthansa already bans power bank use in flight and has tightened carriage rules, yet incidents are still happening mid-cabin. Travellers carrying spare batteries should expect stricter handling and possible knock-on delays if a similar diversion occurs.

Lufthansa rebooked the 198 passengers and six crew on hotel accommodation overnight. D-AISB repositioned to Frankfurt the next day as LH9923, while passengers reached Lisbon later via TAP Air Portugal and easyJet services from Lyon.

Under current Lufthansa rules, power banks must travel in hand luggage only, capped at two per person and 100 Wh each, cannot be used or charged in flight, and must not be stowed in overhead lockers.
