Southwest credits issued from 28 May 2025 now expire, with Basic fares getting just six months and no transfer rights. Credits from tickets booked before that date keep their original non-expiring status.

Travellers with a soon-to-expire credit can book a Choice Preferred or Choice Extra fare, pay with the credit plus a small gift card, then cancel. Southwest returns a Transferable Flight Credit with no expiry date. The recipient must hold a Rapid Rewards account.

Reports from customers across June and July show the method works, including with $15 gift cards. The refundable fare class appears to be what triggers the change. There is no guarantee the loophole will stay open.

Southwest accepts up to three payments per booking, so two credits plus a gift card can be combined. LUV vouchers behave differently and cannot cover taxes alone. Combining credits in this way inherits the earliest expiry, so do the gift card step last.
