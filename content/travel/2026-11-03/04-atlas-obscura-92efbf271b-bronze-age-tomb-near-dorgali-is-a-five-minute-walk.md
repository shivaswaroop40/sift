The Tomba Dei Gigante S'Ena e Thimes is a large Bronze Age tomb near Dorgali, Italy, about five minutes' walk from the SP 38 road. Stone arrows laid on the ground mark the route, and each one is a unique shape. The front face consists of several very large stones set upright. The central stone has an opening at its base, through which you can look straight into the tomb.

For an independent traveller, the appeal is access. You can look into the tomb through the opening in the central stone, then reach its back by slipping between two smaller stones a couple of metres to the right of the front. The stone arrows on the ground make the walk from the road easy to follow. The entry mentions no fee or guide.
