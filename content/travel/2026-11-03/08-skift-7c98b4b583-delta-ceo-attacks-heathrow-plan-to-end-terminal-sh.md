Delta Air Lines CEO Ed Bastian has attacked Heathrow's plan to move the airline from Terminal 3 to Terminal 4 in 2028. Virgin Atlantic, its joint venture partner, would stay at Terminal 3. In a LinkedIn post, Bastian called the proposals "short-sighted, deeply disappointing and bad for airline consumers." His intervention escalates a dispute that has already drawn strong statements from Virgin Atlantic executives and founder Sir Richard Branson.

The two carriers have shared Terminal 3 for more than a decade. Bastian says the co-located operation gives travellers easy connections and access to a wider global network. If the split goes ahead, anyone booked across the Delta and Virgin Atlantic partnership would have to change terminals at Heathrow to transfer between flights. The move is not due until 2028, so the plan could still change under this pressure.
