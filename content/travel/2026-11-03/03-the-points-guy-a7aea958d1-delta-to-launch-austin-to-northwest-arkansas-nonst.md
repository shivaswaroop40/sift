Delta has filed plans for nonstop flights from Austin to Northwest Arkansas National Airport, which serves Bentonville and Walmart's corporate headquarters. Service starts on 7 March 2027 aboard an Embraer 175 operated by SkyWest. The outbound leaves Austin just before 9 p.m., with the return the next morning. XNA is also the closest airport for the University of Arkansas, and parts of Oklahoma, Missouri and Kansas sit within a short drive.

The route extends a rapid build-up at Austin, Delta's fastest-growing focus city. The carrier plans up to 70 daily departures from AUS by March, compared with 28 peak daily departures in 2019. Its first nonstop from Austin to Europe, to Paris, begins in April. More passengers will start seeing itineraries connecting through Austin, though Delta stops short of calling it a hub.

Delta already serves XNA from five hubs, namely Atlanta, Detroit, Minneapolis, Salt Lake City and LaGuardia. Southwest remains the market leader at Austin, and both carriers plan their biggest-ever schedules there in 2027. Delta is also planning a second, larger Sky Club at AUS. XNA is growing too, with 28% more flights this year than two years ago, according to Cirium data.
