American Express Membership Rewards has launched a 30% transfer bonus to Virgin Atlantic Flying Club. The offer starts on November 2. The available text cuts off before the end date, so the full window is unclear. The author calls Flying Club niche in some ways, but argues the bonus has the potential to offer outsized value.

For anyone holding Amex points, the mechanism is simple. A 30% bonus means every 1,000 points lands as 1,300 Flying Club miles. The rate is not the best this pairing has seen. The author would otherwise transfer speculatively, but moved a large batch of points during a 40% bonus last year. If you have a redemption in mind, the bonus adds a third to your balance. If not, a bigger offer has appeared before.
