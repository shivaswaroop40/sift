United and American are contesting gate space at Chicago O'Hare. American sued the city in May 2025 over a gate redetermination that shifted space towards United. It failed to halt the transfer in federal court and later in Cook County Chancery Court. American's Terminal 3 footprint stood at 66 gates as of 1 April 2026, while United holds roughly 95 to 99.

Both carriers also bought Spirit Airlines gates on Concourse G during its restructuring. United paid $30.2 million for G12 and G14. American paid $30 million for G8 and G10, next to an existing Admirals Club lounge. United has expanded its 2026 schedule, and American has added flights to defend its share.

The article says spring 2026 schedule growth collided with airfield construction, and the FAA issued scheduling limits that trimmed peak-hour plans. The source text is cut off at that point, so the details of the caps are not available.
