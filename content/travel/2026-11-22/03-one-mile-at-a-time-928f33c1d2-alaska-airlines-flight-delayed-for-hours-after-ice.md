An Alaska Airlines flight at Seattle was delayed by hours after ICE agents went onto the jet bridge of a departing flight to arrest a Mexican national. The report says he allegedly had a work permit and had overstayed his visa. The arrest turned into a scuffle on the jet bridge.

More arrests followed. ICE claims someone threw hot coffee at the officers. The flight was held for hours because the crew was no longer fit to fly. The Port of Seattle is mentioned as responding, but the text is cut off before any detail.
