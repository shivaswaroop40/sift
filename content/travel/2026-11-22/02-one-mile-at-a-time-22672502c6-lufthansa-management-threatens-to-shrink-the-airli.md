One Mile at a Time reports that Lufthansa management is threatening to shrink the airline substantially and lay off employees. The piece describes Lufthansa as the flagship of Lufthansa Group but the least profitable airline in the group.

The source says 2026 was meant to be a turnaround year, and the carrier's 100th anniversary. Earlier in the year Lufthansa faced major labour problems, including repeated strikes. The text says senior management had recently claimed it wanted to turn over a new leaf.
