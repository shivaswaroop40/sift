The US Department of Transportation's November report shows United last for mishandled bags, at 6.9 per 1,000 in the first nine months, against American's 6.6 per 1,000 and Delta's 3.6. It also finished last in July, August and September. On mainline flights alone, American is still worse, at 7.2 against United's 6.6.

Every listed airline cancelled a larger share of flights than a year earlier. Delta's rate rose from 1.19% to 2.25%, with a third-quarter figure of 2.08%. JetBlue was worst, with 4.08% cancelled and 67.74% on time. Southwest's on-time rate fell from 78.10% to 73.08%.

American cut involuntary denied boardings by 84% in the third quarter, though it still cancelled the most flights among the big carriers, at 2.99%. United mishandled 1,269 wheelchairs against Delta's 373, on almost identical volumes.
