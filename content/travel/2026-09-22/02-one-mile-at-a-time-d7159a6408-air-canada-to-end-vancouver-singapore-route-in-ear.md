Air Canada has updated its schedule to show it will discontinue its Vancouver to Singapore service in early 2027, less than three years after the route launched. AeroRoutes flagged the quiet schedule change. The route is the airline's longest currently operated.

The cut comes despite a wider industry trend of airlines launching more ultra long haul flights and after earlier concerns about higher oil prices. For travellers, it removes a direct link between western Canada and Southeast Asia, likely forcing connections through other hubs.

Singapore had been a Star Alliance partner, so passengers may now route through other alliance carriers or face longer one-stop options. The airline has not publicly given a detailed reason for the cut beyond the schedule update.
