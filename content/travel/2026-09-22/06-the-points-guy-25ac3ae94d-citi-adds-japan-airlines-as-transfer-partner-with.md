Citi ThankYou Rewards has added Japan Airlines Mileage Bank as a transfer partner at a 1:1 ratio, with a 30% bonus running until 24 October 2026. Annual-fee cards such as the Strata Elite and Strata Premier transfer at 1:1, while no-fee Citi cards transfer at 1:0.7. Every 1,000 Citi points becomes 1,300 JAL miles during the bonus window, and transfers posted immediately in the author's test.

This matters if you collect Citi points and want to reach Japan in lie-flat business class, as 43,000 Citi points can cover a US-to-Japan business seat at the 55,000-mile JAL award level. JAL miles also book Oneworld partners including American Airlines and British Airways, and non-alliance partner Air France, sometimes at lower rates than those airlines' own programmes.

JAL has a 60-day rule for new accounts, so opening a Mileage Bank account now is wise before you find an award. Round-trip pricing on partner awards can be far cheaper than two one-ways, such as Houston to London Heathrow in British Airways business for 85,000 miles round-trip. With the 30% bonus, that is about 66,000 Citi points plus taxes and fees.

Caveats: British Airways award tickets carry heavy carrier surcharges, and JAL miles expire 36 months after transfer. Award availability is tight with so many new transfer paths into JAL.
