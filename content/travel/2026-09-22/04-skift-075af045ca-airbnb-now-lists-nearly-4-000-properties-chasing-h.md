Airbnb had 3,939 hotels using a new listing format across hundreds of markets as of September 4, according to AirDNA data cited by Skift. CEO Brian Chesky said hotels are still a single-digit percentage of nights booked in the second quarter, but he wants the category to become one of Airbnb's next multi-billion-dollar businesses.

The push matters to travellers because Airbnb is curating supply, taking a lower commission than Booking.com or Expedia, and pitching its audience of more than 1.6 billion devices a year to hotel operators. Hotels that join get access to a customer pool they may not reach elsewhere, which could affect pricing and availability on the platform.

Chesky said he changed his stance because guests kept requesting hotels. The feature is still new, so how exclusive the customer pool stays, and whether rates stay competitive, remains unclear.
