The FAA issued a ground stop on Monday afternoon after an equipment outage, halting flights at JFK, LaGuardia, Newark Liberty, and Philadelphia International. As of mid-afternoon, departures from all four airports were still facing disruptions, with delays and cancellations reported across the region.

The incident matters because the four airports covered serve as major connecting hubs for transatlantic and domestic routes, so knock-on delays can spread to networks across the US. Travellers booked on US carriers, including United which has a large Newark operation, should check flight status before travelling to the airport.

The FAA did not specify which equipment failed or when normal operations would resume. Source coverage was truncated behind a paywall, so the full scale of cancellations is not yet confirmed.
