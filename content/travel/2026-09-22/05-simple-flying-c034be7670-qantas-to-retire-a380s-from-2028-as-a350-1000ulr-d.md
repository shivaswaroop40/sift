Qantas will begin phasing out its ten Airbus A380s in 2028, bringing forward a previously announced early-2030s retirement. The airline cites confidence in Airbus A350-1000ULR deliveries starting in April 2027 and higher fuel costs in 2026 as drivers for the earlier exit.

The decision matters for travellers booked on Qantas long-haul routes, as Sydney-London via Singapore, Sydney-Dallas, Sydney-Los Angeles and Sydney-Johannesburg are currently flown by the A380. From October 2027 Qantas will run nonstop Sydney-London flights using the new A350-1000ULR, with ticket sales opening in February 2027.

Qantas expects the fleet renewal to lift its operating margin to 10 to 12% from fiscal 2032 and unlock around AUD 300 million ($215 million) in net cash flow between fiscal 2028 and fiscal 2031. The A350-1000ULR will carry 238 passengers in a heavily premium layout, with over 40% of seats in premium cabins versus 30% on the A380.
