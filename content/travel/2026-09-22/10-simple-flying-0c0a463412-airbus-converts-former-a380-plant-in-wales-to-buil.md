Airbus will invest $200 million (£150 million) to convert its former A380 wing factory in Broughton, North Wales, into an A321neo wing production site, creating 480 jobs later in 2026. The facility will house six wing jigs, an equipping line and a paint shop, with all jigs expected to reach full output by the end of 2026.

The move supports Airbus's target of delivering 75 A320-family jets per month globally, driven by an A321neo backlog of roughly 5,700 aircraft. A321neo orders make up around 70-75% of the narrowbacklog, fuelled partly by Boeing's lack of a 757 successor and strong demand for the A321LR and A321XLR.

Engine supply remains a constraint. CEO Guillaume Faury has said Pratt & Whitney delays have pushed the full Rate 75 target into 2027. Airbus is also expanding A220 wing output at its Belfast site in a parallel UK ramp-up.
