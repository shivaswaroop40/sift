JetBlue has raised its checked bag fees for the second time in 2026. A first bag now costs at least $50, up from $39, a 28% increase. Passengers who do not pre-pay will pay $60. Peak travel days cost more, at $55, or $70 without pre-payment. A second bag rises to $70 off-peak, from $59. The new fees apply to bookings made from Nov. 3. Trips booked earlier keep the old rates.

The move matters beyond JetBlue. After the airline raised fees in late March, United, Delta, American and Southwest all followed within weeks. A similar wave looks likely again. Bag fees rarely fall once raised, even if fuel costs drop. Travellers are already paying more, with Frontier reporting customers paid 71% more in fares over the prior quarter.

Some exemptions remain. Holders of JetBlue cobranded credit cards keep free bag benefits, and Mosaic elite members get at least one free bag. Mint and the new BlueFirst seats still include a first bag free. Every fare, including Blue Basic, allows a full-size carry-on, subject to TSA liquids rules. Booking before Nov. 3 locks in the old fees for holiday trips.
