Citi ThankYou members in the United States can earn a 40% bonus when transferring points to Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles between November 1 and November 30, 2026. The bonus is not offered by Citi. Turkish Airlines is funding it on its own side of the transfer.

For anyone holding ThankYou points, a 40% bonus improves the return on every point moved across. The window is fixed at one month, so anyone planning to build a Miles&Smiles balance faces a deadline of November 30, 2026. Because the airline rather than the bank pays the bonus, the terms sit with Turkish Airlines.
