Victoria, the capital of Gozo, has more than fifty surviving street shrines in its old lanes. Known in Maltese as niċeċ, they are set into house walls and above doorways. Some are elaborate stone niches holding carved saints under Baroque canopies. Others are simple painted figures behind glass, with plastic flowers and a lamp that someone still lights at night. Several are over three hundred years old.

They sit in public lanes, so there is nothing to buy and no guide to hire. You find them by looking up as you walk. The listing credits householders as the builders, though the excerpt cuts off before saying more. For a traveller who prefers places with a story, they add a free layer of history to Gozo's capital.
