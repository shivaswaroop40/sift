A TikTok video showing a $12,203 fare between Buffalo and New York went viral as evidence of expensive holiday travel. The price was a roundtrip connecting in Fort Lauderdale, taking seven to eight and a half hours each way. Another option at $11,400 connected in Orlando. Nonstops, roughly 90 minutes in the air, start around $337 roundtrip on Delta, JetBlue, United and American. Frontier sells a Florida-connecting option from $210.

The likely explanation is a fare-combining error. Fares between Buffalo and New York carry routing rules that do not normally allow Florida connections, so the search appears to have stitched together separate fares for parts of the trip. With millions of fare quotes being generated, even a tiny error rate produces outliers, and mistake fares can be too high as well as too low.

Holiday fares are genuinely higher. Hopper puts average Thanksgiving domestic roundtrips at $402, up 31%, and Christmas at $452, up 23%. Before accepting any odd price, search nonstops first, check JFK, LaGuardia and Newark, and book on airline websites directly. A fresh search comparing one-ways with the roundtrip can escape a broken combination. Amtrak's Empire Service is worth pricing on this route too.
