JetBlue has raised checked bag fees again, for bookings made from November 3. A first domestic bag starts at $50 each way, and reaches $70 if paid within 24 hours of departure on a peak date. A second bag reaches $85. Peak dates run November 19 to 30, December 17 to January 18, and May 20 to September 8 next summer. A family of four, one bag each, pays $440 roundtrip on peak dates when paying early, or $560 at check-in.

JetBlue has repeatedly moved first on bag fees, and rivals have followed. In 2018 United matched its $30 first bag within a week. This spring United announced an increase days after JetBlue's. Even if you never fly JetBlue, its price tests can raise what you pay elsewhere. Paying more than 24 hours ahead now saves $5 to $15 per bag each way.

Some travellers avoid the charges. Mosaic 1 elites get one checked bag, Mosaic 2 to 4 get two, and JetBlue Plus and Premier cardholders get a first bag free. Standard transatlantic fares still include a first bag, though Base fares charge $80 to $100. Prepaid bag fees are refundable only if the whole booking is cancelled before departure.

The $50 minimum is 28% higher than the March minimum of $39, and 43% higher than the $35 charged before that. One reason airlines favour fees over fares is tax. Domestic fares generally carry a 7.5% federal excise tax, but separate baggage fees are excluded, so every dollar shifted from fare to fee saves the airline 7.5 cents.
