The TSA PreCheck Gateside programme, which lets cardholders pass through airport security without a ticket to accompany departing passengers, has been scaled back. The scheme launched at 13 airports in September 2026, quietly expanded to around 300 airports weeks later, then retracted to just the original 13 this week. The TSA has not explained the reversal.

For independent travellers, the pullback matters because Gateside made it easier to see off friends and family at the gate rather than saying goodbye at check-in. The programme lets PreCheck members skip standard security lines when accompanying non-passengers. The rollback suggests the scheme may face operational or security hurdles the TSA is still working through.
