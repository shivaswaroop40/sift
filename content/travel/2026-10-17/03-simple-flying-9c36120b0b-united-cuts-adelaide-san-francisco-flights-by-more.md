United Airlines has reduced its second winter of nonstop flights between San Francisco and Adelaide from 49 planned return flights to 23, a 53% cut. The airline will now operate the route from December 17 through February 8, 2027, instead of the originally planned December 3 through March 25.

The cut reflects weak demand on the route's first season. United carried just 11,778 round-trip passengers with a 52.7% average load factor, dropping to 33.7% by February 2026. For an ultra-long-haul 787 service, such loads make the route financially difficult to sustain.

Adelaide's first nonstop US link was expected to generate $22 million in tourism activity and received South Australian Government support, but new long-haul routes face significant risk when demand does not match forecasts.
