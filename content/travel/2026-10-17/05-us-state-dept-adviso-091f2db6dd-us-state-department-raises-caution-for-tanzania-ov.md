The US State Department has issued a Level 2 advisory for Tanzania, recommending increased caution due to crime, terrorism, health risks, civil unrest, arbitrary law enforcement, and targeting of LGBTQ+ individuals. Violent crime including assault, robbery and mugging occurs throughout the country.

For independent travellers planning trips to Tanzania, the advisory affects your risk assessment and travel insurance considerations. Crime is more common in crowded urban areas and at times when you are not paying attention, which shapes how you move through cities and when.
