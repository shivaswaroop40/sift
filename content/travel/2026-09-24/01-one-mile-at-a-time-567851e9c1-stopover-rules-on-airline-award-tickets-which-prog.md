Most major airlines including American, Delta and United have stopped allowing free stopovers on award tickets, a once-common perk that let travellers visit two cities on one redemption. The guide compiles the loyalty schemes that still permit free or low-cost stopovers, so readers can plan a single award booking with an extra destination at no extra miles cost.

Stopovers matter because they stretch the value of a redemption by adding a second city for little or no extra miles, useful for open-jaw itineraries or adding a holiday stop on the way somewhere else. The piece compares how generous each remaining programme is, since some allow free stopovers, others charge fees and a few restrict the routes or regions where a stopover is permitted.

As the source is a guide still being updated, the specifics for each programme will need checking against current airline rules before booking.
