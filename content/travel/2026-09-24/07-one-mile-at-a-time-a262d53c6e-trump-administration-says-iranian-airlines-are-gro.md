The Trump administration claims Iranian airlines are grounded as of today under expanded US sanctions, part of a wider squeeze tied to the ongoing war. Iranian carriers have long been barred from flying to countries with direct US sanctions in place.

Enforcement looks uneven so far. Some Iranian airlines and certain foreign countries are not fully complying, meaning aircraft and routes could still be operating despite the official line. For travellers this affects where Iranian carriers can be booked, ticketed, or through-checked.

The US has imposed broad sanctions on Iran since early this year, and the grounding order extends the existing ban to the global network. Travellers holding or buying tickets on airlines such as Iran Air or Mahan Air should check status carefully before travel.
