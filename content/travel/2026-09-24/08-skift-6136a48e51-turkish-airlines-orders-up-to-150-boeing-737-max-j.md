Turkish Airlines has finalised a deal with Boeing for up to 150 737 MAX aircraft, its largest-ever narrowbody order. The agreement covers 100 firm 737-8s and options on 50 more, plus substitution rights for the larger 737-10. No delivery timeline was disclosed.

The order is the first major fleet decision under new chairman Murat Seker and CEO Ahmet Olmustur, who took over after Ahmet Bolat's departure. Seker, a former chief financial officer, has signalled a shift away from pure expansion towards profitability, network quality and efficiency at the Istanbul hub.

About a third of the order sits in options, giving Turkish flexibility to match aircraft size to demand and airport capacity at its main base. The substitution right on the 737-10 matters for slots-constrained airports and higher-density routes out of Istanbul.
