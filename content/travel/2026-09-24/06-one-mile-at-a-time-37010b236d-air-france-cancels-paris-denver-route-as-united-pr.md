Air France will discontinue its Paris Charles de Gaulle to Denver service roughly five years after launching the route in summer 2021. The airline initially operated the flights seasonally, then moved to year-round service in 2024, before reverting to seasonal operations and now cutting the route entirely.

The timing is not coincidental. United Airlines is launching its own Paris to Denver service, taking over the only nonstop connection between the two cities. Travellers currently booking Air France on this route will need to rebook or accept connections.

The change matters for anyone flying between France and Colorado, as the Denver end loses a SkyTeam option and gains a Star Alliance carrier. Loyalty programme members may need to reconsider which miles they use for this city pair.

Air France has not commented publicly on the decision beyond the schedule update.
