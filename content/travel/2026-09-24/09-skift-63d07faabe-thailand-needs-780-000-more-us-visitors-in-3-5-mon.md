Thailand has set a target of 1.5 million US visitors in 2026 but had recorded only 716,983 arrivals between 1 January and 15 September, leaving it needing more than 780,000 American travellers in the remaining three and a half months. US arrivals reached 1.08 million for the whole of last year.

The Tourism Authority of Thailand is pursuing what it calls a "Value over Volume" strategy to attract higher-spending long-haul visitors, coordinated through offices in New York, Chicago and Los Angeles. The direction was set at a meeting on 17 September chaired by Tourism and Sports Minister Surasak Phancharoenworakul.

Bangkok tour operator EXO Travel reports that forward bookings from the US are tracking slightly behind last year, and the article notes competition from Japan and Vietnam plus the cost of long-haul flights as obstacles to hitting the target.
