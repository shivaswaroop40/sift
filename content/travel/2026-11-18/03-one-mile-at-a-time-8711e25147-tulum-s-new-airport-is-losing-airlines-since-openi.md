Felipe Carrillo Puerto International Airport (TQO) opened in Tulum, Mexico, in late 2023. Tulum sits on the eastern coast of the Yucatan Peninsula and had been a growing tourist destination. Until then, visitors usually flew into Cancun (CUN). The hope was that a new international airport would expand visitor numbers.

The source says the opposite has happened and that airlines are leaving TQO. The text provided is cut off, so it gives no detail on which carriers have left, which routes have gone, or why.

For travellers, this matters if Tulum is on your list. Cancun remains the established gateway to the area. Routes into TQO may be thinner or less stable than the airport's opening suggested. Check that a given flight is still scheduled before booking, and keep Cancun as a fallback.
