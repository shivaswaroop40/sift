A Southwest Airlines Boeing 737 MAX flew 44 times with undetected storm damage to its tail. According to the NTSB, as reported by Simple Flying, a post-storm inspection was missed. The aircraft later passed a maintenance test that was flawed. It then suffered a Dutch roll accident.

The source text is only a short excerpt. It does not say when the storm hit, where the aircraft flew, or how severe the Dutch roll event was. It also does not say whether anyone was hurt or what the NTSB recommends. Those details should be checked in the full report.
