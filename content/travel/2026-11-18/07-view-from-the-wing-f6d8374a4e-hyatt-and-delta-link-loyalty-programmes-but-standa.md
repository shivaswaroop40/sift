Hyatt and Delta have set out how their partnership works. Delta Medallion members earn 1 SkyMile per eligible dollar spent with Hyatt. Hyatt Globalists earn 1 Hyatt point per dollar on Delta premium cabins, Discoverists earn 1 per $2. Delta Comfort, Premium Select, First and Delta One qualify. Standard full-fare main cabin coach earns nothing.

Milestone rewards start on 1 January 2027. Hyatt members can choose Delta eCertificates of $25, $75 and $100, lounge passes at 70 to 90 nights, Gold Medallion at 100 nights and Platinum at 150 nights. Platinum comes without Choice Benefits. From 1 February 2027, Delta Platinum and Diamond members can pick a Hyatt free night as a Choice Benefit, in place of another benefit.

For travellers who used the old American Airlines tie-up, this is a partial replacement. Regular coach no longer counts, Discoverists earn half the rate, and Homes & Hideaways and FIND spending are excluded. The bonus Hyatt points also do not count towards Hyatt status. The article says no direct SkyMiles-for-Hyatt-night redemption was announced.

Launch offers need no elite status. Register and complete a Hyatt stay between 17 November and 31 December 2026 for one bonus qualifying night. Between 4 January and 15 February 2027, a first eligible Delta Comfort-or-higher flight earns 1,000 Hyatt points, and a first qualifying Hyatt stay earns 1,000 SkyMiles. Reciprocal status challenges are promised but not yet detailed.
