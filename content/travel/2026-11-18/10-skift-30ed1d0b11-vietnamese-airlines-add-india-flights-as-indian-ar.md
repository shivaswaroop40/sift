Sun PhuQuoc Airways will start Ahmedabad to Phu Quoc flights on 3 December. Vietnam Airlines will move its Delhi and Bengaluru services to daily from 14 December. It opened its own Ahmedabad route a few weeks earlier. VietJet has the widest network, linking Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang with six Indian cities.

Demand is behind the expansion. Vietnam had 746,000 Indian visitors in 2025, making India its sixth-largest international source market. Arrivals were 138,000 in 2022 and 507,000 in 2024. In the first nine months of 2026 the figure was 670,972, up 33% year on year, against overall growth of around 14.5%.

For independent travellers, more carriers on these routes means more choice of schedule and fare between India and Vietnam, including a new link to Phu Quoc from Ahmedabad. Daily frequencies on Delhi and Bengaluru make short trips easier to plan. Skift's own take is that seats alone will not hold the market, and Vietnam still needs food, guides and events products for Indian visitors.
