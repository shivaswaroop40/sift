A total solar eclipse will cross central Tunisia on the morning of 2 August 2027, bringing several minutes of darkness to El Jem's Roman amphitheatre, the Great Mosque of Kairouan and the desert around Tozeur. The Points Guy has published a seven-day road trip from Tunis to Tozeur and back, written by an editor who made the journey.

Tozeur should also draw Star Wars fans, as 2027 is the 50th anniversary of the first film and several Tatooine locations are nearby. Tunis is outside the path of totality. Sidi Bou Said, a cliffside village, was recently added to UNESCO's World Heritage list, alongside the Medina of Tunis and Carthage.

There are no nonstop flights from the United States to Tunis-Carthage (TUN). The author flew Air France via Paris. Other one-stop options are Lufthansa via Frankfurt, ITA via Rome, Royal Air Maroc via Casablanca and Turkish Airlines via Istanbul. SkyTeam, Star Alliance and other loyalty options are all covered by at least one route.

Much of the advice is tied to the Chase Sapphire Reserve card, which will matter only to US cardholders. The author moved 142,000 Ultimate Rewards points to Flying Blue for a business-class return, and claims 5.83 cents per point. The available text covers only the first two days, so the rest of the route is not visible.
