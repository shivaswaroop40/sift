AirAsia co-founder Tony Fernandes has pushed back the low-cost group's return to growth. In a LinkedIn post on Wednesday, he said 2027 would be "a period of consolidation" and that strong growth would resume only by 2028.

He said engine problems are still keeping part of the fleet grounded. "We are still struggling to get all our fleet back due to engine issues, but hopefully January," he wrote. He also warned of "more painful decisions" on costs.

The new timeline is a step back from earlier guidance. In July, Fernandes said oil prices were falling and AirAsia was ready for a "massive comeback story", according to the New Straits Times. Skift says the group had also promised a fourth-quarter capacity rebound two months ago.

For travellers who rely on AirAsia's network, a flat 2027 suggests limited route and frequency expansion. Cost cuts could also touch routes or service. The source text is truncated, so it gives no detail on which markets are affected.
