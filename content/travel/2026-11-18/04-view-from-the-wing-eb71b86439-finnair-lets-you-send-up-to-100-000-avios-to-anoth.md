British Airways charges most members £50 per transaction to give Avios to someone else, with each transfer capped at 60,000 points. A workaround uses Finnair. The giver moves Avios free at 1:1 into their own Finnair account, then sends them to the recipient through Finnair Shop for €10. The recipient can use them there or move them back to British Airways at 1:1.

Sending 100,000 Avios via British Airways takes two transactions at £50 each. Gold members are exempt from that fee. British Airways also limits senders and recipients to 200,000 Avios per calendar year. Finnair sets no cap on member transfers beyond your balance, so the saving is largest for big transfers.

The route needs preparation. Finnair accounts must be 30 days old to link, and the sender must have been a member for 90 days to transfer online. Newer members can go through customer care for €20. Both people need matching names and emails across accounts, two-factor authentication, and must be 18 or over. Basic members share a 1 million Avios limit over 12 months across linked programmes.

Do not leave points sitting in a dormant Finnair account. Transfers do not reset the 18-month expiry clock. One Reddit user reported losing transferred Avios within two weeks, then getting them back after contacting the airline. You may also not need this at all, as British Airways household accounts pool balances and you can often book an award for someone else.
