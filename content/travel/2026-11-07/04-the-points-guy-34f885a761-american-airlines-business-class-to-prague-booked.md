A reviewer flew American Airlines' resumed nonstop from Philadelphia to Prague in business class for 55,000 Atmos Rewards points, plus $5.60 in taxes and fees. American relaunched the route in May 2026 after dropping it during the pandemic. It had previously flown in 2018 and 2019. The route runs seasonally from May to October and is confirmed to return for summer 2027.

That is a low redemption for transatlantic business class. The writer notes such deals appear with American Airlines AAdvantage miles as well as Atmos Rewards points. The route also forms part of American's Central European network from Philadelphia, and Vienna and Porto join next summer, widening the nonstop options for points travellers from the hub.

The catch is the aircraft. The flight uses an 11-year-old 787-8 with a legacy cabin of 20 business seats in a 1-2-1 layout. The 21-inch lie-flat Safran Concept D seats are a decade old, some face rearwards, and because they are attached to each other you can feel your neighbour move. Panasonic Wi-Fi cost $35 for the flight and was just OK. New Flagship suites and a cabin refresh are planned with no start date, and Starlink Wi-Fi arrives from 2027.

Long-haul business-class passengers can use the Flagship Lounge at Philadelphia, which opened in May 2025 after years of delays and sits near Gate A15 where the long-haul flights depart. A sixth Flagship Lounge is planned for Charlotte, with an Admirals Club of more than 40,000 square feet.
