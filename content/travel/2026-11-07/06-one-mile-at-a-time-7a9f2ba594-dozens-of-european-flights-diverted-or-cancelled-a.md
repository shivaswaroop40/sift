Dozens of flights across Europe have been diverted, cancelled or delayed this week after bomb threats were made against them. The threats appear to be part of a coordinated effort, though the motive is unclear beyond a desire to create chaos. The article describes the threats as fake, and notes that bomb threats called in against flights are nothing new.

For anyone flying in Europe, the practical effect is disruption. A diversion can add hours and an unplanned stopover, and cancellations can strand passengers at short notice. The original headline quotes a warning that "this is only the beginning", so travellers with upcoming flights should watch airline notifications closely and build slack into connections.

Confirmed detail is thin. The article does not say who is behind the threats, how they are being made, or which airlines and routes have been hit hardest. It only notes a coordinated pattern and a history of similar hoaxes, so the specifics should be treated as still developing.
