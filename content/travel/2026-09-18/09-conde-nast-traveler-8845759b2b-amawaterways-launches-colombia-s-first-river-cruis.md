AmaWaterways has begun sailing the Magdalena River between Cartagena and Barranquilla on two new ships, AmaMelodia and AmaMagdalena. It is the first passenger cruise route on the river since steamships ended some 60 years ago.

For travellers the itinerary opens up towns hard to reach on their own, including Usiacurí's iraca-palm weavers, Mompox's filigree silver workshops and the fishing village of Santa Barbara de Pinto, where AmaWaterways built new docking bridges. Excursions are led by Colombian guides who translate and liaise with artisans on the ground.

The company had to drop El Banco from the route because the river's bends made docking impossible, so a cumbia troupe now buses in to perform aboard instead. Seven-night Magic of Colombia sailings start at $3,299 per person.

The article is a first-person review of the sailing, with prices and details of what to expect at each stop. It is a useful primer for anyone weighing this itinerary against more established river cruises in Europe.
