Marriott hotel employees say they are required to sign up 20 guests a month for the Bonvoy loyalty programme or face reduced hours and eventual dismissal. Staff report enrolling guests who did not ask to join, treating acceptance of a welcome gift as consent, and using overnight batch sign-ups with email addresses on file.

The pressure is driven by cash incentives for hotels. In 2025, Marriott paid up to $10,000 a month to top-performing full-service properties in the US and Canada, with smaller bonus tiers for select-service hotels reaching 20% enrollment rates. One SpringHill Suites reportedly earned $20,000 in incentive payments that year.

For travellers, this means existing or duplicate Bonvoy accounts may be created in their name without their knowledge. Members who already have accounts may find their data handled across multiple records, and anyone who values programme integrity may want to check for accounts they did not personally create.
