A screenshot circulating on Reddit, Facebook and Twitter alleges that roughly 120 pilots at an all-CRJ regional airline had their certificates frozen on 14 September after the FAA examined Wi-Fi logs from August repositioning flights. The post claims investigators matched connected devices against approved electronic flight bags and concluded pilots were browsing personally, and that the FAA would seek warrants for their phones.

The original source and airline are unnamed. The poster on Reddit received it through a forwarded group text. The author of the source article notes that PSA Airlines, a likely match, has around 1,845 pilots, so losing 120 would be a 6.5% hit likely to cause visible disruption.

Several commenters report union emails denying the story. A letter of investigation does not by itself suspend a certificate, only an emergency FAA order or airline action can do that. The underlying rule is real: Part 121 flight crew are banned from personal device use at their duty station, and the 2014 rule cites a 2014 incident where pilots overflew their destination by 150 miles while using laptops.
