The Association of Value Airlines, representing Allegiant, Frontier, Avelo and Breeze, has contacted congressional offices in recent weeks seeking temporary relief from jet fuel taxes as fuel costs rise. The trade group's executive director Jonathon Freye told Skift there has been no indication Congress will consider such measures.

Unlike network carriers, ultra-low-cost airlines have limited room to raise fares without losing price-sensitive customers, leaving them more exposed to fuel price increases. A bill to temporarily suspend the relevant tax has been proposed but has not advanced.

The House Transportation and Infrastructure Committee did not respond to a request for comment. The source text was truncated before detailing the specific tax mechanism or the savings figure for carriers.
