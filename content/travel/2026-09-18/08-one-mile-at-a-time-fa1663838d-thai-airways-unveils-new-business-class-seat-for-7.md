Thai Airways has formally unveiled a new business class seat that will be fitted to incoming Boeing 777s, 787s and Airbus A350s, and retrofitted to existing wide body aircraft in the fleet. The airline has been hinting at the project for some time, but this announcement includes the full specifications and rollout scope.

For travellers this means a consistent long haul product across the mainline Thai wide body fleet, rather than the patchwork of older seats that currently appears on different aircraft types. It also removes some of the guesswork when picking flights, since seat type will be a better predictor of the cabin you actually get.

The Thai press release does not yet state when the first retrofitted aircraft will enter service or how quickly the existing fleet will be converted, so the practical effect on bookings is still unclear. Award availability and route deployment have not been confirmed.
