British Airways will start a triangular London Gatwick service to Kilimanjaro and Zanzibar on May 29, 2027, running three times a week until October 31 on a Boeing 777-200ER. Flight BA2163 departs Gatwick at 9:05am, reaches Kilimanjaro at 8:20pm, continues to Zanzibar, and returns via Kilimanjaro overnight to Gatwick by 7:55am.

The route gives BA a one-stop option to two East African leisure markets on a single ticket from a London base that currently serves Accra, Algiers, Marrakesh, Mauritius and Rabat. Travellers can combine a safari park with a beach stay without booking separate flights.

Tanzania is drawing interest from other European carriers. Air France and KLM already run similar triangular services, Turkish Airlines stops at Kilimanjaro on Zanzibar flights, and Neos links Milan and Rome. Air Tanzania is also reportedly planning its own Gatwick service from July 2027 on a 787.

Kilimanjaro handled 1.04 million passengers in 2024, Zanzibar 2.7 million in 2025 and Dar es Salaam 2.9 million in 2024, underlining why the country has become a focal point for European long-haul planning.
