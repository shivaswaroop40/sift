Emirates will deploy an Airbus A380 on one of its two daily Dubai-Düsseldorf flights from December 1 to January 31, 2027. The aircraft will replace a Boeing 777-300ER on flight EK055, which departs Dubai at 8:20 AM and arrives in Düsseldorf at 12:30 PM local time, with a block time of seven hours and ten minutes.

The move restores A380 service to Düsseldorf after it was withdrawn in September. The carrier will use its 517-seat first class-equipped A380 variant instead of the 615-seat two-class version that previously served the route. This increases daily capacity by 23 percent, or over 10,000 seats in each direction across the two-month period.

Düsseldorf has been an A380 destination since 2015. The superjumbo is absent from regular service and returns temporarily for the winter season. Emirates will operate one daily A380 rotation and one 777-300ER rotation on the route during this period.
