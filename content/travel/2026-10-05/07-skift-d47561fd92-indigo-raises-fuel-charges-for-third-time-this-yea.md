IndiGo announced its third fuel surcharge increase in 2026 on Monday, effective from Tuesday for all new bookings. Domestic charges now range from INR 375 to INR 1,300 per ticket, while international charges start at INR 1,000 for South Asian routes. The airline cited a month-on-month surge in aviation turbine fuel prices exceeding 14%.

Frequent flyers on India's largest carrier will see their ticket costs rise again. This is the third hike in ten months, signalling that India's low-fare airline model faces sustained pressure from fuel costs that have reached decade-high levels.

While IndiGo's latest increase is smaller than the April hike, the repeated surcharges suggest fuel volatility will persist as a hidden cost for travellers booking through Indian carriers.
