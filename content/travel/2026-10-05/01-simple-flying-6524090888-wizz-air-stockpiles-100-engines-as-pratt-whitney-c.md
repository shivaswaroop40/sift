Wizz Air is holding 100 spare Pratt & Whitney engines worth around $1 billion, a response to the manufacturer's ongoing supply and reliability issues. The engines power the airline's A320 family aircraft, and shortages have disrupted schedules across the industry.

For an independent traveller, this matters because engine shortages have already caused flight cancellations and delays at budget carriers. Wizz Air's decision to hoard spares signals the airline expects supply problems to persist, which could affect route availability and schedule reliability.

The stockpile represents a shift in ultra-low-cost carrier strategy. Traditionally, these airlines minimise spare parts to cut costs. Wizz Air's $1 billion tied up in engines means less money for fleet growth, network expansion, or fare competition.
