Delta will begin twice-daily nonstop flights from Salt Lake City to Flagstaff Pulliam Airport from June 2027, using 50-seat regional jets. Flagstaff sits less than two hours' drive from Sedona and the Grand Canyon's South Rim.

This expands Delta's regional network from Salt Lake City, which has received multiple new routes over two years. The route receives over $1.3 million in incentives, including an $800,000 federal grant that guarantees minimum revenue.

American Airlines already operates the only other service at Flagstaff, from Dallas Fort Worth and Phoenix. Delta becomes the second carrier at the airport.
