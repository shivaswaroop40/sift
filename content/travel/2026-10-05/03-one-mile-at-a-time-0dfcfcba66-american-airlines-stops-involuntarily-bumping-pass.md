American Airlines has committed to ending involuntary denied boardings, meaning the airline will no longer force passengers off overbooked flights. The policy change was revealed internally and marks a departure from the industry practice of overbooking to account for no-shows and missed connections.

This matters because involuntary bumping directly affects independent travellers' plans. A forced rebooking can destroy tight itineraries, miss onward connections, or strand you in an unplanned location, making this change meaningful for frequent flyers who cannot absorb schedule disruption.
