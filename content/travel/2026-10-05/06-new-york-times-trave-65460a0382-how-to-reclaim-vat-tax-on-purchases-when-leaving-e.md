Value-added tax is built into prices across Europe on goods from clothing to electronics. Tourists can claim refunds on items taken home by following specific procedures at departure.

VAT refunds typically range from 15 to 25 percent of purchase price, depending on the country. For independent travellers, reclaiming this money reduces the effective cost of shopping and affects the value of purchases you budget for abroad.

The process requires documentation from shops, declaration at customs, and submission to refund services. Timing and retailer cooperation matter: not all shops participate, and deadlines for claims vary by country.
