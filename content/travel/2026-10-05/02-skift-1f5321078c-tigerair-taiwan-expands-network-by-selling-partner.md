Tigerair Taiwan has launched Tigerhop, a platform developed with Icelandic travel tech company Dohop. The system lets customers book flights on Singapore-based Scoot directly through Tigerair's booking channels, linking Taipei and Da Nang with Singapore and onward connections to Japanese cities including Akita, Hanamaki and Tottori.

For independent travellers, this matters because it expands what routes you can book through a single carrier without that carrier operating the flight. It sidesteps traditional interline agreements, potentially offering simpler bookings and consolidated loyalty points across a wider network.
