British Airways will begin or resume flights to ten destinations between April 2026 and May 2027. New markets include St. Louis, Tivat, Rovaniemi, Kilimanjaro and Zanzibar. The airline returns to Melbourne for the first time since 2006, and to Tanzania after a 14-year gap. Routes exclude internal transfers between London airports.

For independent travellers, this expands BA's network into leisure and safari destinations alongside business hubs. The St. Louis route has already grown to daily service in peak months despite launching in April 2026, suggesting strong demand. Kilimanjaro and Zanzibar tap underserved safari and beach markets with expected passenger growth of 40 percent or more.

BA last served Dar es Salaam in Tanzania with a 767, but will now focus on leisure destinations. Zanzibar and Kilimanjaro flights operate together on the same service, with three weekly departures from Gatwick from May 2027 using a 777-200ER.
