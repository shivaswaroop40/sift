American Airlines has ordered staff to stop involuntarily bumping passengers, effective September 30. Employees must keep aircraft doors open until enough volunteers accept, delaying departure if necessary. Gate managers can now approve compensation above preset limits without distant approval.

For independent travellers, this matters because American has bumped 60% of all involuntary denied boardings across US airlines in 2025. The airline was losing ground to competitors like Delta, which bumped nobody. This change affects how much you can negotiate if you volunteer.

When volunteering, you can now be confirmed on the very next flight even if it shows no available seats, matching the benefit American reserves for elite members. This makes volunteering more attractive when it means a short delay rather than standby waiting. Compare the voluntary package with your legal compensation entitlement before accepting.
