Air France-KLM has raised the price of earning XPs through sustainable aviation fuel contributions in its Flying Blue programme. The increase is reported at 50%. The change was rolled out quietly, and it reduces the ability to earn elite status this way.

For Flying Blue members, SAF contributions were a way to close an XP gap before a status review or push a tier over the line. A 50% increase means paying half as much again for the same XPs. Anyone planning a status push or renewal should re-cost the option before relying on it.

Flying Blue lets members earn XPs by paying into sustainable aviation fuel, and the group has repriced that path upward. The excerpt does not show the exact before-and-after figures, so check the full article for the per-XP costs before budgeting any contribution.
