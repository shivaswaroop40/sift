Air India has cut four European routes this winter and extended its Delhi to Chicago suspension. It ended four weekly Delhi to Vienna flights on October 31, leaving Vienna without a nonstop link to India. Delhi to Copenhagen and Amritsar to London Gatwick are suspended, and Delhi to Birmingham ends on November 4. Cirium data shows European seat capacity down about 12% between November 2026 and March 2027. Delhi to Newark, also suspended, returns in January 2027.

The airline cited airspace restrictions over certain regions and jet fuel above $206 a barrel. Air India is India's largest international carrier, so the cuts reduce nonstop options between India and Europe. Vienna, which handled about 173,500 passengers to and from Indian cities in 2025, loses its only nonstop India link. Delhi to Paris is the sharpest cut, halved from 14 to seven weekly flights.

Domestic changes are mostly transfers rather than cancellations. Delhi to Goa, Delhi to Aurangabad, Mumbai to Jamnagar and Hyderabad to Tirupati now fly under Air India Express, the group's low-cost arm. Chennai to Madurai ended in August without a transfer. The moves follow Tata Group restructuring, which merged Vistara into Air India and AirAsia India into Air India Express.
