United Airlines is accelerating its Starlink rollout to over 1,000 mainline aircraft by May 2027, installing more than 20 jets per week across 17 maintenance stations worldwide. This is far ahead of its previous targets and involves stations in Houston, Hong Kong, Singapore and elsewhere.

For independent travellers who rely on in-flight productivity, this matters because functional internet changes the calculation on which airline to book. Starlink delivers median speeds of 152 megabits per second and 44 milliseconds latency, compared to 50 megabits and hundreds of milliseconds for competing systems.

Delta, United's main business-class competitor, does not plan to begin installing Amazon's satellite internet until 2028, and only on roughly half its fleet. United is already recruiting Delta frequent flyers with offers of status matches, exploiting the gap in service availability.
