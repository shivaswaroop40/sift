Alaska Airlines has begun selling Breeze Airways flights through its own booking channels and awarding Atmos Rewards frequent flyer points on Breeze tickets. The partnership was not formally announced by either airline.

For independent travellers, this matters because it could signal a deeper commercial relationship between the carriers. It may affect which routes become available through Alaska's loyalty programme and how points accumulate across your flights.

The partnership remains limited in scope so far. Neither airline has explained the arrangement or its long-term intentions.
