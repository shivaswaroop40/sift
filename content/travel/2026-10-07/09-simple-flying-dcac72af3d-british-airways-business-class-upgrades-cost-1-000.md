Upgrading to British Airways Club World from economy costs $1,000 to $3,000 when paying cash, though the price varies by route demand, original fare class, and timing. Upgrades from premium economy are cheaper at $800 to $2,000. Using Avios points costs 24,000 to 30,000 per one-way upgrade from premium economy.

For independent travellers, the premium economy to business upgrade offers better value than economy to business. Frequent flyers use a cost-per-hour rule, targeting less than $75 to $100 per flight hour. Avios upgrades often provide the best value, offering a fully flat bed and private suite against reclining premium economy seats.

Last-minute check-in offers can sometimes undercut advance upgrade prices. Business class includes flat-bed seats with privacy doors, restaurant-style dining, lounge access, and priority services. Premium economy lacks lounge access and ground priorities, though it does include priority boarding.
