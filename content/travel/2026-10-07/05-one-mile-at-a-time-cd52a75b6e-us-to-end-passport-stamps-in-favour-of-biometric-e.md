US Customs and Border Protection will stop stamping passports on arrival, shifting to biometric data collection instead. The change reflects that entry tracking already relies primarily on fingerprints and facial recognition rather than ink stamps.

For independent travellers, this means no more physical proof of entry stamped into your passport. If you collect stamps as travel souvenirs or rely on them as informal records of visits, you will lose that option.
