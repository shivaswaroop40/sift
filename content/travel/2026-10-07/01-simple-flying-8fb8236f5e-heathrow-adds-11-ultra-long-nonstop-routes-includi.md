Qantas will resume nonstop flights from Perth to Heathrow from February 1, taking 17 hours 50 minutes. The route currently stops in Singapore due to airspace restrictions around Iran. Eleven routes from Heathrow now exceed 15 hours block time, shaped by geopolitical detours and aircraft limits.

These routes matter because they define Heathrow's network edge. Slot constraints force airlines to use larger aircraft, and ultra-long flights justify the widebody deployment. Geopolitical reality—avoiding Chinese, Iranian and Taiwanese airspace—adds length and reshapes where carriers can operate.

British Airways will restore nonstop Buenos Aires service in late March 2027, ending a four-year gap. Santiago reverts to one-stop via Buenos Aires on the same date, its first such service since 2000. Both changes hinge on aircraft range and fuel strategy rather than new infrastructure.
