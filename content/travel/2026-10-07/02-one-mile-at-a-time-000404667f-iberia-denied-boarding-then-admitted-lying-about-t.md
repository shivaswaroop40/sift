Spanish airline Iberia denied a passenger boarding on a Milan to Madrid flight, initially claiming the ticket was invalid. The airline later admitted the flight was oversold and it had misrepresented the cause. An Australian YouTuber documented the incident in a video that prompted the airline's eventual acknowledgment.

Independent travellers booking through third-party sites like Expedia face a particular risk when airlines blame the booking platform for denied boarding. Knowing whether an airline is oversold or claiming a ticketing issue affects how you pursue compensation and which party to hold accountable.

Iberia's initial false explanation matters because EU261 regulations require airlines to pay compensation for oversold flights, while invalid tickets carry no such obligation. The lie appears designed to avoid liability.
