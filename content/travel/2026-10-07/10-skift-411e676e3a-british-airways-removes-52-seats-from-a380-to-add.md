British Airways is refurbishing its Airbus A380s to carry 417 seats instead of 469. Economy class shrinks by 88 seats to 215, a 30% reduction. Business class expands to 106 suites across the entire upper deck. Premium economy grows by 29 seats to 84, a 50% increase.

At Heathrow, where landing slots are scarce and expensive, removing seats makes financial sense. The airline prioritises higher-paying passengers over volume. This reshapes how it uses its most constrained resource: takeoff and landing rights.

First class seats drop slightly from 14 to 12, confirming the strategic pivot away from ultra-premium toward broad premium capacity.
