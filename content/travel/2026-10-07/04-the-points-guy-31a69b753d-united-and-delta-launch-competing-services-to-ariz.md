Flagstaff Pulliam Airport will receive nonstop flights from both carriers starting summer 2027. United will fly twice daily from Denver from August 10, and Delta will operate twice daily from Salt Lake City from June 7. Both airlines will use Bombardier CRJ-550 jets with 50 seats each.

The airport lies two hours north of Phoenix and serves as a gateway to Grand Canyon National Park and Sedona. American Airlines has dominated FLG for decades but this new competition will give independent travellers genuine routing options to access northern Arizona attractions.

United previously served this route from 2019 to 2022. The airport received an $800,000 federal grant to encourage new service from Denver or Salt Lake City, which appears to have catalysed both carriers' decisions.
