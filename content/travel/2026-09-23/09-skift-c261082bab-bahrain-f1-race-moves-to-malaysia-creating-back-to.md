Formula 1 has relocated the Bahrain Grand Prix to Sepang, with the race now scheduled for October 2 to 4 after the original April event in Sakhir was cancelled due to Middle East conflict. The Singapore Grand Prix follows at Marina Bay from October 9 to 11, creating a rare Southeast Asia doubleheader.

Forward-looking Amadeus Travel Intelligence data shows booked air arrivals from Kuala Lumpur to Singapore for October 5 to 11 are tracking 74% higher year-on-year. Hotels in both markets are positioned to capture two consecutive race weekends within a single regional trip.

For independent travellers, the doubleheader means overlapping peak pricing and limited hotel availability across Kuala Lumpur, Sepang and Singapore in early October. Travellers flexible on dates may find better value either side of the race weeks.

The Malaysia race is confirmed as a one-off for this year, so the unusual back-to-back schedule is unlikely to repeat. Travellers booking future October trips should treat 2026 as an outlier rather than a new pattern.
