Qantas has confirmed its direct New York to Sydney service will start in 2028, adding to its Project Sunrise ultra-long-haul routes. The 18-hour flights will follow the launch of direct Sydney to London services, which Qantas expects to begin earlier in 2028.

The Sydney to London route is scheduled to take around 22 hours, making it one of the longest scheduled passenger flights in the world. For travellers, this removes a stop on two of the most demanding long-haul corridors and changes how routes between Australia and the northern hemisphere can be booked.

The London service is due to start first, with the New York route following months later. Aircraft type, frequency and fares were not detailed in the announcement, so scheduling details may shift before launch.
