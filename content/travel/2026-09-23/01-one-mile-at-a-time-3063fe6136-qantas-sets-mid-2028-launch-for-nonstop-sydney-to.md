Qantas plans to start nonstop Sydney to New York JFK flights in mid-2028 using specially configured Airbus A350-1000ULR aircraft, the longest commercial flights in the world at 9,950 miles and roughly 20 hours airborne.

The Sydney to London Heathrow nonstop is due to begin a few months earlier, in October 2027, completing the Project Sunrise pairing announced since 2017. Both routes are intended as single-aisle endurance runs rather than connections.

For travellers, the routes offer a one-stop alternative to current Sydney-US connections via Los Angeles or San Francisco, removing a transit point but adding several hours of continuous flight time.

Aircraft configuration, fares and award availability have not been disclosed, so it is not yet clear how the seats will be priced or how easy they will be to redeem using frequent flyer points.
