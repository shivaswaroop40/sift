Just north of Bologna Centrale station, the 20th-century Bolognina neighbourhood sits in deliberate contrast to the medieval centro storico, with tree-lined avenues and pastel low-rise blocks built for railway and factory workers.

It now hosts a multi-ethnic community and a wave of independent restaurants, bars and cultural spaces, from a vegan cantina sourcing from the Bolognese hills to La Memoria di Ustica museum, housed in a former tram shed.

For travellers, it offers a cheaper, less crowded base within walking distance of the centre, plus a concentration of Georgian, Ethiopian, Sichuan and halal food that is hard to find in the old town.
