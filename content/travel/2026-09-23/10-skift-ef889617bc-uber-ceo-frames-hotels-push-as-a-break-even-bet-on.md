Uber CEO Dara Khosrowshahi said at the Skift Global Forum that adding hotels and short-term rentals to the app is part of a broader bet on becoming a fuller travel platform, not a profit centre on its own. Hotel bookings launched in April through a partnership with Expedia, and flights are a possible next step.

For travellers this means a new place to shop for stays, one tied to an Uber account they may already use for rides. Pricing and inventory will reflect Expedia's supply rather than a separate Uber-only network, so selection should match what Expedia offers elsewhere.

The strategic logic rests on Uber's reach. It operates in more than 70 countries and delivers in over 100, which the company argues puts it in front of travellers before they choose where to book. Hotels are positioned as a hook that keeps users inside the app rather than a stand-alone revenue line.
