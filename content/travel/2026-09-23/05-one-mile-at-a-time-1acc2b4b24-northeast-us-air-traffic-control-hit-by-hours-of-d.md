On 21 September 2026, air traffic control systems in the northeast United States suffered hours of outages after a backup fibre optic line was accidentally cut. Thousands of flights were delayed or cancelled across the country as a result of the disruption.

The FAA has not yet published a full timeline, but early reporting points to a single physical cut of a redundant line, raising questions about the resilience of ATC infrastructure. For travellers, it shows how a localised utility failure can cascade into widespread schedule disruption.

Officials are still investigating who cut the line and why the backup failed, with finger-pointing underway between agencies and contractors. Until a post-incident report is released, the exact chain of failures remains unclear.
