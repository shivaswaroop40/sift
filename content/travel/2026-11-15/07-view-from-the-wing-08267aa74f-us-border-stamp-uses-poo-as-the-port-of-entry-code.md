A View from the Wing roundup notes that US Customs and Border Protection uses POO as the port-of-entry code for Portland, Oregon, rather than the airport code PDX. Obvious alternatives such as POR, PTL and POL were apparently rejected. The result is that thousands of passports carry a stamp reading POO.

The roundup also covers a proposed $170,000 FAA fine against Sonoma County Airport, and Hyatt's pipeline of more than 150 upper-midscale hotels. Mississippi is reported to be looking to borrow money to fund flight subsidies.

The FAA alleges the airport failed to provide required rescue and firefighting staff and vehicles for two passenger flights in March 2025, and did not tell the airline. For one flight, it also alleges missed wildlife runway inspections.

Hyatt says over half of signings for Hyatt Studios, Hyatt Select and Unscripted by Hyatt are in markets where it had no presence. It says research in 2023 and 2024 found members staying elsewhere due to limited Hyatt availability.
