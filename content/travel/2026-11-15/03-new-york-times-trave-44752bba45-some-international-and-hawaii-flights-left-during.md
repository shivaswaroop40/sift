The Federal Aviation Administration issued only the third nationwide ground stop of this century on Friday. Cirium counted more than 8,200 cancellations, over 31 percent of scheduled flights. Even so, Flightradar24 recorded more than 60 flights leaving the US for overseas during the stoppage. Some West Coast departures to Hawaii were also allowed.

The FAA says 21 air traffic control facilities oversee the country's airspace, 20 in the contiguous US and one in Alaska. As some of those centres recovered, flights were allowed to depart through coordination with the Air Traffic Control System Command Center. The FAA said this in an advisory less than two hours after the order.

A ground stop is not always absolute. Whether a flight leaves can depend on which control centre it relies on and whether that centre is working. Passengers on long-haul or Hawaii routes may find their flight is not automatically cancelled, so check status directly with the airline.
