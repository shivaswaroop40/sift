Footage from a TV episode of Airport Security: USA, now circulating again, shows a Japanese woman handcuffed at Honolulu airport after refusing to go to secondary inspection. She screams for help and insists she is on holiday. Officers then rush to put her on a flight to Nagoya leaving in 30 minutes, because missing it would mean a night in federal detention.

The officers say her answers did not overcome the presumption that she intended to immigrate. She said a man she had not contacted paid for her flights and hotel. The narrator links him to organised crime, though the wording shifts. Officers also suspected she meant to work in a club, which a tourist entry does not allow.

The clip appears to be about two years old, and the source does not say when the incident took place. It is edited television, so it shows only what the producers chose. The article also recalls two other Honolulu cases, including a woman held after an 88-day return ticket and an Australian detained over an onward-ticket problem.
