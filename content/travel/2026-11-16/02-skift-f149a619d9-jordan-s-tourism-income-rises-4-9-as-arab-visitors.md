Jordan's tourism income rose 4.9% to $6.35 billion in the first nine months of 2026, according to the Central Bank of Jordan. Spending by Arab visitors jumped 18.8% and income from Asian visitors rose 12.3%. Income from European visitors fell 23.7%, and from U.S. visitors 16.3%.

The cause is the U.S.-Iran war, which began in late February. Jordan's army said in April that 281 missiles and drones from Iran had targeted the country, and Iran has at times hit bases hosting U.S. forces there. Skift says Western travel warnings are holding back the Western-dependent circuit, with Petra hit hardest.

Recovery is uneven. Average hotel occupancy was 38% in September, per the Jordan Hotels Association. Amman led at 59%. The source text cuts off before the figures for other destinations, so the Petra numbers are not available here.
