Japan Airlines Mileage Bank prices partner awards by total distance flown. A coach trip on American or Alaska costs 12,000 miles if all flying stays within 1,000 miles. The rules allow six segments and three stopovers, meaning stays of over 24 hours. JAL's own example links Boston, Washington and Philadelphia, returning to New York.

Mixing American and Alaska needs JAL's oneworld award. For up to 4,000 flown miles it costs 25,000 miles in coach or 48,000 in business, which includes domestic first class. That allows eight flights and seven stopovers. A purely domestic round trip is permitted. Connections count towards the eight flights.

Miles are easier to get than they were. Bilt, Rove and annual-fee Citi ThankYou cards transfer 1:1, so the 12,000-mile award costs 12,000 points. Capital One transfers at 1,000 to 750, making it 16,000 miles. Non-fee Citi cards transfer at 1,000 to 700.

The limits are real. The 12,000-mile price is tight once you add cities. Every flight needs award space JAL can book, so check before transferring. Routing changes and airline substitutions are not allowed, and unused segments are not refunded. Awards are restricted to you and eligible family members.
