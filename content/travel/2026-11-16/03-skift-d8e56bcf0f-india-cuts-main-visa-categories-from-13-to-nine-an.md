India has simplified its visa system. The number of main categories falls from 13 to nine, and sub-categories drop from 49 to 23. The home ministry says the information required on application forms is cut by as much as 70%. The revised system should go live by the end of 2026.

This is the second overhaul in 13 months, following an earlier consolidation in October 2025. For independent travellers, the practical effect should be fewer categories to choose from and shorter forms. The source text does not say which categories were merged or how fees and processing times change.

The context is weak inbound numbers. India received 9.2 million foreign tourists in 2025, down 9.4% on 2024 and about 16% below the 2019 peak of 10.9 million. The government targets 100 million visitors by 2047. Skift argues an easier visa alone will not close that gap. The article is paywalled, so the rest of its argument is not available here.
