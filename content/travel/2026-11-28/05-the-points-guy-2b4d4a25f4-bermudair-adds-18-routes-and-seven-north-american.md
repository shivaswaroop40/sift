BermudAir has announced 18 new routes. They add seven North American airports: New Orleans, Nashville, New Haven, Burlington, Portland (Maine), San Antonio and Fredericton. New destinations are Ocho Rios in Jamaica, San Salvador and San Pedro Sula. Most flights are twice weekly or up to twice weekly, and run from May or June to September.

Several routes link two non-Bermudian points, such as New Orleans to Grand Cayman, Providenciales or Ocho Rios, and San Antonio to Belize. BermudAir says it will operate these as fifth freedom flights originating in Bermuda, Turks and Caicos, Grand Cayman or Anguilla. All are British Overseas Territories, which the source says appears to satisfy the regulations.

For travellers in smaller US cities, this means possible nonstop Caribbean and Central American options that rarely exist. Examples include Nashville to Ocho Rios and Baltimore to San Salvador. Schedules are thin and seasonal, so flexibility matters. Baltimore to Guatemala City stops in San Salvador.

BermudAir launched in 2023 as a boutique carrier using small Embraer jets. It only began flying between North America and other destinations in winter 2026-27. The source calls the growth rapid and says success would make it a regional force. That is still an if. The article gives no fares or loyalty details.
