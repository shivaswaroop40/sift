A roundup post relays reports, including a social media post by Adrian Pandev, that the US is refusing ESTA applications from former US citizens. They are being told to apply for a full visitor visa instead. This is said to apply even to people who hold, and live in, a visa waiver country, and who have visited on ESTA for 15 years.

The reports are anecdotal. The blogger says he has had first-hand accounts too, but gives no official policy statement or figures. Treat it as an emerging pattern, not a confirmed rule.

A visitor visa generally needs a consular interview and proof that the applicant will leave after the trip. For anyone with family, a business or assets in the US, that could mean delays and refusals. Anyone weighing renunciation should factor this in.
