Ryanair has quietly changed how it assigns seats to passengers who do not pay the seat selection fee. According to The Irish Times, as cited by One Mile at a Time, free seats are now allocated closer to departure rather than earlier.

The airline says the change responds to customer feedback, not a wish to raise revenue. The source writer finds that justification amusing, though he does not consider the policy itself unreasonable.
