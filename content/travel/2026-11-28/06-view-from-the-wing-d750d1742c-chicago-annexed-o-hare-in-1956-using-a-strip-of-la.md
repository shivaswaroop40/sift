In the 1950s Chicago wanted O'Hare inside its borders so it could collect the airport's tax revenue. Suburbs such as Norridge and Harwood Heights blocked a direct annexation. In 1956 Mayor Richard J. Daley annexed a thin strip of land along Higgins Road instead. At its narrowest, the strip was less than 33 feet wide.

The source adds a view on the city's finances. It says Chicago's metro economy grew 5% from 2018 to 2023, against 12 to 14% for big-city peers. It also cites an $882 million budget gap, with debt and pension payments taking about 40% of the operating budget.

The author questions how long Chicago will stay a major credit card spend market. United and American are competing hard for the city, and he thinks that still makes sense, but asks for how much longer. This is opinion, not analysis, and the source gives no figures on card spend.
