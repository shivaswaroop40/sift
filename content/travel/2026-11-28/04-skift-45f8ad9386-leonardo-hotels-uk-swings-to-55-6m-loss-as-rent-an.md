Leonardo Hotels' UK business made a pre-tax loss of £55.6 million ($74 million) in 2025, against a £50.1 million profit in 2024, according to accounts filed at Companies House. The 2024 profit included an £89.8 million one-off gain from revaluing leases. Without it, 2024 would have been a loss of about £40 million, so the loss widened by roughly £16 million.

Revenue at Leonardo Hotel Management (UK) Ltd rose 1.3% to £350.1 million, with no new hotels opened. Gross profit fell 12% to £37.6 million and gross margin slipped to 10.7% from 12.4%. Staff costs rose 6% to £102.5 million, and employer social security costs rose 34%.

Skift's reading is that the guests are paying enough to cover operating costs but not the rent. Rising wages and payroll taxes add pressure, but the lease burden is described as the bigger problem.
