Lufthansa Group has introduced a policy requiring a Travel ID for online check-in. A Travel ID is an account passengers use to log in to the group's services. The change was flagged by the aviation outlet aeroTELEGRAPH. Passengers without an account now face an extra registration step before they can check in online.

The move runs against standard practice. Most airlines encourage online check-in because it saves passengers time and saves the airline resources. A mandatory account adds friction, especially for infrequent flyers who simply want a boarding pass. If you fly Lufthansa Group carriers, be prepared to register before checking in from home.

Details in the report are thin. It does not state when the requirement takes effect, which airlines within the group it covers, or whether any exceptions apply. Treat this as an early signal rather than a confirmed final policy.
