Emirates has released its latest financial results, and profits plunged. The airline still made money despite the fall. The report attributes the drop to the war between the United States and Iran, which has pushed up jet fuel prices across the industry and brought attacks on airports across the Gulf.

This matters for anyone routing through the Gulf. Airports in the region have been attacked during the conflict, and the report says some people are reconsidering travel through the area. Jet fuel prices are up across the industry. Those pressures can shape fares, capacity and schedules on the long-haul routes that Gulf carriers serve. Emirates staying profitable suggests the region's hubs are still functioning.

One caveat. The excerpt is truncated and carries no figures. It does not give the size of the profit fall, the exact reporting period, or any management comment. The detail behind the headline is thin here, so treat the numbers as unverified until the full report is read.
