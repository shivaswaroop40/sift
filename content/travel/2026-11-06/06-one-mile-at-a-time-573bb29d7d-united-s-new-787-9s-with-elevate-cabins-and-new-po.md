United Airlines has introduced its long-awaited new Boeing 787-9s with all-new cabins, branded United Elevate. The airline presents this as the evolution of its long haul experience, and it includes an all-new Polaris business class seat. The aircraft are now in scheduled service, and the guide sets out which planes carry the new cabins and which routes they fly.

For anyone booking United long haul, the aircraft now decides whether you get the new Polaris seat or the older version. The new cabins are fitted only to specific 787-9s, so route and aircraft selection matters when planning a business class trip. The specific route list sits in the full guide rather than this excerpt.
