United Airlines will fly nonstop from Los Angeles and San Francisco to Traverse City, Michigan, next summer. Cherry Capital Airport says the routes give it its first coast-to-coast nonstop service. Flights run on Saturdays from June 19 to September 11, with extra Wednesday flights from June 30 to July 7 and September 1 to 8. United will use a Boeing 737-800 with 16 first-class seats and 150 in the main cabin. Tickets are on sale now.

The service removes a connection for West Coast travellers heading to northern Michigan in high summer. Traverse City sits on Grand Traverse Bay, an arm of Lake Michigan, and hosts the century-old National Cherry Festival each year. The added Wednesday flights fall before and after the festival. United already serves the airport from Chicago, Denver, Houston and Washington Dulles.

United also announced a new San Francisco route to Jacksonville, starting March 29 and running twice a week year-round. Counting that and the San Francisco-Traverse City link, United has announced seven new routes from its Bay Area hub in recent weeks. Several are transcontinental, including Hartford, Richmond, Norfolk and Charleston.
