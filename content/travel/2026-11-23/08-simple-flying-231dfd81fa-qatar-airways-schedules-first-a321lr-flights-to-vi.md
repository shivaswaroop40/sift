Qatar Airways has filed its first two Airbus A321LR routes, both from Doha. Daily service to Vienna is due to start on 4 January 2027, with Zurich following on 16 January. The aircraft has not yet been delivered, though delivery is expected at the end of 2026. Dates could change.

The 160-seat A321LR will replace a 132-seat A320ceo on Vienna, on QR189 and QR190 from 5 January. On Zurich it replaces the 283-seat A350-900, on QR93 and QR94. It will be the first Qatar narrowbody to Zurich since 2013. Qatar's order covers ten A321LRs.

Each aircraft has 16 lie-flat business seats in a reverse herringbone layout with a privacy door, plus 144 economy seats. Narrowbodies will make up only 11% of Qatar's Doha-Europe departures in the first half of 2027, against 73% at Gulf Air and 35% at Etihad.
