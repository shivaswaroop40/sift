American has begun withholding its own saver-level award space close to departure. The change was introduced in May 2026. At first, all domestic nonstop saver awards within six days of departure were pulled from sale.

The biggest effect falls on people booking American flights through partner programmes, such as Alaska Atmos Rewards or British Airways Club. Anyone using a programme other than AAdvantage is affected, because those bookings rely on saver space.

The source text is cut off after the initial restriction, so it is unclear how the policy has developed since. Check the original for any later changes before relying on last-minute partner awards.
