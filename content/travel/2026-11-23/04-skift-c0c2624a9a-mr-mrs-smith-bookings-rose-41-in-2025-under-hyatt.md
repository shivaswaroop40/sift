Smith Global, the UK company behind Mr & Mrs Smith, made 209,565 bookings in 2025, up 41% on the year before, according to accounts filed at Companies House. More than half came from World of Hyatt members. Total booking value rose 19%, but the average booking fell about 16% to around £633 ($842). The UK business posted a loss.

Hyatt paid £58 million ($72 million) in cash for the boutique hotel booker in June 2023. The aim was to bring boutique and independent hotels into its free loyalty programme. About half the collection, more than 1,250 properties, can now be booked on hyatt.com, according to Hyatt.

For travellers, the deal means more boutique hotels can be booked and paid for with World of Hyatt in mind. The source text does not say how that changes rates, perks or availability on the Smith site itself.

The accounts do not explain the fall in average booking value, so it is unclear whether it reflects cheaper hotels or something else. Skift notes Hyatt has taken a goodwill charge in the segment that includes Smith, and the wider payoff is not broken out. The text is truncated, so further detail is missing.
