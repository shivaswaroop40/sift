Air traffic controllers at Skeyes, Belgium's air navigation provider, said they could not safely provide normal service from 7am to 9pm local time on Monday. Some staff had joined a nationwide demonstration against government austerity measures. Only emergency and safety-critical flights would be supported.

Brussels Airport said every arrival and departure in that 14-hour window would be cancelled. Charleroi Airport said its whole Monday schedule would be affected. The airline body BAR Belgium called it the 11th strike affecting Belgian airspace in 11 months and demanded stronger safeguards against strike disruption.
