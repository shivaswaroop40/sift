Sant Pere dels Arquells, a village of about sixty people in rural Catalonia, holds an unusual aviation display. Three Soviet fighter jets stand in a private garden, alongside one American fighter jet. Atlas Obscura describes it as perhaps the strangest aviation museum in Spain.

The village sits right beside the motorway, so it is easy to reach by car without a long detour. For a traveller who prefers odd, story-led stops to resorts, it is a possible break on a drive through inland Catalonia.
