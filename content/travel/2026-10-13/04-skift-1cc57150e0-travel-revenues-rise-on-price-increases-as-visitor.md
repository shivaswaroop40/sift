Global travel performance flatlined in August 2026 versus a year earlier, according to Skift's Travel Health Index. Airfares and hotel rates have risen across several markets, but traveller numbers have not grown. Visitors are becoming more selective about when and where they travel.

This matters because it shows the industry is generating more revenue without expanding its customer base. Higher prices are masking weak underlying demand. Affordability pressures mean travellers are cutting trips or choosing cheaper destinations.

The pattern suggests uneven demand across markets. Revenue growth depends on sustained pricing power, which may face pressure if price-sensitive travellers defer or skip bookings.
