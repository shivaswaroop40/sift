Etihad Rail opened its first passenger service between Dubai and Abu Dhabi on 30 September, with trains departing from Al Yalayis Station near Jumeirah Golf Estates. The journey takes one hour and four minutes at an average speed of 200 kilometres per hour. Comfort tickets start at Dh39 online, Premium at Dh129. Onboard amenities include free Wi-Fi, charging ports, Arabic coffee and a snack box.

This route replaces the standard two-hour car journey on Sheikh Zayed Road. Passengers gain time and avoid traffic while the service integrates with Dubai Metro via a pedestrian bridge and Nol card system. The Abu Dhabi terminus is at Mohamed bin Zayed City, with shuttle buses to Corniche Road, ADNEC Centre and Reem Island.

A faster high-speed service will launch in the coming months, cutting journey time to 35 minutes. Etihad Rail plans to open ten passenger stations by March 2027 as part of its phased expansion across the UAE.
