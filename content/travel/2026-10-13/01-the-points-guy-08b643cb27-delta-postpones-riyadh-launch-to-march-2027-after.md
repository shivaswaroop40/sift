Delta Air Lines has delayed its planned October debut of nonstop flights from Atlanta to Riyadh until at least March 2027. The airline had scheduled daily service to start on 23 October, before moving to three times weekly. Strikes on King Khalid International Airport last week killed at least 16 people and damaged terminals and aircraft.

The route matters to independent travellers because it would be the first nonstop US carrier service between America and Saudi Arabia, opening access to Gulf and Indian subcontinent connections. Delta is also using it to build a partnership with Riyadh Air, matching relationships its competitors already hold in the region.

Delta says the route remains important and it is committed long-term. The airline cites safety considerations for the delay. Early reports suggested bookings were weak, so few passengers are immediately affected. Riyadh airport was scheduled to reopen on 17 October with multiple carriers suspending service.
