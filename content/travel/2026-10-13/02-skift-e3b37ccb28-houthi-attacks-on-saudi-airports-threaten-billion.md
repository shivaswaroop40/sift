Yemen's Houthi movement has carried out repeated attacks on Saudi airports over one week, targeting King Khalid International in Riyadh multiple times. The group has explicitly warned international airlines and travellers to avoid Saudi airspace, claiming airports as targets in their ongoing conflict with Riyadh.

Saudi Arabia has invested heavily in aviation infrastructure and tourism as part of a strategic economic diversification plan. These coordinated strikes directly undermine that ambition by raising safety concerns and deterring international connectivity.

The sustained nature of the campaign represents an unusual threat to commercial aviation. A single incident would be manageable; repeated attacks within days signal a shift in the conflict's scope and targeting.
