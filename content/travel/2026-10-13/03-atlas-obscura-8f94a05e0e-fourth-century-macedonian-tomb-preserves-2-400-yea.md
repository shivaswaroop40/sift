A late 4th-century BC Macedonian tomb near Agios Athanasios holds frescoes in nearly perfect condition, protected beneath a 12-metre burial mound and concrete shell. Ancient grave robbers took the gold but left the paintings untouched.

For travellers seeking places with historical depth, this site offers rare access to detailed ancient artwork. The tomb's facade features golden-winged griffins, a symposium scene with six reclining banqueters and musicians, and life-sized guardian figures.

The frescoes capture vivid scenes of Macedonian life: a banquet with garlanded men, female musicians, hunters, horses, and armed infantry. The preservation is exceptional enough to show individual details across 2,400 years.
