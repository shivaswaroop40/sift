Delta Air Lines has delayed the start of its planned nonstop service from Atlanta to Riyadh to March 2027. The airline cited a series of attacks on airports across Saudi Arabia and said safety considerations guided the decision.

For independent travellers planning routes to the Middle East, this affects options for reaching Riyadh from the US. The delay removes a direct transatlantic connection that was due to launch this month, forcing a return to connecting flights for at least the next five months.
