Foreign airlines are starting to come back to Dubai, but not to Riyadh. Dubai's return is tied to 31 January, when the European Union Aviation Safety Agency's conflict-zone bulletins for the Middle East expire. Riyadh's depends on Iran-backed Houthi strikes stopping. No foreign long-haul airline plans to fly there before January.

Hotel figures show the gap. According to CoStar's STR, Riyadh occupancy fell to 34.3% in October from 69% a year earlier. Dubai fell to 68.4% from 84.9%. Perfetti Hospitality's CEO, Turab Saleem, said cancellations and postponements are rising across the Saudi hotels it advises, with corporate and group business hit hardest.

The source text is cut off by a paywall. It says Lufthansa Group's return to Dubai is partial, and that it restarted Munich-Dubai, but the rest is missing. Details on other carriers are not available here.
