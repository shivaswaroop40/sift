Virgin Atlantic is dropping its Heathrow to Cancun route. The final flight is on 11 April 2027, and the seasonal service will not return in winter 2027/28. It began in October 2025, making it the first Heathrow link to Mexico other than the capital. Virgin cited a review of its flying programme and pointed to Phuket, the Maldives and South Africa for winter sun.

The route ran three times a week. Virgin used a 258-seat Boeing 787-9 in the first season, then a 397-seat Airbus A350-1000 from October 2026. Weekly round-trip seats rose 54%, from 1,548 to 2,382. Business class seats fell 48%, from 186 to 96, while premium economy rose from 210 to 336.

UK Civil Aviation Authority data shows 35,790 passengers in the first season, a load factor of about 87.8% against 40,764 seats. The author infers that demand was good but yields were disappointing. Fare data is not available, so that is an assumption. Virgin also flew Cancun from Gatwick between 2012 and 2019.
