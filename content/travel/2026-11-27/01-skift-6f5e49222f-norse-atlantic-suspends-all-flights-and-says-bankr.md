Norse Atlantic Airways suspended all its flights on Thursday. The board said it intends to file for bankruptcy unless a short-term financing solution is found urgently. The Norway-based low-cost, long-haul carrier had called its financial position "critical" a day earlier.

Norse is talking to investors about a possible takeover that would include financing. It met holders of its $52 million senior secured bonds on Wednesday. It warned there is no guarantee a viable solution will emerge. It apologised to passengers but gave no details on refunds or alternative travel.

Anyone holding a Norse booking has no stated route to a refund or rebooking for now. Skift's own summary says the cash crisis reaches beyond Norse, with airline and cruise partners reassessing their flying plans. The source text is cut off, so further detail from the CEO is missing.
