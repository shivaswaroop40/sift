Viracopos Airport in Campinas, São Paulo, is donating a retired ex-VASP Boeing 737-200, registration PP-SMR, to the city of Pedreira, about 40 km away. The jet last flew in 2004 and has served as a fire training aid at the airport since. It is due to go on display at the Morro do Cristo Tourist Complex.

The plan is at an early stage. Pedreira City Hall announced it last week after meeting the airport's operators. The exact spot within the complex is undecided, and no opening date has been given. Pedreira once displayed an ex-VASP Vickers Viscount there, from 1981 to 1993, before a fire destroyed it.

The jet first flew in July 1969 and was delivered to Pacific Southwest Airlines as N984PS. It joined VASP in May 1974 and was impounded in September 2004 after reaching its cycle limit. VASP stopped operating in January 2005. Its layout seated 107 in a single economy cabin.
