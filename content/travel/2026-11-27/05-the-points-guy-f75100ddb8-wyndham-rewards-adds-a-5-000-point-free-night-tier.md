Wyndham Rewards has changed its award chart. It adds a new lowest tier of 5,000 points for a free night and a new top tier of 45,000 points. The writer tested the cheap end with a stay at a Super 8 near Kansas City International Airport.

The review found a clean, comfortable room with complimentary breakfast. The decor was dated and amenities were minimal. The writer says the stay beat expectations for a property with a low rating, and it gave a restful night before an early flight.

The new top tier is a devaluation for higher-end properties, which now cost up to 45,000 points. The 5,000-point tier is limited to select locations, so the cheap rate will not apply everywhere.

The summary says some transferable points programmes move to Wyndham at favourable ratios, and eligible cardholders may get discounts on free-night redemptions. It gives no specific ratios or discount figures, so check the terms before transferring.
