American Airlines and Israir signed an agreement on 19 November. Israir will put its code on American's JFK to Tel Aviv flight once American returns, planned for daily service from 27 March 2027 with Boeing 777-200ERs. An interline deal will also connect passengers beyond JFK across the US, Canada, Latin America and the Caribbean.

Israir flies JFK to Tel Aviv five times a week on two of American's retired A330s. American has not flown to Israel since 7 October 2023 and has delayed its return before. Israir's filing targets initial implementation by the end of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals, technology and operational readiness.

Israir says the deal widens its potential market by 20 to 25 per cent, worth $20 to 40 million a year. The filing describes that as addressable market, not a revenue forecast. American has not said it will put its code on Israir's own A330 flights.
