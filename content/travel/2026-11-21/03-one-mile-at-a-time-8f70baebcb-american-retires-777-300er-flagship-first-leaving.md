American Airlines has officially retired Flagship First on its Boeing 777-300ER. As a result, no US carrier now sells an international first class product. The airline had been phasing out the cabin, and it no longer sells those seats.

The source says an updated business class product is being rolled out on the aircraft. It describes this as something that should benefit a lot of travellers, though the excerpt gives no detail on the new seat, timing or which aircraft come first.
