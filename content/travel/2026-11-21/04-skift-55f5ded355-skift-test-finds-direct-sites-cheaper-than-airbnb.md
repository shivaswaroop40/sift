Skift used an AI agent called Instinct to compare 16 Airbnb listings with the rates on each property's own website. The direct site was cheaper in 10 cases, by as much as 42%. One was essentially a tie. The article is paywalled, so the detail on the other five is not available.

The test followed a post on X asking whether AI agents checking prices at the point of booking would hurt Airbnb's profits. Chief executive Brian Chesky replied: "You presume we could never offer a price-match guarantee." It was not an announcement. Airbnb recently started price-matching for hotels, not short-term rentals.

Sixteen listings is a small sample, and the source text does not explain how they were chosen or whether fees and taxes were compared like for like. Airbnb could also argue that its trust and safety backing is worth something that direct sites may not offer.
