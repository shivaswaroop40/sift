Parvus Asset Management, which holds about 14.1% of Accor, said on Thursday it will ask shareholders to remove Sébastien Bazin as chairman and CEO at the next annual meeting. It cited Accor's €1.6 billion buyout of its partners in the Ennismore joint venture and years of weak returns. The meeting date has not been set. Annual meetings are usually held in May.

Parvus published a letter setting out its case. It says Accor shares returned 99% from December 2013 to November 2026, against 593% for IHG, 863% for Hilton and 950% for Marriott. It blames an unfocused strategy, inconsistent capital allocation and weak governance oversight. Accor did not respond to Skift's request for comment.
