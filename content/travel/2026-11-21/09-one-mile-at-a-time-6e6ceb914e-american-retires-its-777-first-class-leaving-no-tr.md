American has formally retired first class on its Boeing 777. With that, no US airline now offers a true international first class cabin. The source calls it the end of an era, as US carriers pioneered many premium products over past decades.

The piece asks why US airlines cannot make first class economically viable, even as they keep stressing how premium they are. The text provided is truncated, so the reasons and the argument that it shouldn't have failed are not included here.
