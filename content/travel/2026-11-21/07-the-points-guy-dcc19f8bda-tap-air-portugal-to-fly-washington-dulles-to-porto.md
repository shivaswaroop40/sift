TAP Air Portugal will start a thrice-weekly service from Washington Dulles (IAD) to Porto (OPO) on 30 March 2027. Westbound flights run on Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays through the summer. From October they move to Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays. The route will use an Airbus A321LR with 16 lie-flat business seats and 155 economy seats.

The route gives Washington a second TAP link to Portugal, alongside the existing Lisbon service. TAP is a Star Alliance partner of United, so the new flights should suit travellers who want to use United miles or status. Porto is a useful entry point for northern Portugal without a connection through Lisbon.

The source says Lufthansa Group plans to buy an initial 44.9% of TAP, aiming to close by the first quarter of 2028. Lufthansa intends to bring TAP into its transatlantic joint venture with Air Canada and United afterwards. That could change how fares and partner awards work on TAP, though the source gives no detail.

Dulles is adding other long-haul routes, including United to Toulouse in April and Milan in May. Cirium data shows international seats from IAD rising nearly 7% in the first half of 2027. A $22 billion redevelopment is under way, and a new Concourse E section has opened.
