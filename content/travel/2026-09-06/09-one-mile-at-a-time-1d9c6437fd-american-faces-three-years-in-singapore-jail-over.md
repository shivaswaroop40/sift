A 41-year-old American woman posed as the sister of a Marriott employee to obtain a discounted stay at a Singapore hotel. She now faces up to three years in prison under Singapore law, where fraud is treated more seriously than in many other jurisdictions.

Marriott offers staff discounted rates that can sometimes be shared with family members, which is why people buy or sell these discounts online. Singapore treats fraud cases aggressively, so using a purchased employee rate there carries real legal risk rather than a minor policy violation.

Cheap rates advertised on platforms such as Craigslist may look tempting, but using them abroad can mean criminal charges in the local jurisdiction. The case is a reminder that what might be a loyalty hack in one country can become a serious offence in another.
