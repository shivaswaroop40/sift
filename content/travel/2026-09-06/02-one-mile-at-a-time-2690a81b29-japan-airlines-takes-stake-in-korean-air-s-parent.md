Japan Airlines has acquired a stake in Hanjin KAL, the parent company of Korean Air, with an expanded strategic partnership expected to follow.

The move is unusual because Korean Air sits in SkyTeam with a Delta joint venture, while Japan Airlines is in oneworld alongside American Airlines. The two carriers have historically been rivals rather than partners.

For travellers, an expanded partnership could mean new codeshare routes, smoother connections between Japan and South Korea, and the possibility of reciprocal mileage or status benefits, though details have not yet been confirmed.
