SeaPort Airlines, a Juneau-based carrier, runs scheduled Pilatus PC-12 flights between general aviation airports in the Pacific Northwest, including Boeing Field in Seattle and Felts Field in Spokane, with no TSA-style security, free parking and a 20-minute arrival window. Flights began between Seattle and Spokane in March 2026, following a Portland route in May 2025 and seasonal Redmond service this summer.

Advance bookings cost roughly $50 more than Alaska Airlines or Delta on the same corridor, but last-minute fares match the majors and seats are reliably available, which matters to self-employed and business travellers who book close to departure. Passengers can carry 50 pounds of luggage plus a small pet at no extra charge.

The aircraft seat only nine people, and routes are limited to four cities. Demand appears to be growing, with corporate travel teams now testing the service, and existing passengers worried that wider awareness will reduce last-minute availability.
