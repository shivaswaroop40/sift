The Global Entry mobile app will stop working on Sept. 15 and be replaced by a new Trusted Traveler app launching the same day, according to US federal authorities. Memberships stay active and no reapplication is required.

For travellers, nothing changes at the airport kiosks or the membership itself. Existing logins carry over and the new app performs the same functions, so current Global Entry members only need to swap apps rather than re-enrol.

The Trusted Traveler app will also cover other US Department of Transportation programmes such as Nexus and SENTRI, and is the new place to start applications or renewals. Separately, camera-based entry processing is live at Boston, Dallas/Fort Worth, JFK, Philadelphia, San Francisco and Seattle airports.
