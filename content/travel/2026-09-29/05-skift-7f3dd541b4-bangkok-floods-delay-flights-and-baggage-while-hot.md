Nearly 300 millimetres of rain fell on Bangkok from Thursday through the weekend, flooding roads and disrupting operations at Suvarnabhumi and Don Mueang airports, though both stayed open. Blocked routes stopped some Thai Airways ground staff from reaching work, causing departure delays and a baggage backlog.

Hotel demand was largely unaffected, with bookings holding steady despite the flooding across the city.

A Rangsit University study put Bangkok losses at 10.6 billion baht ($315 million), more than 40% of an estimated 25.3 billion baht in nationwide damage to commerce, services and travel.

The Thai Meteorological Department forecasts heavy rain across up to 80% of the area in the coming week, raising risks for Golden Week and the expected surge of Chinese visitors.
