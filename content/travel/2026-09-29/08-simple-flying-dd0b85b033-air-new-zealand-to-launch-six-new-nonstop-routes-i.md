Air New Zealand will add six nonstop international routes in 2026, including the first international service to Western Sydney Airport from Auckland starting October 26, three times weekly on an A320neo. The airport, on the outskirts of Penrith, will not be subject to Sydney Kingsford Smith's overnight curfew.

Long-haul flying returns to Christchurch after six years, with Boeing 787-9 services to Singapore from October 28, Tokyo Narita from November 28, and Perth from November 30, each operating three times weekly. Christchurch to Narita will be the only nonstop link between Japan and New Zealand's South Island.

The airline also launched seasonal A320neo services earlier in 2026, linking Queenstown to Brisbane from June 22 and Christchurch to Rarotonga from May 26, both running three times weekly. The Perth-Christchurch route will compete alongside Jetstar's seasonal A321LR service.

The expansion follows the return of all Air New Zealand Boeing 787-9s from storage after Rolls-Royce Trent 1000 engine issues, freeing capacity for new international flying.
