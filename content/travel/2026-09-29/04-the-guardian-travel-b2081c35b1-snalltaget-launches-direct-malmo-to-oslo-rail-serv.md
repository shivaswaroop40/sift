Snälltåget has launched a direct train from Malmö to Oslo running along Sweden's western coast, taking about 6.5 hours. Tickets start at £11 each way, and the service has carried more than 25,000 passengers since starting this summer. One daily departure leaves Malmö Central at 6.38am, with plans to add more next year.

Until now the trip required bus segments and multiple train changes. The new direct service cuts out transfers and competes on price with flights and driving, useful for independent travellers plotting a Scandinavian rail trip.

Stops include Varberg, a coastal spa town with the Moorish-style Kallbadhuset bathhouse set on wooden piles above the Kattegat, and Gothenburg, a 39-minute fast-train ride from Varberg. Malmö is also positioned as a starting point, linked to Copenhagen via the Øresund Bridge.
