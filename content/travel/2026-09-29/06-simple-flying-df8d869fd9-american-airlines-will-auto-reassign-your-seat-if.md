American Airlines has launched an automated seat-monitoring tool for AAdvantage members. Available up to 24 hours before departure, travellers opt in after booking and set a preference such as window, aisle, or front-of-cabin. The app then checks the seat map repeatedly and, if a matching seat becomes free, assigns it and notifies the customer at no extra cost.

For regular travellers, the feature removes the need to keep refreshing the seat map in the days before a flight. It is limited to eligible seats within the cabin already booked, so it will not upgrade you to a higher class, and it stops working if you have manually moved seats or are within 24 hours of departure. Paid preferred seats and extra-legroom seats still cost extra.

American says it is the first major US carrier to offer this, built through its ADEPT technology programme using AI-assisted development tools.
