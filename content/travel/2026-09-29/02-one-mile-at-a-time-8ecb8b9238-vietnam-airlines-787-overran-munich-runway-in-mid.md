In mid-August 2026, a Vietnam Airlines Boeing 787-9 overran the runway during takeoff from Munich, damaging runway lights and the aircraft. The plane eventually became airborne and none of the 287 people onboard were injured. Investigators have since revealed a cause described as bizarre, though the article excerpt does not specify it.

For travellers, runway overrun incidents are rare and this one ended safely, so the immediate flight risk is low.
