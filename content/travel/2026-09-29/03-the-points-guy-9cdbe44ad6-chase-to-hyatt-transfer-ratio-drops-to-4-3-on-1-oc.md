From 1 October, Chase Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred cardholders who opened accounts before 15 June will see their Chase-to-Hyatt transfer ratio change from 1:1 to 4:3, meaning 33% more Ultimate Rewards points are needed for the same Hyatt balance. Chase Sapphire Reserve and Sapphire Reserve for Business holders keep the 1:1 rate.

The change matters because transferred points cannot be moved back, and Hyatt points expire after 24 months without qualifying activity. Cardholders with a concrete Hyatt redemption in mind, or a track record of using Hyatt at least once or twice a year, should consider transferring before the deadline.

Those who have never redeemed with Hyatt or lack a plan for the next 18 to 24 months should hold back. Ultimate Rewards points stay more flexible at Chase, where they can fund Points Boost bookings through Chase Travel or move to other partners instead of being locked into Hyatt.
