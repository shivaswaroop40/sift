American Airlines has added an automatic seat change feature to its mobile app for AAdvantage members. The system scans for open seats that match the traveller's stored preferences, such as aisle or window, and moves them without requiring the passenger to act.

For independent travellers, this removes the need to keep refreshing the seat map in the days before departure to chase an upgrade. It only works within the same cabin and fare class, so it will not bump you into first class from economy, but it should at least improve odds of avoiding a middle seat or a bad location.
