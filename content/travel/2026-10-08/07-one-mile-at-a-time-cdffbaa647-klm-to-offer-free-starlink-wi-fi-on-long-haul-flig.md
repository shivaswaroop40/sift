KLM will introduce Starlink Wi-Fi on intercontinental flights starting mid-2027. The service will be free for all Flying Blue frequent-flyer members across all cabin classes. This makes KLM the latest European carrier to adopt Starlink after IAG, Lufthansa Group and Air France.

For independent travellers, free Wi-Fi on long-haul flights removes a significant cost and connectivity gap. It improves the flying experience on routes where KLM competes directly with carriers that already offer the service, potentially affecting route and airline choice.
