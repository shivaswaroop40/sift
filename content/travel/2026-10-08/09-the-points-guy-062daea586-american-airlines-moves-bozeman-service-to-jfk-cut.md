American Airlines is relocating its seasonal nonstop to Bozeman from LaGuardia to JFK in 2027, enabling four-days-a-week operation instead of Saturdays only. The carrier will also add twice-daily service from JFK to Seattle starting April 2027. Simultaneously, American is cutting six routes, including the LaGuardia-Bozeman flight.

These changes reflect American's effort to return to profitability as jet fuel costs have more than doubled. Fuel averaged $4.43 per gallon in October 2026, compared to $2.42 in 2025, prompting the airline to moderate summer growth. The new JFK routes support American's joint venture expansion with Alaska Airlines.

Three smaller markets—Lincoln, Provo and Tulum—will lose their nonstop American service entirely and connect only via Dallas-Fort Worth. No destination is losing service to the airline altogether.
