Icelandair will fly from Reykjavik to St. Louis four times weekly from May to October 2027, using a Boeing 737 MAX 8 with 160 seats. The seasonal service is the carrier's 16th U.S. gateway and first new American destination this year.

St. Louis is Southwest Airlines' busiest hub, and the new route creates a connection point for passengers traveling between North America and Europe via Iceland. Icelandair's stopover programme allows free stays of up to seven days in Reykjavik.

Icelandair becomes the third European airline to serve St. Louis, after British Airways and Lufthansa. A predecessor budget carrier, WOW Air, briefly operated the same route in 2018 and 2019 before collapsing.
