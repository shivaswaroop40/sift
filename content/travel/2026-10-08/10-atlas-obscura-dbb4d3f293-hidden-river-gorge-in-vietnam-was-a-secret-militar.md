Ham Ho, a gorge in Tay Son district carved by a mountain river through granite boulders and forest, contains natural stone bridges, waterfalls, and emerald pools. The remote landscape appears largely unchanged by time, accessible only to those who know the way through its maze of channels.

The site held strategic importance during military conflict, serving as a concealed training ground for armed forces. For independent travellers seeking places with layered history, Ham Ho offers both geological drama and the chance to walk where soldiers once trained in secret.

Access requires navigation through difficult terrain and knowledge of local routes. The combination of natural features and hidden historical purpose makes it a destination for travellers interested in Vietnam's interior beyond conventional tourist paths.
