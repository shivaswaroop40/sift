Sean Duffy's Transportation Department is rolling back consumer protections that shield airline passengers. The specific protections being dismantled are not detailed in the available text, but advocates say the changes benefit airlines over travellers.

For independent flyers, fewer rules mean less recourse when airlines cancel flights, lose baggage, or change schedules. If compensation rules weaken, you lose leverage to claim refunds or alternative arrangements.

Passenger advocates oppose the rollback, suggesting the changes are material enough to warrant attention from regular flyers who depend on these protections.
