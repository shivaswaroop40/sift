Southwest's September on-time arrival rate dropped more than 10 percentage points compared with September 2025, falling from 81.46% to 71.14%. Every US airline tracked by Cirium showed worse punctuality and higher cancellation rates than a year earlier, though Southwest still cancelled fewer flights than competitors at just 0.38%.

The airline has been densifying its schedule to reduce aircraft costs, adding redeyes and cutting ground time between flights. This leaves less buffer to absorb disruptions or longer boarding times caused by increased carry-ons since it stopped the free bags policy. Chief operating officer Andrew Watterson acknowledged that full aircraft were taking longer to turn, particularly in final stages.

Delta recovered strongly in September after summer problems, improving on-time arrivals to 82.52% and cutting cancellations to 0.92%. American improved too but still lags Delta by 5.41 percentage points. WestJet led North American carriers at 85.31% on-time arrivals.
