Montreal Metropolitan Airport opened on June 15, 2026, with a $450 million terminal designed to handle four million passengers yearly. Located 15 km from downtown on the South Shore, it aims to complement Montreal-Trudeau rather than replace it. Porter Airlines launches with 138 weekly flights to 12 domestic destinations.

The new airport learns from Mirabel's failure, which opened in 1975 as an isolated megaproject 55 km away and never attracted passengers. YHU's compact design and focus on convenience over scale reflects a different strategy. Successful multi-airport systems work when each airport serves distinct roles rather than competing.

YHU's 226,000 square-foot terminal includes nine boarding bridges and handles 15,000 passengers daily at peak. Porter operates Embraer E195-E2 jets and Bombardier Dash 8-400 turboprops from the facility.
