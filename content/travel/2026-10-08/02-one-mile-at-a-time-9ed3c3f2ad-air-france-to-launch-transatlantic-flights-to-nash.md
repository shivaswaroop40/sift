Air France will begin transatlantic service to Nashville, Tennessee and San Diego, California in summer 2027. The airline is reallocating aircraft from its recently cut Denver route to these new destinations.

These routes matter because they open transatlantic gateways from two mid-size US cities where Air France previously had no long-haul service. For independent travellers in these regions, direct access to Paris becomes a springboard to the wider European network.
