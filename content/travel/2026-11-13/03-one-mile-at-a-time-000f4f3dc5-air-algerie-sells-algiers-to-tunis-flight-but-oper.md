A reviewer flew Air Algerie business class from Algiers (ALG) to Tunis (TUN), a one-hour sector. The aircraft was an Airbus A320. Air Algerie does not operate A320s, so the flight was run by Avion Express, a Lithuanian and Maltese charter operator, under a wet lease.

The reviewer says Air Algerie is poor at disclosing this during booking. He flew the airline's A330neo just before this leg, and the contrast frames the review. The source text is a short extract, so it gives no detail on the seat, service or crew.
