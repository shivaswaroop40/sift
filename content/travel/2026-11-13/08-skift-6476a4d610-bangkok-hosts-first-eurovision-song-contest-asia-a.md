Bangkok hosts the first Eurovision Song Contest Asia on 14 November, with 11 countries competing at One Bangkok Forum. Skift describes it as an early test of Thailand's new tourism approach, which aims to earn more from each visitor while arrivals stall.

The Tourism Authority of Thailand governor, Thapanee Kiatphaibool, called it part of a "Value over Volume" direction. Hospitality consultant Nat Srinara expects visibility and discovery to be the main gains from this first edition, rather than large visitor spending. Skift's early data suggests the event is riding Bangkok's seasonal upswing, not driving it.

Thailand's draft tourism plan targets at least 4 trillion baht ($118 billion) in annual revenue by 2032, about 48% above 2025. The source text is cut off by a paywall after this figure, so further detail on the plan is not available.
