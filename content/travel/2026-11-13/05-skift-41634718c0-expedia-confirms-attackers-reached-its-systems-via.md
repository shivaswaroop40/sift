Expedia Group confirmed on Thursday that attackers got into its systems through a compromised third-party data platform provider. It described this as a supply chain attack that also affected multiple other companies. The confirmation followed reports on cybersecurity sites that an extortion group called n0n claimed to have stolen Expedia data.

The source text is cut off by a paywall after the start of Expedia's statement. Skift notes that the company has not disclosed how much data, if any, was exposed. The vendor is not named in the available text, and the n0n claims are allegations that the text does not verify.
