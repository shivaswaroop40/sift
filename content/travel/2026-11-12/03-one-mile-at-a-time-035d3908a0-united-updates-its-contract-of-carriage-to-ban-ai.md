United Airlines has updated its contract of carriage to prohibit passengers from using "AI agents" to book or service tickets. The contract is the set of terms a passenger accepts when buying a fare. The change was flagged by a reader, TravelinWilly, and covered by One Mile at a Time.

The source frames the move as odd for United, which it calls the country's most tech-savvy airline. It reports that United says the ban is meant to protect customers. The text supplied is cut off after the start of the section on prohibited conduct, so the exact wording is not available here.

For independent travellers, this matters if you use automated tools to search fares, rebook or handle changes. A clause in the contract of carriage can in theory be used against a passenger who breaks it. How United would enforce it, and what counts as an AI agent, is not clear from the text provided.
