A deadly flash flood hit Grand Canyon National Park over the weekend. Search and rescue is under way along the Colorado River, and some visitors are still unaccounted for. Phantom Ranch, its cabins and canteen, Bright Angel Campground and the whole North Kaibab Trail to Phantom Ranch are closed until further notice. The Silver and Black footbridges were affected.

Damage to the Transcanyon Waterline means the South Rim hotels close at noon on Monday 31 August. All South Rim water comes through that line, and reserves are low. Grand Canyon Lodges projects a reopening on 20 November 2026. Only dry camping is allowed. The park stays open for day use and food and drink services continue, some with shorter hours.

The Colorado River is closed to river traffic because metal structures and debris have entered the water. Rafting customers should contact their commercial outfitter or the NPS River Permits office. Hotels and restaurants in Tusayan, outside the park at the Route 64 entrance, are unaffected.
