Delhi recorded a US Air Quality Index reading of 581 on the morning of Saturday 7 November, according to reports citing IQAir data. That made it the most polluted major city in the world at that moment. Readings of 301 and above count as hazardous. By the time of writing, the hourly figure had fallen to 176, second only to Lahore.

The region sits on the Indo-Gangetic Plain, where the Himalayas stop air flowing north. Pollution is trapped from late October to February. Diwali firecrackers often make Delhi's air worse. October to March is also the busiest period for foreign visitors, and Delhi is a common entry point.
