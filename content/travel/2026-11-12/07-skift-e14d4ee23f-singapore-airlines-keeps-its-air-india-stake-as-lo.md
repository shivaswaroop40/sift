Singapore Airlines is holding its 25.1% stake in Air India. For the six months to 30 September it reported a net profit of S$134 million ($104 million), down 44%, on record revenue of S$11.3 billion. Its share of Air India's losses was S$361.7 million, nearly 2.7 times that profit.

The loss share was smaller than the S$429.5 million a year earlier. The improvement came in the second quarter, when losses from associated companies, mainly Air India, were S$110 million lower year on year. Across the half the gain was only S$68 million, so first-quarter losses were worse than a year before.
