Citi's Abhinav Anand, head of value cards, lending and commerce for U.S. consumer cards, said liability for mistaken AI bookings should fall on the company behind the agent. He spoke at the Skift Commerce Summit in New York on 11 November 2026. He said it should not fall on the hotel, airline or card issuer.

He called liability the biggest open question for banks, payment networks, merchants and AI companies. One idea he raised is that agents wanting to transact on Visa or Mastercard might have to accept some liability. The source text cuts off mid-sentence, so the detail of that proposal is missing.

Skift's own take is that this would spare hotels and airlines but could slow how quickly agents can transact. This is one bank's stated view, not an agreed industry rule.
