The Tren dels Llacs runs on a single track from Lleida to La Pobla de Segur. The route is 55 miles (89km) with 40 tunnels and 75 bridges. A 1960s Tren Historic carries a period dining car, and a modern Tren Panoramic is the alternative.

The writer used request stops to reach the Pallars Jussà area. Santa Linya station has no facilities, and a 3.5-mile hike with a 190-metre climb leads to the village. A further four miles east lies the Mirador del Balconet, a cliff viewpoint 300 metres above the Collegats Gorge.

Bikes can be hired in Tremp. Autumn temperatures in the foothills are described as reliably pleasant into November. Hotel Terradets, near Cellers and also on the line, offers bike and kayak hire, a workshop and a lakeside setting.
