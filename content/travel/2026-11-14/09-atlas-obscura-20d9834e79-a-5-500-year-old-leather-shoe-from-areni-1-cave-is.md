A leather shoe found during excavations in Areni-1 cave in 2008 is now listed as a visitor stop in Yerevan, Armenia. It is made from a single piece of cowhide and still has its leather laces. Radiocarbon dating places it at 3600 to 3500 BC.

The shoe is roughly a modern US size 7, EU 37 or UK 6. It was stuffed with grass, either to hold its shape or to keep the foot warm. Researchers do not yet know whether it belonged to a man or a woman.
