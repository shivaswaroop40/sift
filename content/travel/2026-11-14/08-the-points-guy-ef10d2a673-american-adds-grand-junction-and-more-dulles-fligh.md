American Airlines has announced changes to nine routes from Los Angeles International. Grand Junction, Colorado, returns for the first time since 2021, as seasonal service from 27 May to 9 August on a CRJ700. Reno becomes year-round at three flights a day from 27 May. Bozeman, Montana, returns seasonally over the same dates. Both use Embraer 175 jets.

Denver, Las Vegas and Seattle get extra daily frequencies from next spring. Washington Dulles doubles to twice daily from May. American will end Cleveland and Des Moines service from LAX on 11 February. The source gives no frequency figures for Denver, Las Vegas or Seattle.

For travellers, the Grand Junction flight matters in 2027, when it effectively replaces American's Aspen service. Aspen's airport will close next summer for construction. Anyone planning a western Colorado trip from Los Angeles gets a one-airline option, though on a small regional jet.

The changes follow comments from chief commercial officer Nat Pieper, who said American will grow and invest in the local LA market. He said expansion will be gradual and will build as LAX infrastructure work progresses over the next two years. LAX service still sharply lags its 2019 level.
