The FAA issued a nationwide ground stop that was not fully lifted for more than four hours. One Mile at a Time describes it as the biggest grounding of US flights since 9/11. Around 8PM that evening, Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy and Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin held a press conference.

The article's headline says the press conference involved self-praise, a $50 billion funding request and conflicting stories. The source text supplied is cut off after the opening lines. It does not give the cause of the ground stop, the details of the ask or what the accounts disagreed on.
