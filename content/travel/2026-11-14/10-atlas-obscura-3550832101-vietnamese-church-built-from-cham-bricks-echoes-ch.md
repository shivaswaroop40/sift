Xuan Phong Church in Tuy Phuoc Dong, Vietnam, began as a modest thatched sanctuary in the late nineteenth century. The current building dates to around 1890. It was built with old Cham bricks salvaged from the nearby Binh Lam area.

The facade has three tall spires with tapered profiles that resemble traditional Cham towers. Church archives have also compared its structure to Khmer temple architecture. The church was recently restored in a white-and-purple colour scheme and remains an active place of worship.
