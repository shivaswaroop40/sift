Alaska Airlines will start its first nonstop Anchorage to Tokyo Narita service on 13 May 2027, flying daily until 7 September. It will return in January 2028 at three flights a week. AS21 leaves Anchorage at 2:10 PM and lands at Narita at 3:25 PM the next day. AS22 leaves Narita at 9:45 PM and reaches Anchorage at noon the same day.

The aircraft is a 737 MAX 8, the only 737 in Alaska's fleet with enough range. The route is about 2,980 nautical miles, against a quoted MAX 8 range of roughly 3,500. Westbound flying time is up to eight hours and 15 minutes. The larger MAX 9 and MAX 10 have shorter listed ranges of 3,300 and 3,100 nautical miles.

The cabin is Alaska's standard domestic layout with 161 seats, 16 of them in First. First has recliner seats, not lie-flat beds, and there are no seatback screens. Alaska promises multi-course dining in First, free meals in Main Cabin and Premium Class, and Starlink Wi-Fi on select flights. Introductory return fares started from $999 for travel between 13 May and 7 June 2027.
