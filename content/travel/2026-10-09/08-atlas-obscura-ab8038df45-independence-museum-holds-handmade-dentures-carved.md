George Washington Hancock, a rancher in Independence, California, carved his own dentures from animal teeth around the early 1900s when he could not afford a dentist. The Eastern California Museum now displays this set as part of its collection, a physical record of frontier ingenuity and hardship.

For independent travellers exploring rural California and small-town museums, this object offers genuine historical texture. It tells you something true about how people actually lived in the American West, without resort to sentiment or spectacle.
