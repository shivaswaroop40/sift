Delta Air Lines lowered its 2026 earnings guidance to $5.10 to $5.60 per share, down from $6.50 to $7.50, citing jet fuel prices averaging $4.50 per gallon. The carrier expects an additional $6 billion in fuel expenses this year.

Fuel price volatility directly threatens airline profitability and affects ticket pricing strategy. Independent travellers should watch airline earnings revisions as signals of potential fare increases or route changes ahead.

Delta remained profitable in the third quarter and has already set aside $900 million for employee profit sharing in 2027, suggesting the airline expects to weather this cost pressure.
