On 8 October 2026, Yemen-based Houthis attacked King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh and destroyed a Saudia Airbus A320. The group warned that no aircraft in Saudi Arabian airspace is safe, part of an escalation in their campaign against the country.

For independent travellers, this creates immediate uncertainty about flights to and from Saudi Arabia. Route availability, fares, and schedules may shift if airlines reduce operations or reroute services. The risk assessment for visiting Saudi Arabia or connecting through Riyadh has changed.

The Houthis' warning appears designed to deter airlines and passengers rather than reflect an absolute operational closure. King Khalid remains open, but carriers will now factor attack risk into their decisions.
