More than 26 percent of flights leaving Saudi Arabia were disrupted on Thursday as Houthi rebels targeted Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport and Abha International Airport. Cirium tracked 312 cancellations or failed departures out of 1,194 scheduled flights. At Riyadh airport alone, nearly half of departures were affected by late Thursday.

The attacks undermine the Gulf hub model that depends on reliable connections. With major airports now military targets, airlines and passengers face unpredictable disruptions to regional and long-haul itineraries. This threatens the competitive advantage these hubs hold over European and Asian alternatives.
