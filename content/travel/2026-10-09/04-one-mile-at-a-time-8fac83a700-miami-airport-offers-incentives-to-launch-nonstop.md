Miami International Airport is pursuing nonstop service to Tokyo by offering financial incentives to airlines. The airport has pursued this route for years but officials are now escalating efforts with concrete proposals to attract carriers.

For independent travellers based in or near Miami, a nonstop Tokyo connection would create a new hub option. It would avoid Los Angeles or San Francisco layovers and open routing flexibility across Asia.
