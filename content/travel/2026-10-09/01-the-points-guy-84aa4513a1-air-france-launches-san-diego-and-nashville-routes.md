Air France will start nonstop service from Paris to San Diego on May 3, 2027, and to Nashville on May 14, 2027. Both routes operate three times weekly on the same days, using an Airbus A350-900. The Nashville flight number, AF46, marks Dolly Parton's birth year.

The expansion marks Air France's continued growth in the U.S., though it comes at Denver's expense. The airline will end its seasonal Denver service on October 10 after six years, reallocating that capacity elsewhere in the network.

Air France's moves align with broader SkyTeam expansion. Partner Delta is adding three European routes from Austin, Boston, and Detroit in 2027, while United will launch daily Denver-Paris service.
