American Airlines will fit Starlink to its entire mainline fleet of more than 1,030 aircraft, including widebodies, beginning installations in early 2027. The expansion includes more than 500 Airbus narrowbodies, over 400 Boeing narrowbodies, and over 130 Boeing widebodies. American will use Starlink's Performance Aero Terminal, capable of up to 1 gigabit per second.

This matters because American's older widebody aircraft have poor Panasonic internet, a significant weakness for long-haul passengers who want to work. Starlink's lower latency, from satellites closer to earth, offers a markedly better experience. Fast wifi can justify premium ticket prices and support higher revenue.

American Eagle regional jets are excluded from the plan. Regional two-cabin jets recently got satellite wifi, but smaller regional aircraft remain without service. American has not announced which Boeing aircraft will receive equipment first or when installations will complete.
