Air Canada Premium Economy fares are running at roughly 60% of Signature Class (business) on comparable routes in 2026. A sample Toronto-Vancouver fare showed Premium Economy at CA$850 versus Signature Class at CA$1,401. The premium cabin offers 38-inch seat pitch and 19.5-20 inch width with priority services and two checked bags, but no lie-flat bed.

For independent travellers, the choice hinges on flight direction and duration. On westbound daytime transatlantic routes, Premium Economy's extra space and recline become more valuable since passengers spend much of the flight awake. The bed advantage of business class matters far less when you won't use it for eight hours.

Air Canada's new fleet expansion sharpens this distinction. The A321XLR brings lie-flat business class to narrowbody transatlantic routes for the first time, while the 787-10 adds Signature Plus with enhanced privacy. These changes make the premium cabin hierarchy clearer on specific routes and flight times.
