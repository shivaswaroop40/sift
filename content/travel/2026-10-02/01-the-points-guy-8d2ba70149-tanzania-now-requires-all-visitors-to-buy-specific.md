From October 1, 2026, most foreign tourists entering Tanzania must purchase a mandated travel insurance policy through either National Insurance Corporation (mainland) or Zanzibar Insurance Corporation (islands). This applies regardless of existing trip insurance or credit card coverage. Transit passengers, diplomats and certain permit holders are exempt.

The policy costs around $44 per adult and $22 per child aged 3-17, valid for up to 92 days with multiple re-entry allowed. It covers emergency assistance, accidents, health emergencies, repatriation, baggage delays and theft. You must buy the correct policy at your point of entry or risk being turned away.

This aligns with an existing Zanzibar rule and follows Kenya's similar mandate. While a modest cost addition to safari travel budgets, the requirement is mandatory and non-negotiable for entry.
