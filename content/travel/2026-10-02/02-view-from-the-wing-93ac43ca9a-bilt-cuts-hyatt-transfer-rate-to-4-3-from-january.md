Bilt Rewards will reduce transfers to Hyatt from 1:1 to 4:3 effective January 1, mirroring a recent change by Chase on its Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred cards. Chase Sapphire Reserve remains the only major Chase card offering 1:1 transfers to Hyatt.

The shift suggests Hyatt pushed for better terms across multiple card programmes rather than Chase or Bilt voluntarily reducing value. Both card issuers have made the change simultaneously, indicating pressure from the hotel chain rather than card-issuer reluctance.

Members have until December 31 to transfer points at the current 1:1 rate. The source advises against waiting until the last day, as systems often become overwhelmed during deadline rushes and Bilt's ability to resolve technical issues under new arrangements is uncertain.
