A FlyDubai Boeing 737 MAX 8 descending from Dubai to Tel Aviv suffered a rapid altitude loss of 15,000 feet in about one minute. The aircraft transmitted a hijacking code during the descent. The structural damage was severe enough that the rudder tore away from the tail.

The incident raises serious questions about flight safety on Middle Eastern routes, particularly those serving Israel. For independent travellers using budget carriers in the region, understanding what triggered such a failure matters when evaluating risk on these routes.
