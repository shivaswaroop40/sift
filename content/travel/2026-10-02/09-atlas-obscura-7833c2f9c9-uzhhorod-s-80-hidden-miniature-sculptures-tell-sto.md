More than 80 miniature sculptures are scattered throughout Uzhhorod, hidden in alleyways, on railings, and beside cultural monuments. They capture pieces of Transcarpathian culture and local life, from internet memes to regional figures and outsiders with tenuous local ties like Andy Warhol, Mozart and Harry Houdini.

For travellers planning their own routes through Ukraine, these sculptures offer a way to discover the character and humour of a city often overlooked in favour of better-known destinations. They reward the kind of wandering exploration that independent travellers prefer.
