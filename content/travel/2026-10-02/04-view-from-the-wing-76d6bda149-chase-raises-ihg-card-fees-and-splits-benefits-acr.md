Chase is restructuring its IHG credit cards, raising fees across the range and launching a new $350 Premier Select card. The Premier card's annual fee rises to $150, the Business card to $200, and the free IHG One Rewards card now requires $5,000 spending for its fourth night free benefit. Anniversary free night certificates are growing in value to offset some increases.

The changes matter because they tie elite night credits directly to card spending, letting cardholders work toward confirmed suite upgrades and hotel lounge access without staying 20 nights first. Premier Select earns 20 elite nights annually; Premier and Business each earn 15; even the no-fee card earns 5. At 40 elite nights total, members can choose annual lounge membership.

The suite upgrade and lounge access path reshapes the cards' value proposition. Premier Select reaches 40 nights with 20 annual credits plus $50,000 spending in one year. Premier and Business need 25 paid or points stays to match it. The maths favour dedicated IHG users willing to concentrate spending, not casual cardholders chasing everyday cashback.
