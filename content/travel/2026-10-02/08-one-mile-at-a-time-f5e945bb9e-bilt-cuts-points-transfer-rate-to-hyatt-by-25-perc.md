Bilt Rewards will devalue its points transfer to World of Hyatt starting January 1, 2027. The exchange rate falls from 1:1 to 4:3, meaning holders will receive only 750 Hyatt points per 1,000 Bilt points transferred.

For frequent Hyatt visitors, this cuts the redemption value of Bilt points by a quarter. Those planning to funnel Bilt earnings into Hyatt bookings should transfer sooner rather than later to lock in the current terms.
