United Airlines has installed Starlink satellite internet on more than 600 aircraft, with 1,000 planned by year end. The carrier is offering status matches to American and Delta frequent flyers to trial the service. American's own satellite rollout does not begin until 2027, and Delta chose Amazon's Leo system, which has not yet flown commercially.

For independent travellers who work on planes, reliable internet matters. Conventional airline Wi-Fi frequently cuts out, crawls, or requires repeated logins. Starlink delivers normal broadband speeds for several uninterrupted hours, a substantial improvement that justifies switching airlines for some passengers.

Airline technology leads rarely persist. United's current advantage in satellite internet will likely erode as competitors deploy their own systems and service quality normalises across the industry.
