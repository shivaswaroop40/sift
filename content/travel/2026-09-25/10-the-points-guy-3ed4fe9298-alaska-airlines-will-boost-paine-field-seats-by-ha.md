Alaska Airlines is increasing seat capacity from Paine Field (PAE) near Seattle by 50 percent through next spring. The plan adds a new PAE to San José route, extra daily flights to Los Angeles and Portland, and shifts some services to Boeing 737s from smaller Embraer E175s.

The PAE to San José service starts April 14, 2027, with two daily E175 flights, reviving a route Alaska last operated in 2019 and 2020. A second daily flight will be added to both Los Angeles (LAX) and Portland (PDX), with the LAX route switching to all 737s for at least a 63 percent per-flight seating increase.

The PAE to San Diego service moves to a 737 from an E175 on November 1. Timing has not been announced for upgaging the three daily PAE to San Francisco flights.

Alaska is the sole carrier at PAE after Frontier and United both left. The expansion gives the airline more capacity in the Puget Sound region as Seattle-Tacoma (SEA) faces growth constraints and Delta operates a competing hub without PAE service.
