A US bankruptcy judge has approved Spirit's sale of 27 Airbus narrowbodies for $668.1 million, the carrier's last major aircraft portfolio nearly five months after it stopped flying on 2 May. The jets comprise 10 A320ceos and 17 A321ceos built between 2015 and 2018 with IAE V2500 engines.

Save 2026-B LLC, a vehicle controlled by Spirit's aircraft lenders, is taking 23 planes for $567.4 million and offsetting part of what Spirit owes them against the price. FTAI Aircraft Leasing Bermuda, an FTAI Aviation affiliate, is paying $100.7 million cash for four A321s registered N661NK, N665NK, N670NK and N671NK.

Spirit advisers contacted 137 prospective buyers before the sale, with eight groups submitting bids. Only FTAI's offer beat the lenders on the four A321s, after which the auction was cancelled and the split deal presented to the court.
