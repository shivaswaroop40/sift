easyJet has announced 18 new routes from UK airports for summer 2027, with six of them originating in Scotland. Edinburgh gets four additions (Tivat, Hamburg, Bilbao, Rome Fiumicino), while Aberdeen gains Barcelona and Palma de Mallorca. Spain features heavily, including the airline's first flights to Girona from Gatwick, Liverpool and other bases.

The bulk of the new services start in late March 2027, running through to July. Most routes will operate twice weekly, with the Girona service from Gatwick increasing to three times weekly from late June. Only five of the 18 routes depart from London airports, underlining easyJet's regional strategy.

Independent travellers benefit from new non-London options including Newcastle to Larnaca, Manchester to Bastia, Birmingham to Milan Malpensa and Tivat, Bristol to Burgas and Montpellier, and Liverpool to Girona and Murcia. Southend gains Rhodes, and Luton gains Thessaloniki.
