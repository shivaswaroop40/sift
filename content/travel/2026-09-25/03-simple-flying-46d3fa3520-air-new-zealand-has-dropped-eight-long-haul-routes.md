Over the past ten years Air New Zealand has cancelled nonstop service to eight long-haul destinations from Auckland, including London Heathrow, Buenos Aires, Seoul Incheon, Beijing, Tokyo Haneda, Osaka Kansai, Ho Chi Minh City and Chicago O'Hare. The Heathrow route, operated via Los Angeles with fifth freedom rights, ended in 2020. Services to Buenos Aires and Osaka were cut during the COVID-19 pandemic and have not returned. Seoul was dropped in 2025 due to supply chain and maintenance issues with the Boeing 787-9 fleet.

Chicago O'Hare ended in 2024 for similar 787-related reasons, though the airline signals the route may return. Tokyo Haneda lasted only from 2017 to 2018 before the carrier consolidated Tokyo operations at Narita. Beijing, launched in 2008 around the Olympics, is now served only by Air China. The Buenos Aires and Seoul cuts leave LATAM and Korean Air respectively as the sole operators on those Auckland links, while Korean Air is now the only carrier between South Korea and Auckland.

The cancellations are tied mainly to pandemic restructuring and ongoing Boeing 787-9 maintenance constraints, not to commercial failure across the board. The Chicago route in particular is flagged as a likely future reinstatement once fleet availability improves.
