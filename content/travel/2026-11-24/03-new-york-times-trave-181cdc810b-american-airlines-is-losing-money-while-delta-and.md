American Airlines carries more passengers than any other U.S. airline. Even so, it is losing money, according to the New York Times. The paper points to debt, fuel costs and mistakes by the airline as the causes. Other big U.S. carriers are reporting profits in the billions.

The source text available here is only a short standfirst. It names Delta and United as the airlines pulling ahead but gives no figures, dates or details of the mistakes. The full article would be needed to judge how deep the problem runs.

For travellers who choose airlines by route network and loyalty scheme, an airline under financial pressure is worth watching. The text does not say what American plans to do about it, so any effect on routes, fares or AAdvantage is not yet clear.
