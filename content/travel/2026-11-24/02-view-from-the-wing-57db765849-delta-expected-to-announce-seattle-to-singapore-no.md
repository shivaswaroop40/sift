Delta is holding a Delta Night Market at Seattle's Museum of Pop Culture on 2 December. The Asian-themed invitation promises seven destinations. The source reads this as a likely reveal of nonstop Seattle to Singapore, roughly 17 hours. Delta's July presentation to a Pacific air traffic group already listed Manila and Singapore as new 2027 markets.

This is not confirmed. Delta currently serves Tokyo, Seoul, Shanghai, Taipei, Hong Kong and Manila, which is six cities. Singapore would make seven. Delta last flew to Singapore in September 2019, from Tokyo Narita. Since then it has sent Singapore traffic through Seoul on partner Korean Air. Los Angeles to Manila starts on 28 March 2027.

For travellers on the West Coast, a nonstop would remove the Seoul connection and compete directly with Singapore Airlines. Seattle is also a shorter trip to Singapore than Los Angeles, which helps on a very long route. Alaska has no Singapore flight, so Delta would hold that route alone from Seattle.

There is a commercial caveat. Delta's Pacific passenger revenue per seat mile rose 5% in the third quarter, against 10.9% at United and 11.3% at American. Delta added 8% more capacity while the share of seats filled fell two points. Including other revenue, the gain was about 10%.
