Air Saint-Pierre will run its seasonal transatlantic service between Paris Charles de Gaulle and Saint Pierre and Miquelon again in 2027. The route began in summer 2018. It ran once a week, using a Boeing 737-700 operated by ASL Airlines and marketed by Air Saint-Pierre.

Saint Pierre and Miquelon is a French territory off Canada, so this counts as a domestic flight across the Atlantic. That makes it an unusual way to reach a small, little-visited place with a clear story of its own. The source says there is no premium cabin on board.

The source text is truncated, so it does not give 2027 dates, fares or booking details here. Check the original and the airline before planning around it.
