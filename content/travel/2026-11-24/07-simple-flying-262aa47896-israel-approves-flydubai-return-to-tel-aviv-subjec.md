Israel's Transportation Ministry has approved the resumption of flydubai flights between the UAE and Israel. The airline had been barred until at least 15 December 2026 after a pilot tried to crash flight FZ1073 from Dubai to Tel Aviv. The approval follows a visit by Israeli officials to the UAE, where Emirati authorities presented new aviation security measures.

The approval is conditional. The ministry said it is subject to clearance from security authorities, and no restart date has been given. flydubai says it is working with the authorities and will announce updates on its official channels. The details of the new UAE security steps have not been made public.

Dubai to Tel Aviv was once a major route. The Times of Israel reports flydubai flew up to ten flights a day each way, carrying local traffic and connections to Asia. Etihad is currently the only airline linking the two countries. El Al, Arkia and Israir have not resumed UAE flights because of security issues, and Emirates has not flown to Tel Aviv since February.

Israel has also tightened screening of crews on flights to the country or through its airspace. For anyone planning Israel to Asia itineraries, options remain thin until flydubai returns and capacity rebuilds.
