Ryanair operated its last flights to Dalaman and Volos on 23 October 2026, and to Bodrum, Kalamata and Niš on 24 October. Simple Flying says these are permanent network cuts, not winter seasonal pauses. Ryanair has 14% of Europe's short-haul flights and 17% of its seats, according to OAG data.

Turkey is now off the map. Ryanair served Dalaman and Bodrum from Dublin and the UK, with only 10 weekly departures in August 2026. Turkey has no open skies agreement with the EU or UK, which capped growth. Turizm Ekonomi suggests promised marketing support did not fully arrive. Niš was Ryanair's only Serbian airport, and a fuel regulation problem is cited as the reason.

For independent travellers, this removes cheap direct options to Turkish coastal resorts from Dublin and Stansted, and to Niš from Malta, Corfu, Vienna and Stockholm. Kalamata loses Stansted, Milan Bergamo and Vienna links. Volos had only Brussels Charleroi left.

The article says some airports may return, as two did after earlier exits. Ryanair has said Belgrade is too expensive for now, though it says it keeps watching the market. The Turkey and Serbia explanations come from secondary sources, not Ryanair.
