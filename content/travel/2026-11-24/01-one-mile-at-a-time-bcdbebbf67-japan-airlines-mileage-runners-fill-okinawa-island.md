A Japan Airlines promotion has reportedly led mileage runners to take up to 12 flights a day on routes to remote Okinawan islands. In some cases, local residents have struggled to buy seats. The island's mayor has publicly appealed to the runners to stop.

The report comes from News On Japan, as summarised by One Mile at a Time. The source text is truncated, so details of the promotion itself, the islands involved and any response from the airline are not available here.
