Delta plans to fly between Seattle and Singapore with the Airbus A350. The route would be the airline's third longest. Delta executives confirmed some months ago that they intend to return to Singapore, which the airline has not served since 2019.

The source frames this as part of Delta's push across the Pacific, particularly to Asia. It says Delta has fallen behind United there in recent years and now hopes to catch up and even overtake it. The article notes it remains to be seen how costly that growth will be.
