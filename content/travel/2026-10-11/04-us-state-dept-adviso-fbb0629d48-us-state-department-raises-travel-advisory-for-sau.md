The US State Department has upgraded Saudi Arabia to level 3, advising Americans to reconsider travel. The advisory cites risks from Iranian drone and missile attacks targeting American interests, Houthi armed conflict, terrorism, exit bans, and local laws on social media activity.

Independent travellers planning trips to Saudi Arabia should factor this into risk assessment. The advisory affects flight routing decisions and may alter insurance or visa approval processes. US government employees now require special authorisation to use Riyadh's main airport.

The advisory does not constitute an outright ban, leaving the decision to individual travellers. However, level 3 status typically signals heightened dangers beyond normal travel disruption.
