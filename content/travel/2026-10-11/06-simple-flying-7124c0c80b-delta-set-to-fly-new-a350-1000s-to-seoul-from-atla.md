Delta Air Lines is expected to debut its new Airbus A350-1000 aircraft on nonstop flights to Seoul Incheon from its hubs at Atlanta and Detroit, though the airline has not confirmed the plan. The first of 20 ordered A350-1000s is due to arrive in early 2027, with an option for 20 more.

Seoul makes strategic sense for Delta. It is the airline's largest Asian hub and operates under a joint venture with Korean Air that moves approximately 2,000 daily passengers between the Americas and Asia-Pacific region. Both carriers use the same terminal at Incheon, simplifying connections and baggage handling.

The A350-1000 burns 20 to 25 percent less fuel than the older 767-300ER aircraft it replaces, and will be the largest aircraft type in Delta's fleet since the 747 retired in 2017. The cabin will feature Delta's next-generation premium suite with larger seatback screens.
