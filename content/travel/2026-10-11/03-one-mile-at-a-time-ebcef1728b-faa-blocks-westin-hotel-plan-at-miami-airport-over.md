Marriott's planned Westin hotel at Miami International Airport, which was meant to open in 2026 with direct terminal access, has been cancelled. The FAA blocked the project because it would create a radar blind spot that could compromise aircraft safety and operations.

The radar restriction affects travellers by preventing the airport from adding hotel capacity near the terminals. For frequent flyers and airport hotel users, this means fewer convenient options when catching early flights or between connections at one of America's busier hubs.
