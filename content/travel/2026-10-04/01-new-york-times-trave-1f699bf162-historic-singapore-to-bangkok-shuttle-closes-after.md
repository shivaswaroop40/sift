A writer spent a week travelling overland from Singapore to Bangkok using the final trains on a historic rail route before it closes. The journey covered 1,200 miles through Southeast Asia via the last operating shuttle service on this connection.

The closure matters because this route has long been a working alternative to flying for independent travellers crossing the region. It offers access to smaller towns and a different pace of travel than air routes provide.

The piece documents the final journeys on this shuttle, capturing the experience before the service ends. For regular users of Southeast Asian rail, it marks the loss of a established overland link between two major hubs.
