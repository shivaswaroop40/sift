Emirates has completed retrofits on 53 Boeing 777s, replacing the old 2-3-2 business class layout with a 1-2-1 configuration where every passenger gets direct aisle access. The new seats are fully flat beds measuring 79 inches, with 23-inch screens, personal minibars, and large tables. Each refurbished aircraft has 38 business class seats.

For frequent flyers on US routes, this matters because it eliminates the worst aspect of the old layout: middle-seat passengers trapped between two people, with the window seat passenger potentially blocked in. The new staggered arrangement also means better privacy and no compromised legroom for overnight services.

Emirates added premium economy to these 777s as well, a cabin that did not exist before. The 24 premium economy seats offer significantly more width and legroom than comparable products from Delta, United, American, British Airways, and Virgin Atlantic. Premium economy passengers receive priority boarding after business class but no lounge access or lie-flat beds.
