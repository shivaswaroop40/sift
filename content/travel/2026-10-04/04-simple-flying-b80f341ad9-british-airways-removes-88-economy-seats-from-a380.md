British Airways is retrofitting its 12 Airbus A380s this winter, reducing total capacity from 469 to 421 seats. Economy class will shrink from 303 to 215 seats, while premium economy grows from 55 to 84 and business from 97 to 110. First class drops from 14 to 12 suites.

For independent travellers, this signals BA's shift toward higher-yield premium seating on its long-haul flagship routes. The A380s serve major destinations including Los Angeles, Johannesburg, Miami, Dallas, Singapore and Melbourne. The retrofit runs November to February, with other widebodies substituting on some routes.

No retrofitted aircraft have entered service yet, despite two undergoing heavy maintenance earlier in 2026. BA announced the programme in 2024 with a mid-2026 target that has slipped. Business class gains aisle access and privacy doors, while premium economy gets larger screens and improved power.
