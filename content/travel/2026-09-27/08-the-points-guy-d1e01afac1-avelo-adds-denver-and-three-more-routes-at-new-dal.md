Avelo Airlines will add four new routes from McKinney National Airport (DTX) in northeast Dallas, including its first-ever Denver service. Flights to Atlanta and Denver start December 16, running four times weekly on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays. Service to Nashville and New Orleans begins December 17, twice weekly on Thursdays and Sundays. The Houston-based carrier is the only airline so far to announce DTX service.

The FAA issued DTX its commercial operating certificate on September 26, and Avelo's first flights from the airport launch November 11 to five Florida and Las Vegas destinations. Avelo will base three Boeing 737-800s at DTX by December. McKinney is roughly 35 miles from both Dallas Fort Worth and Dallas Love Field, the area's established hubs for American and Southwest.

DTX gives north Dallas travellers an alternative to driving south to DFW or Love Field. It also gives Avelo a foothold at a third commercial airport in the metro area, with no competition yet announced. Anyone within range now has a new low-cost option for several nonstop routes.
