Binh Lam Tower is a brick Cham sanctuary in Tuy Phước Đông, Vietnam. It is about 20 metres tall and dates from the late tenth to early eleventh century. Unlike most surviving Cham towers, it sits on flat, green lowland in a quiet village, not on a hilltop.

Atlas Obscura describes it as a link between two building styles. It falls between the delicate My Son A1 style and the taller, spear-like forms of classic Binh Dinh towers. The source text cuts off while describing the weathered facade, so further detail is missing.
