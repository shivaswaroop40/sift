Frontier Airlines has put its GoWild All You Can Fly Pass for 2027 on sale at $399, or $349 for Discount Den members. It is valid for travel from purchase until 31 December 2027, covering over 100 destinations in the US, Mexico, the Caribbean and Central America. Buyers must be Frontier Miles members.

Passholders pay $0.01 in airfare plus taxes, fees and extras such as bags and seat selection. International taxes can add $60 to $110 each way. There are no blackout dates, but peak dates carry a surcharge of $59 to $199 per segment. Elite status benefits, including free seats and bags, still apply.

The main limit is timing. Standard GoWild bookings open one day before departure on domestic flights and 10 days before on international ones. A return outside that window needs a separate booking later. Booking earlier costs extra, and an open seat is not always GoWild eligible.

The pass autorenews, and cancelling means contacting the airline. It suits flexible travellers who fly Frontier often and travel light. Anyone planning trips well ahead, or needing a fixed return, will find it awkward.
