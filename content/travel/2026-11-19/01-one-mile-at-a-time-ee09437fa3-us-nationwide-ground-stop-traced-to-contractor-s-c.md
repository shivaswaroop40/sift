Last Friday, air traffic across the United States shut down for over four hours. The source calls it the worst disruption to US air travel in more than 25 years. It says the cause was maintenance by L3Harris, a government contractor, which was moving telecommunications lines from copper to fiber.

The article asks how one contractor's work could halt the whole aviation system. Its headline points to an oversight gap and says an FAA technician only unlocked the door. The text supplied is cut off after the mention of the Transportation Secretary, so the detail is not available here.
