Lufthansa announced in January 2026 that it would retrofit its Airbus A380s with new business class seats. The first refitted aircraft entered service in April 2026. The airline had committed to finishing the whole fleet by the middle of 2027.

One Mile at a Time reports that the work is running well ahead of that plan. All the A380s should be retrofitted within the coming weeks, which points to completion in late 2026. The headline notes that the new seat is not Allegris, the airline's newer business class product.

The source text is truncated, so details of the seat itself and the exact schedule are not available here.
