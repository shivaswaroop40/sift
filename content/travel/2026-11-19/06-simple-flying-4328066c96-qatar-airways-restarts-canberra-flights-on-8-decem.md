Qatar Airways will resume flights to Canberra on 8 December. It last served the Australian capital in March 2020. The service will be daily on a 354-seat Boeing 777-300ER with 42 QSuites and 312 economy seats. It has no first class or premium economy. The flight runs Doha to Melbourne, then on to Canberra.

The route exists mainly because of how Australian traffic rights work. Qatar Airways can fly up to 28 times weekly to Brisbane, Melbourne, Perth and Sydney, and it has used that allowance. Continuing to a regional city carries no frequency limit, so Canberra lets it run two daily flights to Melbourne.

The second daily Melbourne flight will continue to Auckland, replacing the one-stop service via Adelaide. For January 2027, Qatar Airways lists daily 777-300ER flights to Brisbane, Perth and Sydney, five weekly A350-900 flights to Adelaide, and the Canberra service. Virgin Australia adds flights to Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane and Perth, and flies Sydney twice daily.

There is a caveat. Cabotage rights between Melbourne and Canberra do not exist, and the source cites data suggesting those legs have been nearly empty. The article also says multiple carriers continue to suspend Gulf routes because of the war.
