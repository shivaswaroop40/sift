Sixt demanded $1,589 from Christopher Nienhuis for splatter and scuffs on a Buick rented at Milwaukee airport. Nearly $500 was in fees Wisconsin does not allow. He disputed it, complained to consumer agencies, and says it went to collections. Sixt dropped the claim a week after a news reporter made contact.

Three other renters from the same location also had claims withdrawn. Todd Lofgren was billed $6,265, including $1,161 in prohibited fees. Brenda Bassett was billed nearly $600 for a dent already listed on her agreement, with about $137 in diminished value and appraisal fees. Autumn Wojciechowski was billed $872 for an underside scratch.

Wisconsin limits liability to reasonable repair costs or the car's pre-damage value, whichever is lower. Loss of use, administrative charges, appraisal fees and diminished value are barred. Sixt settled a similar damage billing lawsuit in 2025, in a package worth about $11 million.

The airport says Sixt's lease requires it to follow the law, but it is waiting for the state consumer protection agency to find a violation. It is not clear whether Sixt has stopped charging these fees in Wisconsin.
