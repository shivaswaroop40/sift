Lufthansa has announced Columbus, Ohio's first scheduled nonstop service to Europe, a Frankfurt route starting 5 May 2027. Columbus city council has since unanimously removed $300,000 of airline support from a proposed $1 million grant to One Columbus, the regional development body. It approved the other $700,000.

The route is set for Wednesdays, Fridays and Sundays on a Boeing 787-9 with 28 business, 28 premium economy and 231 economy seats. A revenue guarantee backs it. The city and Franklin County are each reported to offer up to $1 million over three years. JobsOhio and local businesses are also contributing, with amounts not confirmed.

For travellers, the flight is still scheduled, but the funding is not fully settled. Lufthansa would collect if revenue falls below an agreed target, subject to caps and a three-year limit. If backers do not fill the gap, the route's future could be less certain than the announcement suggested.

The council's move may not be final. The member who proposed the amendment reportedly supports the funding and may bring it back for another vote. The Columbus Partnership says it still expects the flight to operate. The airport also offers Frankfurt service marketing support and fee waivers.
