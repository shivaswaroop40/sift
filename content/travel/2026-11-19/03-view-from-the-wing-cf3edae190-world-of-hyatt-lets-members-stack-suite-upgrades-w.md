From 1 January 2027, World of Hyatt members can combine awards on one booking, including a free night certificate with a Suite Upgrade Award. Guest of Honor and a new Free Parking Award can be stacked too. Milestone Rewards will start at 10 nights instead of 20, and milestone free night certificates will last a year rather than 180 days.

Stacking works with Pay My Way. Hyatt's example is a three-night stay with a suite upgrade, parking and Guest of Honor across all nights. Payment could be points one night, a promotional free night another, and cash on the third. Suite availability and award inventory must still exist.

There are cuts. Suite upgrades will expire sooner. The 50-night choice drops from two Suite Upgrade Awards to one. At 60 nights, My Hyatt Concierge becomes one of two picks from a menu, not an automatic benefit. Free parking will no longer come automatically on award stays for Globalists.

Qualifying night credits offered as rewards can total 10 nights across the first four milestones. That makes a route to Globalist at 50 nights from other sources, though the threshold stays at 60. The Hyatt credit card also adds qualifying nights.
