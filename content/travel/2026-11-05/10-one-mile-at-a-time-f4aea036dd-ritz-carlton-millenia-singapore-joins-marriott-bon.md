The Ritz-Carlton Millenia Singapore, the only Ritz-Carlton in the world that has stayed outside Marriott Bonvoy, will join the programme on 1 December 2026. The 608-room hotel will then let Bonvoy members earn and redeem points and use elite benefits there.

This matters for anyone planning a Singapore stay on points. The property has been a conspicuous gap in Bonvoy coverage, since every other Ritz-Carlton already takes part. From December 2026, points bookings and elite recognition become possible at one of the city's flagship luxury hotels.

The caveat is generosity. The author cannot imagine the hotel being particularly generous with the programme, though that is expectation rather than confirmed policy. The change also does not take effect until 1 December 2026, so points redemptions there remain impossible until then.
