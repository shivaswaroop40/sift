KLM plans to cut its workforce by 15% and simplify its fleet, and has warned that its future is not guaranteed. The Dutch carrier faces high jet fuel prices, growing limits on its Amsterdam Schiphol hub, and competition from regional low-cost carriers and Gulf airlines. It has run efficiency initiatives in recent years and is now escalating them.

For travellers who connect at Schiphol or fly KLM, a cut of this size points to a leaner operation ahead. A simpler fleet usually means fewer aircraft types, which can change the cabins and schedules on offer. Anyone with bookings on the carrier should watch how the plan takes shape.

The article text available here is cut off before the detail. It confirms the scale of the reduction and the direction of the fleet plan, but not the timeline, the aircraft involved, or which parts of the workforce bear the cut.
