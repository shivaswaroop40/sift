The Living Museum occupies a building on the grounds of the Creedmoor Psychiatric Center in Queens, New York. It was established in 1983 and functions as a working creative space rather than a conventional museum. Psychiatric patients make paintings, sculptures, installations and mixed-media works there. Artwork fills the walls, rooms and exhibition spaces, and individual artists keep their own studios, developing work alongside one another.

For travellers who prefer places with a story, this is an unusual stop. The art is made on site by the people who attend the centre, not gathered from elsewhere. The space is described as feeling nothing like a conventional museum, and visitors encounter working studios alongside filled walls and exhibition rooms.
