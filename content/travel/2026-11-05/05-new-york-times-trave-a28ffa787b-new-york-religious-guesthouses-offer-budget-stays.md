Hotel rooms near Manhattan's main tourist attractions can cost hundreds, and sometimes thousands, of dollars a night. A New York Times guide points to an alternative. It lists five religious guesthouses in the city that rent rooms to budget travellers. The properties welcome guests of any faith, so no religious affiliation is required to book.

Accommodation is usually the largest single cost of a New York trip, so a cheaper base changes what a traveller can afford. A guesthouse run by a religious institution can cut that bill. The available text names neither the five properties nor their rates, so those specifics sit in the full article.
