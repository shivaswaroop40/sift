Emirates president Sir Tim Clark has named Denver, Detroit and Philadelphia as candidates for new nonstop flights to Dubai. He made the remarks at a conference in late October. The airline already serves 12 US airports. Any of the three cities would gain its first nonstop link to Dubai, extending the carrier's American network beyond its current footprint.

For travellers in those metros, a direct Dubai flight would remove a connection on journeys that currently require a stop elsewhere. It would also open one-stop Emirates itineraries from Denver, Detroit or Philadelphia to destinations across the carrier's network beyond Dubai.

The three cities remain candidates rather than confirmed routes. Clark was speaking about possibilities at a conference, and the report carries no launch dates, aircraft plans or service frequencies. Travellers should treat them as speculative until Emirates makes a formal announcement.
