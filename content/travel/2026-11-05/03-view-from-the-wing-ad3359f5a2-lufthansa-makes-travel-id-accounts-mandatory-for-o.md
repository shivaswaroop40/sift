Lufthansa Group is making its Travel ID account mandatory for online check-in. Departures from the United States are covered from November 16, and the rollout finishes on November 30. Tickets issued by other airlines are exempt, and airport check-in desks remain open to anyone without an account.

The change matters for anyone flying Lufthansa Group carriers and checking in online. Without a Travel ID, passengers must queue at a desk, which costs time at busy hubs. Infrequent travellers who do not want another account are hit hardest. Registering before a future departure avoids the desk queue.

The same roundup carries loyalty changes. Amex Platinum UK will limit Priority Pass to 20 visits a year from January 1, 2028, unless the cardmember spends at least £30,000 a year on the card. Two transfer bonuses run through November 30. Amex points go to Virgin Atlantic at a 30 percent bonus, and Citibank transfers to Turkish Airlines Miles & Smiles earn 40 percent more. The author would not take the Virgin bonus without a specific plan for the points.
