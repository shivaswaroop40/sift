Certares, a travel-focused private equity firm, has invested about $2.3 billion in European and UK rail operations. The founder Greg O'Hara said the firm plans to operate a challenger railway to SNCF, France's national rail operator, focusing on high-speed routes.

European governments are restricting short-haul flights to push travellers toward trains. France already bans domestic flights where a direct train journey takes less than 2.5 hours, with certain connecting flights exempted. Other European countries are expected to pass similar legislation.

For independent travellers planning trips across Europe, this signals growing rail capacity and competition on key routes. Lower fares and better service could make trains more competitive with flights on short distances, affecting how you route multi-country journeys.
