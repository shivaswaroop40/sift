A United member discovered they could move 45,000 miles from their sister without paying the usual $700 transfer fee. By creating a mileage pool, having the sister contribute miles, then having her leave and dissolving the pool, all miles ended up in the original member's account. Multiple flyers have since confirmed this method works.

For independent planners moving miles between accounts, this saves significant money. However, the process requires careful sequencing: the recipient creates the pool as leader, the donor joins and waits 72 hours, contributes miles and waits another 24 hours, then leaves. The leader can only dissolve once alone.

When a pool dissolves, remaining miles split equally among members still in it. If only the leader remains, they receive all miles. The entire process takes at least four days because of the waiting periods United requires.

United may eventually close this loophole. Participants must wait 90 days before joining or creating another pool after dissolution, which prevents rapid cycling.
