American Express will now create separate Membership Rewards accounts for newly opened U.S. cards instead of automatically combining points with your existing balance. You can still link them manually through the website, app, or phone call. The change takes effect immediately and applies only to new cards opened going forward.

This matters because closing a new card without first consolidating its points could forfeit that balance entirely, even if you keep another Amex card open. Previously, points were automatically pooled, so cancelling a card risked nothing as long as you held any Membership Rewards card. Now you must actively combine balances or lose them.

The separate balances mean you need to verify points are linked before cancelling any card. New York billing addresses get a 90-day redemption window under state law, and corporate cards may allow 30 days, but this doesn't apply everywhere. Confirm the link in your app before taking action.
