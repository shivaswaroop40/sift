From 2027, Southwest will tie the Companion Pass to its new Platinum elite status, which requires 140,000 Tier Qualifying Points or 100 qualifying flights. Previously, credit card welcome bonuses counted toward the Companion Pass even though they did not count toward elite status. Welcome bonuses on Southwest cards can reach 80,000 points, which previously went a long way toward the 135,000 points then needed.

This matters because the welcome bonus route has been the quickest way for independent travellers to earn a Companion Pass without buying many tickets. Once earned, the pass lets a designated companion fly for only taxes and fees. Losing this shortcut makes the perk harder to reach through credit card strategy alone.

From 2027, Southwest cards will earn 1 Tier Qualifying Point per dollar spent, and a 10,000-point boost kicks in at 130,000 points. A new Milestone Reward offers three Companion Pass trips at 95,000 points. Travellers who earn a Companion Pass under current rules in 2026 keep the benefit through the end of 2027.
