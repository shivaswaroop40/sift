Southwest Airlines is restructuring its Rapid Rewards loyalty scheme, set to take effect in 2027. The overhaul affects elite tier status, the airline's signature companion pass, and partner award redemptions. The details have been formally announced but not yet fully disclosed in the source.

The changes matter because Southwest's Rapid Rewards programme has long defined the airline's competitive position. The carrier has relied on simple earning rules and the companion pass to differentiate itself. Any restructuring may alter how frequent flyers accumulate status and claim rewards.

The scope of the changes suggests a shift toward industry norms. Southwest has historically stood apart through its rewards structure. This 2027 revision appears to align the programme more closely with competitors.
