Southwest is replacing its current elite tier system with three new levels (Silver, Gold, Platinum) starting in 2027. Credit card spending will now count towards elite qualification on all Southwest cards. The Companion Pass will move into the Platinum tier and will no longer be earnable via credit card sign-up bonuses.

This matters because qualification paths are shifting. Some tier thresholds are being reduced, but the Companion Pass—historically Southwest's most valuable benefit—will require more actual flying and spending to obtain. Sign-up bonus shortcuts are gone.

Tier qualifying points will drive Companion Pass eligibility instead of the current system. Southwest is also adding international partner awards and airport lounges to offset some changes, though earning rates on certain fare classes are being adjusted.
