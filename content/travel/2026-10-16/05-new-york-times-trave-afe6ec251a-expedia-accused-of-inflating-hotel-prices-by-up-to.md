A lawsuit alleges that Expedia breaches federal antitrust law by inflating hotel prices for millions of customers, with some overcharges reaching 30 percent. The claim centres on the booking platform's market dominance and pricing practices.

Independent travellers rely on Expedia to compare rates across properties. If the platform artificially inflates prices through anticompetitive conduct, users cannot find genuine market rates even when checking multiple sites, undermining the value of booking platforms.

The lawsuit is at an early stage and presents allegations rather than proven facts. The actual extent of price inflation, the mechanism Expedia allegedly uses, and whether courts will accept the antitrust theory remain unresolved.
