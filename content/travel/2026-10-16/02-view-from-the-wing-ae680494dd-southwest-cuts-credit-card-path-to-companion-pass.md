Southwest is overhauling Rapid Rewards from January 2027, eliminating credit card bonuses, referral points and partner activity from counting towards Companion Pass qualification. Only tier qualifying points will count. The required points rise from 135,000 to 140,000 for unlimited Companion Pass, or drop to 95,000 for three trips.

Independent travellers who have relied on credit card sign-up bonuses to reach Companion Pass quickly will lose that route. This was one of travel's best deals, especially when timed for early January to extend the pass nearly two years. The new structure makes Companion Pass substantially harder to obtain.

Customers approved for Southwest cards by 2 December 2026 get a transition exception: their card bonuses will count towards Companion Pass if they meet spending requirements in early 2027. Flight requirements also rise by 25 percent across existing tiers, while Southwest cuts points earning on premium fares by 20 to 28 percent.
