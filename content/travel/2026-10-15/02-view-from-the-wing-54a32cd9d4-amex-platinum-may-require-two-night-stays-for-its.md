American Express is testing a requirement that Fine Hotels & Resorts bookings must be at least two nights to qualify for Platinum's $300 semi-annual hotel credit, effective 1 January 2027. The language was found on Amex's test site. The Hotel Collection already requires two nights, but Fine Hotels & Resorts currently qualifies with one.

The change improves Amex's economics. A two-night stay at $800 generates roughly $160 in commission against the $300 credit. A one-night stay at $400 generates only $80 against the same credit. For cardmembers, the effective cost to use the credit rises from $100 to $500 at typical nightly rates.

This aligns with discussions Amex has held since at least 2022. Cardmember service costs at Amex rose 50% in the second quarter, driven by the Platinum refresh and benefit usage. Test site changes do not guarantee final approval and could be delayed or abandoned.

Amex is reportedly expanding the hotel credit to cover Airbnb Luxe stays and adding a $250 annual Airbnb Experiences credit, likely requiring a Luxe booking first. This addition lets Amex market the benefit as expanded whilst managing its cost.
