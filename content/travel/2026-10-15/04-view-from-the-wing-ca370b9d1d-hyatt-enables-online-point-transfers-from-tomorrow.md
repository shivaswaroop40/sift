Hyatt launches online point transfers between members starting 15 October, ending the need for printed forms and email processing. Members can send or receive up to 525,000 points per calendar year, a new restriction on a previously unlimited benefit.

For most travellers, the convenience of online transfers outweighs the new cap. However, the limit now matters more because Hyatt's premium categories cost significantly more since May, when top-tier nights jumped to 75,000 points each.

The cap likely aims to curb fraud and points broking. Hilton and Marriott introduced online transfers earlier without annual limits, instead using manual review for suspicious activity.
