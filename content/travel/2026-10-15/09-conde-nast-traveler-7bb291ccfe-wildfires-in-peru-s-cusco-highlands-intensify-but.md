Dozens of wildfires have burned tens of thousands of acres across Peru's Cusco region in recent weeks, killing at least five people. El Niño has brought higher temperatures and drier conditions to the Andean highlands, making fires harder to control than the seasonal burns farmers normally use to clear land.

Machu Picchu and other major tourist sites remain open and unaffected. The fires are confined to remote highland areas away from hotels and attractions. Air quality in parts of Cusco has a poor rating, though this mainly affects sensitive groups.

Local authorities recommend sticking to the traditional tourist circuit and avoiding higher mountain areas. Most tour operators continue running trips normally. Responsible travel supports local communities economically while conditions remain stable, though visitors should monitor updates from their operators.
