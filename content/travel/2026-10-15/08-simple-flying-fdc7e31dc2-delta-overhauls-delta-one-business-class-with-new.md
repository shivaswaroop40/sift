Delta has unveiled a redesigned business-class experience launching this fall on transoceanic flights from Los Angeles before expanding globally in early 2027. The overhaul includes new menus developed with three chefs, premium beverages including Blanton's bourbon and Dassai sake, redesigned tableware, and enhanced service protocols tested by over 200 staff across 36 trial flights.

For frequent international travellers, this matters because Delta is the first US carrier to undertake such an extensive business-class refresh in three decades, backed by more than $1 billion in fleet modernisation. New Airbus A350s will debut the Delta One suites alongside redesigned A330 interiors, expanding choice on key routes.

Passengers gain practical control: pre-selecting meals up to 18 hours ahead, choosing whether to skip full breakfast service for sleep, and accessing a new "sip and settle" service that minimises interruptions. The phased rollout through 2027 means crews will adapt gradually to new procedures.
