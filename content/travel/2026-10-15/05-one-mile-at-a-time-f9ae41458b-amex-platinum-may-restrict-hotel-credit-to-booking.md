American Express may impose a minimum two-night stay requirement on its Platinum Card's up to $600 annual hotel credit. The change has not been officially announced and details remain unclear.

The hotel credit is a core benefit that helps justify the card's annual fee for many independent travellers. A two-night minimum would exclude single-night stays, reducing the credit's usefulness for those who break longer trips into separate bookings or stay one night between connections.
