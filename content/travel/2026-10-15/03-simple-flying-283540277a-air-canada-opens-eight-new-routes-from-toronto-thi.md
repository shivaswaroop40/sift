Air Canada is launching eight new international routes from Toronto Pearson starting this October. Five routes begin this winter: twice-weekly service to Tenerife from October 25, twice-weekly to Merida from November 21, and single weekly flights to Guatemala City, Quito, and Roatan in December. Three more routes start in June 2027: four-weekly to Oslo, three-weekly to Shannon, and twice-weekly to Nice.

The expansion matters because it creates new connection options from Canada's largest hub. The airline is deploying its A321XLR, a newer long-range narrow-body jet suited to thinner routes that don't justify larger aircraft. Air Canada will become the only North American carrier to the Canary Islands when Tenerife service starts.

The winter season runs October 25 to March 27, with Air Canada operating over 36,000 flights from Toronto, a 10% capacity increase across its network. The airline also plans a daily Heathrow service from May 2027 using the A321XLR, adding to existing transatlantic capacity.
