Singapore Airlines has discounted selected KrisFlyer awards for November travel booked between October 15-31. New York to Frankfurt costs 39,900 miles each way in premium economy, and West Coast to Singapore costs 30,800 miles in economy. The promotion applies to limited seats on specified flights with blackout dates.

For independent travellers, these fares offer genuine value on long-haul routes. The Frankfurt premium economy fare at 39,900 miles each way is particularly competitive for a transatlantic booking. However, the restrictions are material: no changes or cancellations, no waitlisting, and ineligible flights can disqualify an entire itinerary.

The San Francisco premium economy option works only from Singapore to San Francisco, not the return. Travellers with existing bookings can rebook the promo award and cancel the original, though they will pay a US$75 redeposit fee on the cancelled Saver award.
