Lufthansa Group has secured a 44.9% non-controlling stake in TAP Air Portugal for €1.025 billion, beating Air France-KLM. The offer could rise to €1.2 billion for a 49.9% stake. Portugal's government said the German carrier's bid exceeded its rival's by 16 per cent.

For Lufthansa, this extends its strategy of consolidating European carriers after its ITA Airways investment. TAP operates an extensive network to Brazil, positioning Lufthansa in a strategically important market between Europe and the Americas.

Portugal retains the controlling stake for now, keeping the flag carrier nominally independent despite the German group's substantial minority holding.
