Alaska Airlines plans to install 12 Aurora Suites with fully lie-flat beds on at least 25 Boeing 737 MAX 10 aircraft starting in 2028. Each suite includes a 19-inch 4K screen, direct aisle access, and multiple charging options. The aircraft will also carry 12 Premium Reserve seats with 38 inches of pitch and enhanced recline.

For long transcontinental routes, lie-flat beds on a narrowbody aircraft change the economics of premium capacity. Alaska can sell a high-value business-class experience without deploying a widebody on every route, increasing schedule frequency while raising revenue from premium cabins toward 60 percent of total.

The airline is restructuring all widebody aircraft too. Boeing 787-9s will have 34 Aurora Suites, while Hawaiian's A330s will receive 22 Leihōkū Suites. Alaska expects long-haul capacity to nearly double from 8 percent to 15 percent by 2030, with Seattle growing from seven to at least 15 intercontinental destinations.
