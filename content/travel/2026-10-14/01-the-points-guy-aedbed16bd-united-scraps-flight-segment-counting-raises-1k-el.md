Starting in 2027, United will stop counting individual flight segments to award frequent flyer status. Members seeking Premier status must now earn points through spending on tickets and cobranded credit cards alone. The airline is phasing out the dual pathway that previously allowed frequent flyers to combine numerous short flights with fewer points.

This hits two groups differently. Frequent flyers who earn status through spending will see little change, except those targeting 1K status, which now requires 32,000 qualifying points instead of 28,000. Those who historically logged many short segments to reach status will need either more spending or a United credit card to bridge the gap.

United will still require at least four United flights annually for any member to qualify for elite status, regardless of points accumulated. Members with existing elite status and a United card will receive a 5 per cent head start on next year's requirement.
