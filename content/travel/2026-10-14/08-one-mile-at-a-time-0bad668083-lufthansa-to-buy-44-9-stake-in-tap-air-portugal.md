Lufthansa Group has won Portugal's privatisation process for TAP Air Portugal, acquiring a 44.9% stake in the national carrier. The German airline group beat competing bids, including one from Air France-KLM, after Portugal launched the sale process in summer 2025.

This reshapes the European airline map and gives Lufthansa a foothold in southwestern Europe. TAP operates key transatlantic routes from Lisbon, valuable for Lufthansa's network and frequent flyer programme. The deal expands Lufthansa's reach beyond its German hub-and-spoke model.

The transaction reflects consolidation in European aviation as major groups seek scale and route access. TAP has been loss-making but holds strategic value for its Lisbon position on routes to Brazil and North America.
