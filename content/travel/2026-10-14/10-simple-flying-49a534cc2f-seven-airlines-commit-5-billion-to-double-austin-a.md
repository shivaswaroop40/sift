Southwest, Delta, United, American, Alaska and two cargo carriers signed ten-year leases to fund a $5 billion expansion of Austin-Bergstrom International Airport from 34 to 66 gates. The airport currently handles 15 million passengers annually against a design capacity of 11 million, with projections to reach 22 million by year end.

For airlines, the deal secures permanent gate allocations at a rapidly growing hub. For travellers, boarding fees will rise from roughly $15 to $20 per passenger, likely flowing into ticket prices. The expansion addresses operational gridlock created by Central Texas's technology sector growth and events like SXSW and Formula 1.

A new midfield concourse with 26 gates, expanded terminals, 2 miles of taxiways, and upgraded baggage systems capable of handling 30 million passengers annually will open through the early 2030s. Budget estimates have risen from $4 billion to as much as $6.5 billion, with all cost increases passed to airlines and passengers.
