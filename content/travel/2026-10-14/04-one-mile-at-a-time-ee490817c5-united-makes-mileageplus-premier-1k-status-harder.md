United is changing how members earn elite status in its MileagePlus programme from 2027, reducing flexibility in the qualification path. Top-tier Premier 1K status will become materially harder to achieve, though the airline frames the changes as simplification.

Frequent flyers who rely on elite benefits like seat upgrades and lounge access will find it harder to maintain their tier. Those currently close to Premier 1K may need to reassess their travel or spending patterns to stay qualified.
