Phu Quoc is adding thousands of hotel rooms ahead of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in November 2027. Sun Group's Ruby Beach complex alone will deliver over 7,000 rooms in its first phase, with potential for nearly 20,000 across later phases. Marriott and other international brands are involved.

The island's peak-season occupancy already exceeds 90%, but CBRE analysis shows room-night demand must grow about 10% annually through 2030 just to maintain current occupancy levels. The real test comes after the summit ends, when the market must prove the surge in popularity is sustained rather than event-driven.

Recent deals include Marriott's agreement to develop ten hotels and resorts with nearly 4,500 rooms across Phu Quoc and Vung Tau opening through 2030. These signings reflect developer confidence, but oversupply is a genuine risk for those opening after APEC.
