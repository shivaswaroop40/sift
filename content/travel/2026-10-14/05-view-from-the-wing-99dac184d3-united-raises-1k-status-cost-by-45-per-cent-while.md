United is increasing its top-tier 1K status requirement to 32,000 qualifying dollars in 2027, up from 22,000. For flyers using the flight-count route, that represents a 45.5 per cent jump. The airline is also eliminating the option to qualify via a combination of flights and spending, forcing all members to meet spending thresholds.

This matters because upgrade availability—the main benefit of elite status—has already fallen sharply. United is replacing its fixed PlusPoints upgrade chart with dynamic pricing in February 2027, allowing the airline to change redemption costs without notice. Members also lost instant upgrades on expensive coach fares in August 2025.

The credit card requirement for the annual head start bonus is now mandatory, and card spending alone may no longer be enough to reach 1K without additional activity. United has also reduced mileage earning rates for customers without a qualifying card, making status pursuit costlier overall.
