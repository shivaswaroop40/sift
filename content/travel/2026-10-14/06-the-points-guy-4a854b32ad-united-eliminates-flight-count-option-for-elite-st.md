United Airlines is removing the ability to qualify for elite status by combining flights taken with spending, forcing all qualification through spending metrics alone starting next year. Travellers currently combining 45 flights plus reduced spending for Platinum status will no longer have that pathway.

For independent travellers flying frequent shorter routes from hub locations, this change makes elite status significantly harder to earn. Someone flying 43 times annually but from a central location may spend less per flight than a less frequent flyer on longer routes, yet now cannot offset lower spending with flight count.

Alaska Airlines is moving the opposite direction, offering three qualification paths for 2027: distance, flight count or spending. United's shift toward spending-only qualification also raises the bar for top-tier 1K status from 28,000 to 32,000 qualifying points, the highest requirement in the market.
