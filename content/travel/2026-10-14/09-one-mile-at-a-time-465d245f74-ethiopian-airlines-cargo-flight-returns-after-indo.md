An Ethiopian Airlines cargo flight to Israel turned back shortly after takeoff from Addis Ababa. An Indonesian pilot was aboard, breaching Israel's new rules that ban crews from certain nationalities.

Israel has introduced citizenship restrictions for airline crews operating there. The policy forced Ethiopian Airlines to offload the Indonesian pilot, delay the flight, and eventually divert to remove him before continuing to Israel.
