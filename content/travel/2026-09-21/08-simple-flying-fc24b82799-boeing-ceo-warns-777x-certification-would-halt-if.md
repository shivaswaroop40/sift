Boeing's 777X certification programme would shut down if 17,000 SPEEA-represented engineers and technical staff strike after their contracts expire on 6 October, CEO Kelly Ortberg has said. The same workforce is also tied to 737 MAX production, currently being ramped from 38 to 47 aircraft a month.

A strike would not only delay certification but also hit production and delivery, pushing out timelines for the already late twin-aisle. The 777X was originally meant to enter service in 2020 and is now targeted for 2027, with a separate engine mid-seal issue also complicating ETOPS approval.

SPEEA rejected Boeing's first pay offer, prompting 88% strike authorisation, but has now recommended a revised deal with a 10% wage rise from 16 October.
