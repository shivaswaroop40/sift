A one-time $250 million injection from Congress in 2022 has let Brand USA keep spending at near pre-pandemic levels, even after federal funding fell by as much as $80 million a year. The body plans to spend $158 million in fiscal 2026 and $165 million in fiscal 2027.

After drawing down $114.1 million of that windfall, Brand USA expects to end September 2027 with around $51 million in reserves, most of it earmarked for emergencies. Without renewed federal support, fiscal 2028 spending could drop sharply.

The funding lapse comes ahead of the 2028 LA Olympics and a target of 100 million international visitors by 2030. Reauthorisation via the Visit USA Act would restore a $100 million annual federal match, but the bill is not yet law.

For travellers, the immediate effect is on US destination marketing abroad rather than entry rules, though a leaner campaign could mean fewer international visitors competing for hotels and flights during major events.
