Chase will open a 16,000-square-foot Sapphire Lounge at Seattle-Tacoma's A concourse, investing $70 million in build-out and a midterm refurbishment. The space combines the current Priority Pass-affiliated The Club at SEA location with an adjoining cancelled expansion area. Rent starts at $500 per square foot, generating about $7.9 million a year for the airport under a 15-year lease up for approval on 22 September.

The deal triggers a chain of relocations. Once United finishes its new club on the B concourse, The Club at SEA moves into the vacated United Club. Chase then develops its lounge in The Club's old space plus the expansion room next to the Delta lounge complex. Exact handover dates are unclear, though Chase will be working with existing lounge space rather than a bare shell.

Sapphire Reserve, J.P. Morgan Reserve and Ritz-Carlton cardholders will get standard Sapphire access: free entry plus up to two guests, $27 per extra guest. Employee Reserve cards are excluded. With Seattle, the network reaches 12 lounges once Los Angeles, an expanded Phoenix and Miami all open.

Construction timing depends on United completing its new club and any refurbishment of the old United space. Chase has not published a timeline, and access rules for arriving passengers could differ from the Seattle location's current setup.
