Japan Airlines Mileage Bank is now a transfer partner of the Citi ThankYou programme, with points moving at a 1:1 ratio from eligible Citi cards. A 30% transfer bonus is running to mark the launch, so 1,000 Citi points convert into 1,300 JAL miles.

This matters because JAL awards reach some markets where partner pricing is scarce, and a 30% bonus effectively lowers the per-mile cost from Citi points. It also gives ThankYou a new long-haul option alongside existing oneworld and Star Alliance transfer routes.

The article notes that transfers are processed through Citi's website, though specific transfer times and which card tiers are eligible are not stated in the supplied text. Readers should check the partner page for minimum transfer amounts before moving points.
