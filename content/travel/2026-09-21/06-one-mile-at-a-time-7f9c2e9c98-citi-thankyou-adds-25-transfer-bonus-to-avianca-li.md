Citi ThankYou is offering a 25% bonus on transfers to Avianca Lifemiles from 20 September to 24 October 2026. The promotion is limited to US-based Citi ThankYou cardholders transferring points during the window.

Avianca Lifemiles is a useful option for booking Star Alliance award flights, including some United and ANA routes that can be hard to find elsewhere. A 25% bonus effectively reduces the miles needed for a redemption, though Lifemiles is known for unpredictable partner award pricing and fuel surcharges on some airlines.
