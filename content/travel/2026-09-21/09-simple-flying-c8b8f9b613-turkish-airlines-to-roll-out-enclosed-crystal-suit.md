Turkish Airlines is replacing its open 1-2-1 and 2-2-2 business class layouts with a new enclosed product called the Crystal Suite, developed in-house by subsidiary TCI Aircraft Interiors. Installations begin on newly delivered A350s in early 2027, followed by 777-300ER refurbishments.

The shift matches moves by Air France and Lufthansa toward private suites. New CEO Ahmet Olmuştur is also evaluating a premium economy cabin on widebodies from 2028, potentially reallocating 8-9% of current economy capacity. The carrier says two-thirds of long-haul passengers would pay extra for more comfort.

The plan is part of a wider growth target of more than 800 aircraft, over $50 billion in annual revenue and 345 international destinations by 2033. The airline currently serves more countries than any other carrier and is adding secondary-city routes such as Minneapolis-St. Paul and Philadelphia.
