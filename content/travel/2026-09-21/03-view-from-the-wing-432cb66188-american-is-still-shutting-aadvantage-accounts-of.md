Some AAdvantage members banned around 2018 over stacked Citibank card bonuses are seeing their new accounts closed by American Airlines, even after Barclays opened a fresh account tied to a 2025 card approval and they flew an award trip. American then confirmed the ban was permanent.

This matters because a second chance now looks conditional. Members who were approved for a new card, spent on it, and redeemed miles lost those miles retroactively. Any traveller holding an American-issued card benefits linked to AAdvantage, such as Admirals Club passes on a Barclays Strata Elite, is exposed to the same risk if a past ban resurfaces.

One previously banned member has kept a new account for about nine months by receiving only Strata Elite Admirals Club passes, with no miles ever deposited. The underlying dispute dates to 2018, when Citi mailed offers without the usual 48-month bonus restriction and American argued everyone should be treated as if it existed.

The account closures appear to be triggered by later review rather than real-time checks, so a card approval does not guarantee an account will survive. Travellers who think they have a flag in their history should verify their status before pursuing large redemptions.
