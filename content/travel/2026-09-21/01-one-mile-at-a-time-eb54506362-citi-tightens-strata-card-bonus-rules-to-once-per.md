Citi has updated the welcome offer eligibility terms for its Strata card lineup, replacing the previous 48-month rule with a once-per-lifetime restriction. The change affects the Citi Strata Elite, Citi Strata Premier, and other cards in the range. It is unclear how strictly the new rule will be enforced on existing cardholders who previously earned bonuses.

For travellers who collect ThankYou points, this matters because earning a Strata welcome bonus was previously a repeatable play every four years. Under the new terms, a single application locks you out of that bonus forever, which changes the calculus on whether to apply now or wait for a higher offer later.

The full extent of the change depends on enforcement, and Citi has not publicly clarified whether existing cardholders fall under the old or new rules.
