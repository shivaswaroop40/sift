Citibank has added Japan Airlines Mileage Bank as a transfer partner, joining Bilt, Capital One and Rove. Through 24 October, ThankYou Points on annual-fee cards convert at a 30% bonus, so 1,000 points become 1,300 JAL miles. No-annual-fee cards transfer at 1,000 to 910.

JAL's distance-based chart prices roundtrips as one award, so doubling the flights often costs far less than two one-ways. The article cites Miami–Madrid and Newark–Athens Emirates business class at 85,000 miles roundtrip and Miami–Buenos Aires on LATAM at the same level, with around $250 in taxes and fees.

Transatlantic Air France business class can be cheaper than booking through Flying Blue and avoids the higher carrier-imposed surcharges, according to the post. Space on partner awards is described as harder to find than in the past, especially for JAL metal.

JAL miles expire after 36 months and awards are restricted to the member and eligible family. Card-specific bonus offers for Citi Strata Elite and Strata Premier are also listed.
