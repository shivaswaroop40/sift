A technical issue at NATS' Prestwick centre in Scotland on 21 September 2026 has caused delays at airports in Scotland, Northern Ireland and northern England, with Manchester reporting hold-ups of up to an hour and Belfast City and City of Derry seeing cancellations.

NATS says the fault is unrelated to the wider outage on 8 September that grounded more than 2,000 flights and says airports south of Manchester are broadly unaffected, though flights heading north or departing Scottish airports face delays while engineers work on site.

easyJet has called the repeat failure into question and questioned the resilience of NATS' systems. Ryanair has noted an equipment failure but is expected to comment further. NATS has applied traffic regulations to manage demand and maintain safety.

Travellers on UK domestic routes to Scotland and Northern Ireland, or connecting through Manchester and East Midlands, should expect knock-on delays and check with their airline before travelling.
