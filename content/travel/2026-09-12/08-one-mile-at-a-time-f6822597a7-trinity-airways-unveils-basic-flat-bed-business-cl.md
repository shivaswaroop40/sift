South Korean low-cost carrier T’way Air has rebranded as Trinity Airways. The airline will soon take delivery of Airbus A330-900neos, and has unveiled the business class product for those planes. It is described as the world’s most basic flat bed, with no recline and no entertainment.

This matters because Trinity is expanding long haul as Korean Air and Asiana merge. The airline wants to compete with the larger group by offering a lower fare alternative. For travellers, that could mean a cheaper premium cabin on long-haul routes, but with fewer of the comforts normally associated with business class.
