The TSA is reportedly considering allowing PreCheck members to carry water bottles larger than 3.4 ounces through airport security in the coming months. The change would end a decades-long ban on liquids. Reports say the allowance would apply only to water bottles, not other liquids, and serious restrictions are expected.

For frequent flyers with PreCheck, this could remove a small but familiar annoyance: buying water after security or finishing a bottle before the checkpoint. The practical benefit depends on the restrictions, which have not been announced. If the policy is limited to water bottles, it will not cover coffee, soft drinks or toiletries.

The TSA has not confirmed the plan, and the report is thin on detail. No timeline, screening method or enforcement mechanism has been given. Until the agency publishes rules, travellers should assume the current 3.4-ounce limit still applies.
