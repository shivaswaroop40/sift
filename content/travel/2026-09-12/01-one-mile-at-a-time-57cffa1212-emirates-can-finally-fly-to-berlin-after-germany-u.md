Germany and the UAE have agreed to expand their air transport agreement, giving Emirates the right to fly to Berlin for the first time. The airline has sought the route for more than two decades. The announcement came during UAE President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan's state visit to Germany on Thursday, September 10, 2026.

For travellers, this means Emirates can finally operate between Dubai and Berlin, a route it has pursued for over 20 years. The route has been a long-standing gap in Emirates' network, so this is a significant expansion for the airline and a new option for anyone flying between Germany's capital and the UAE.

Lufthansa is not happy about the move and has accused the German government of various things, according to the report. No specifics of the accusations are given. The airline's opposition highlights how contentious the expanded air agreement is for Germany's flag carrier.
