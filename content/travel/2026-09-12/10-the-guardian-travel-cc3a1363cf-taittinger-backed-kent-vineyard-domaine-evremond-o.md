French champagne house Taittinger owns 51% of Domaine Evremond, a Kent vineyard near Chilham that has opened a visitor centre and released its first wine, Edition I. Visitors can sample the sparkling wine for £12.50 a glass or £55 a bottle, Wednesday to Sunday, with tours and tastings year-round.

It matters because this is a rare chance to taste a French-backed English fizz at the cellar door, in an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. The wine has serious credentials: Jancis Robinson scored it 16/20, and King Charles III served it to President Macron at a state banquet.

The vineyard's 25-metre-deep cellars, dug by Channel tunnel contractors, hold 1.5 million bottles. Writer Henry Jeffreys says English wine has gone from a joke to world-class in 30 years, and south-east England's climate now resembles Champagne in the 1970s, with harvests up to three weeks early.
