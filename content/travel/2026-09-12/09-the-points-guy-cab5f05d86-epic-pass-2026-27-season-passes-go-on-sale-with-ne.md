Vail Resorts has put Epic Passes for the 2026-27 ski season on sale, with a new 20% discount for teens and young adults on many pass options. The Epic Local Pass costs $675 for those aged 13 to 30, compared with $849 for adults. The full Epic Pass costs $909 for under-31s, against $1,145 for adults.

Buying now can still beat paying per day. A one-day adult lift ticket at Beaver Creek Resort runs $230 to $356 this season, while a one-day Epic Pass bought now costs at most $144, or $171 on a peak date. Passes are likely to rise once more before sales end in November. Adult season passes work out from $48 per day; children aged 5 to 12 pay from $25, and under-4s ski free.

One caveat is that the 10 Epic Friend Tickets offered to early buyers are no longer included with new purchases. Those tickets gave 50% off window prices for friends and 25% off child tickets at Vail's 37 North American resorts. The Epic Local Pass also has restrictions, including 10-day limits at Vail, Beaver Creek and Whistler Blackcomb, plus some peak-date blackouts.
