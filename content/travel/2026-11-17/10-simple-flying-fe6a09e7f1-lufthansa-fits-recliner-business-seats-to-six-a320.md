Lufthansa will fit six Airbus A320-200s with a 'Premium Business Seat' in the first two rows of business class. The seats are four-abreast recliners with almost one metre of pitch, a leg rest, a footrest and a fold-out table. The jets also get Starlink WiFi. Lufthansa says it wants to test demand before deciding whether to expand.

The seats can be booked from March 1 on Frankfurt to Athens, Cairo and Nice. Cirium data shows Athens has three or four flights a day, Nice two to four and Cairo two. Retrofitted jets may appear on other routes from December 16, so some passengers could get the seat without having booked it.

The change matters because European business class on short-haul flights is usually an economy seat with the middle one blocked. This is a step towards a product closer to North American domestic first class. The six jets are 13.6% of Lufthansa's 44 A320-200s, so availability will be limited.

Lufthansa has not given pricing or said how the seat will be sold, only that it is testing different booking options. Eurowings already sells a similar Premium BIZ product on Dubai flights and from December on some routes to London, Malaga and Palma.
