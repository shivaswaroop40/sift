Hoang De Citadel stands in the countryside of An Nhon, Vietnam. It was built in the eleventh century as Vijaya, the stone-walled capital of the Champa Kingdom. In 1776 Emperor Nguyen Nhac re-established the site as the imperial stronghold of the Tay Son movement.

The site now shows overgrown earthen ramparts. According to Atlas Obscura, layers of earlier construction lie beneath them, though the text supplied cuts off before saying what those layers are.
