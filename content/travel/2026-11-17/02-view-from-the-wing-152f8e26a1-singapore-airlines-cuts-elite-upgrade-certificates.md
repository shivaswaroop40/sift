From 1 January 2027, Singapore Airlines will cut the upgrade certificates its top PPS Club members earn from three to one per 100,000 PPS Value. Regular KrisFlyer members lose both milestone upgrades, the 75,000 short-haul and 100,000 economy to premium economy awards. Most are replaced with mileage discounts, such as 10,000 miles off at 75,000 Elite miles.

Solitaire PPS members lose the upgrades at 60,000 and 75,000 PPS Value. They gain two 50,000-mile discounts at 70,000. The maximum number of upgrade certificates falls from fifteen to five. Someone who reaches 100,000 gets three 50,000-mile discounts plus one upgrade, or five discounts if they convert it. Those who use upgrades to move paid business fares into first class or Suites lose the most.

There is one improvement. From 1 January, an unused PPS advance upgrade can be converted into two 50,000-mile redemption discounts, with a fresh twelve months. Conversion happens automatically at expiry. This does not cover the KrisFlyer milestone upgrades, which must be used before they expire.

Smaller rewards up to 15,000 Elite miles now last six months, not a year. The Scoot discount moves from 2,500 to 7,500 Elite miles and applies to the base fare only. The offline service charge doubles to US$50 or 5,000 miles from 1 December 2026. PPS Club members are exempt. KrisShopper closes in January, and details of its replacement are not yet disclosed.
