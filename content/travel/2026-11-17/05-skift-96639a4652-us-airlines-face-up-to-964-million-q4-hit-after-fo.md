A ground stop on Friday 13 November lasted more than four hours and halted US flights. From Friday to Sunday, airlines cancelled close to 18,000 flights. By Monday afternoon, FlightAware showed about 300 cancellations and more than 3,000 delays, so operations were recovering.

Raymond James estimates the four largest US carriers will take a combined fourth-quarter hit of $370 million to $964 million. American faces $136 million to $354 million, Delta $94 million to $244 million, United $88 million to $229 million and Southwest $53 million to $137 million.

Airlines offered travel waivers over the weekend to affected customers. The source says Delta, United and Southwest saw the fewest disruptions, but the text is cut off there. The headline says holiday demand is holding steady, though the visible text gives no figures on that.
