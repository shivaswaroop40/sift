Lufthansa has announced plans to put "real" business class seats on some of its narrow-body aircraft. In Europe, business class on short-haul flights is normally an economy seat with the middle seat left empty. This would be a step up from that.

The concept was first trialled on Eurowings, the Lufthansa Group low-cost subsidiary. The source says it was understood at the time that the trial was a test for wider use across the group. The text provided is cut short, so it gives no routes, dates or seat details.
