Capital One has launched a points transfer promotion with Air France-KLM Flying Blue. Members who move rewards to the airline programme during November 2026 will receive a 20% bonus. The offer opens at 12am ET on 1 November 2026 and closes at 11:59pm ET on 30 November 2026.

A transfer bonus stretches an existing points balance. A 20% bonus means more airline miles for the same number of Capital One rewards, which matters to anyone building toward a Flying Blue award. The window is fixed, so points must be moved within November to qualify. The author describes the deal as potentially excellent.
