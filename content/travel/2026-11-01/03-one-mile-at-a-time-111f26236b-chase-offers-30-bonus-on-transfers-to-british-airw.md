Chase Ultimate Rewards has launched a 30% transfer bonus covering three airline programmes. Points moved to British Airways Club, Iberia Club, or Aer Lingus AerClub earn an extra 30% as Avios. The bonus applies to transfers made in a limited window that begins at 12AM ET.

The bonus is a straight lift in value. Each 1,000 Chase points transferred lands as 1,300 Avios rather than the usual 1,000. One Mile at a Time calls this a popular bonus when available and expects some members to find it worthwhile. Anyone with a use for Avios gets more currency per point during the window.

The excerpt is thin beyond the headline terms. It does not show the offer's end date, any registration step, or a cap on how many points earn the bonus. Anyone planning a transfer should read the original article first and confirm the deadline before points leave a Chase account.
