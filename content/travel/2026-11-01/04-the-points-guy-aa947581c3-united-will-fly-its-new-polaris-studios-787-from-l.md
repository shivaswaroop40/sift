United plans to fly its newest Boeing 787-9, the 'Elevated' Dreamliner with next-generation Polaris Suites and eight Polaris Studios, between Las Vegas and Tokyo Narita on 9, 10 and 11 January 2027. The Points Guy has learned of the deployment. The dates sit at the tail end of CES, which runs from 6 to 9 January. Inbound legs from Tokyo on 3, 4 and 5 January will use an older 777-200ER.

United normally reserves these aircraft for its major hubs, flying them from San Francisco and Houston to destinations such as London, Singapore and Seoul. Las Vegas is not one of those hubs, and United does not typically fly the route to Tokyo at all. The CES deployment gives travellers a rare chance to book the newest cabin without routing through a hub.

The aircraft carries 99 premium seats, including 56 standard Polaris suites, 35 Premium Plus recliners and the eight Studios, against 123 main cabin seats. Airlines often add temporary routes around major events, as American did with its A321T for the 2024 Super Bowl. Delta is also adding CES flights, with one-off services from Las Vegas to Hong Kong and Taipei plus extra daily flights to its hubs and Austin.
