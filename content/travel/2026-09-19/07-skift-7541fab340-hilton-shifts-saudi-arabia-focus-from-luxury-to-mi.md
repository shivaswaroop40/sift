Hilton’s EMEA president, Simon Vincent, told Skift the luxury build-out in Saudi Arabia is largely complete, with brands such as Conrad, Waldorf Astoria, Curio and DoubleTree already well represented. The next phase targets mid-market brands and franchise deals.

For independent travellers, this means more Hilton Garden Inn and Hampton properties are likely in Saudi cities beyond the main hubs, following the pattern Vincent set out. He pointed to Turkey, where Hilton grew from its 1955 Istanbul Bosphorus hotel to over 100 properties, mostly Hilton Garden Inn and Hampton in provincial and secondary cities.

Vincent said these mid-market hotels are often the best in their town, even if the brand sits below Hilton’s luxury flags. The franchise-led approach helps Hilton expand without owning the real estate, a model the group is now exporting to Saudi Arabia and the wider Middle East.
