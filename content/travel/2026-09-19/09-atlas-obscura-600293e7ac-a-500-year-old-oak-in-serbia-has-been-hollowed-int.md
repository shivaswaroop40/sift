The Holy Trinity chapel in Čibukovac, a village near Kraljevo, is built inside a pedunculate oak thought to be over 500 years old and possibly the oldest in Serbia. The tree stands more than 30 metres tall, with a trunk circumference of about 8 metres and a diameter of roughly two and a half metres. Local Mihailo Erotijević, working with Father Radojko and others, hollowed out the trunk to create a small functioning chapel.

The dimensions are what make the feat possible. A trunk more than two metres across gives enough space for a usable interior, which is why the tree could be converted into a chapel rather than just a shrine or niche. Details on its current liturgical use, visitors, or preservation status are not given in the source.
