United Airlines has pushed back the launch of ten domestic routes from Chicago O'Hare until late 2027, after the FAA extended its scheduling cap at the airport through October 2027. The affected routes connect O'Hare with Bloomington-Normal, Champaign-Urbana, Kalamazoo, Lansing, Marquette, La Crosse, Central Wisconsin, Tri-Cities, Erie and Rochester.

For independent travellers, this means new nonstop options to United's Chicago hub from these Midwest communities will not arrive next year, and connections through O'Hare to the airline's wider domestic and international network remain delayed.

The FAA imposed the limit in response to a 2026 summer schedule that had planned more than 3,080 daily operations, a 14.9 per cent increase over the previous year. The cap holds O'Hare to 2,708 daily arrivals and departures, distributed across half-hour periods.

While those regional routes wait, United has added twelve new domestic services from O'Hare during 2026, including leisure and western markets such as Santa Barbara, Key West, Idaho Falls, Cody, St George and Monterey. Under a fixed movement ceiling, the airline is choosing routes likely to produce stronger yields over thinner regional spokes.
