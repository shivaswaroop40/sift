American Express paid Delta $8.2 billion in 2025, working out at about $37.78 per $1,000 charged on Delta-branded cards. Delta cards made up roughly 13% of Amex's $1.6698 trillion in worldwide billed business, implying $217.1 billion in spending on those cards.

That figure is well below Delta's claim that card spending is approaching 1% of the US economy. Using 2025 US output, it was about 0.71%.
