IndiGo has increased fees for several optional services, including infant travel, domestic excess baggage, unaccompanied minors, travel certificates and its Fast Forward priority check-in and boarding product. Infant fares for children under two rose from INR 2,000 to INR 3,000, excess baggage from INR 700 to INR 800 per kilogram, and Fast Forward from INR 450 to INR 650 on domestic routes.

The carrier has kept its base fares unchanged while squeezing more revenue from add-ons, the classic low-cost airline playbook. With former IATA chief Willie Walsh now in a senior role, the fee adjustments point to a more deliberate push to grow ancillary income on India’s largest domestic network.

For travellers, headline ticket prices should not move, but the real cost of flying with an infant, extra luggage or a seat near the front is now higher. The text is truncated, so the full list of revised charges and any international route changes are not visible.
