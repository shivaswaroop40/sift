American Airlines is reportedly planning changes to how it deals with rolling delays, where a late flight cascades into repeated late departures for the same aircraft. The exact mechanics of the trial have not been publicly detailed.

Rolling delays are one of the worst experiences for frequent flyers because they turn a single problem into hours of repeated waiting, often on the same plane. Any move to break that cycle matters most to travellers on tight connections or short return trips.

Details on routes, aircraft types and start dates are thin so far. The source notes the trial is reportedly underway but flags that executives took until 2026 to act, suggesting the change may be limited in scope until results emerge.
