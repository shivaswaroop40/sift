Marriott has connected its central reservation system directly to Spotnana, a corporate booking platform. Business travellers can now book its more than 10,000 hotels at the same real-time rates, inventory and room content shown on Marriott's own site and app. The integration was announced on Thursday.

The move closes a gap in corporate travel. Marriott's main lure for business travellers is Bonvoy, its loyalty programme, and it has the largest footprint of any hotel loyalty programme. Employees like earning points on work trips to redeem for holidays. But until now Marriott could not show loyalty information inside the booking tools that corporations require staff to use.

Spotnana says travellers can link their Bonvoy accounts to their Spotnana profiles, so the corporate tool recognises them. The direct connection gives Marriott a way to market Bonvoy inside the booking portals that corporations force employees to use. It also shows the same real-time rates, inventory and room content as Marriott's own site and app.
