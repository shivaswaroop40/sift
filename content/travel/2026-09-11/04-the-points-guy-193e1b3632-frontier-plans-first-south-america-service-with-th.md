Frontier Airlines has announced its first-ever service to South America with nonstop flights from Orlando International Airport (MCO) to three Colombian cities. The routes are Medellin (MDE) from December 10, Bogota (BOG) from December 14, and Cartagena (CTG) from December 19. Bogota starts at six flights a week and becomes daily after March 6, 2027; Medellin will be daily and Cartagena weekly.

The move fills part of the gap left by Spirit Airlines, which collapsed earlier this year. Spirit had been the largest US ultra-low-cost carrier in Colombia and South America, and all three new Frontier routes were previously served by Spirit. For travellers, it means budget nonstop options return on routes that had lost their operator.

Frontier says it is taking a measured approach to picking up former Spirit routes. CEO James Dempsey said in July there were opportunities to take markets Spirit had exited over the coming year or two. The airline also plans to leave New York's JFK airport next month, ending its presence in the city.
