United Airlines will end all service to Tulum International Airport just after the holidays. Flights from Newark run for the last time on 2 January. Nonstop service from Houston ends after the 4 January flight. United had already dropped its Tulum routes from Chicago and Los Angeles. The airline cited seasonality, demand and other market factors.

For travellers, the exit removes two more nonstop options to a beach destination. Anyone planning a Tulum trip after early January will need to connect, fly American from Dallas or Miami, take Delta from Atlanta, or use Cancun and drive more than two hours. The airport was billed as a more convenient link to Tulum's resorts than Cancun.

United is not alone. JetBlue has pulled out of Tulum. Delta will serve the airport only from Atlanta this winter, having dropped Detroit and Minneapolis. American still flies from Dallas and Miami but once offered Charlotte too. Cirium data shows US air service to Tulum is down more than 50% versus two years ago.

United is also adding two transcontinental routes from San Francisco. Twice-weekly flights to Richmond begin on 28 March, running Thursdays and Sundays. Norfolk service starts the same day, operating Wednesdays and Saturdays. It is the carrier's second San Francisco expansion in as many weeks.
