Nearly 20 airlines from outside the Gulf are still not flying to Dubai, and several will not return until the end of March. Singapore Airlines said on Friday it will stay out until February 28. KLM decided on October 26 to skip Dubai for the whole winter season, and Lufthansa Group has pushed its return to November 25. Finnair, United, Air Canada and Wizz Air are also staying out until spring. Air France has extended its Riyadh suspension to December 12.

For anyone booking a Dubai trip this winter, the practical effect is fewer long-haul routes and less choice on timing. Since the affected airlines are all based outside the Gulf, travellers will lean more on Gulf carriers to reach the city. The gap also hits Dubai's hotels. Some operators had expected European airlines back with the late-October winter schedules, and some said that was already lifting December bookings.
