Demonstrations are expected to continue this weekend in Paris and other French cities. Some museums and tourist sites will be closed as a result. Visitors in affected areas should also expect a strong police presence.

Anyone travelling to France this weekend should check whether specific museums and sites are open before setting out. The report does not name which attractions will close or which cities beyond Paris are affected, so travellers will need to confirm with individual venues or local sources.
