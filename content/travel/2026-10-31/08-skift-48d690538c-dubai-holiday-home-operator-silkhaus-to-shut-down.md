Silkhaus, a Dubai-based short-term rental operator, will stop operating at the end of November 2026 after five years. The company announced the closure on LinkedIn. It blamed the U.S.-Iran war, which began in late February, for its sustained effect on travel across the GCC and on all segments of tourism and hospitality.

For travellers, Dubai looks set to have fewer independent holiday homes and fewer flight choices. Several airlines are staying out of the city into 2027. Forward bookings are well below last year, after the disruption of March and April. Skift expects the winter peak season to bring no rescue for smaller operators, and predicts more exits.

The report does not say what happens to bookings for stays after the closure. The visible portion of the article also cuts off before giving figures for the fall in international visitor arrivals. Guests with upcoming Silkhaus reservations should check with the company or their booking platform.
