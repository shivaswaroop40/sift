Mumbai airport operator MIAL will remove 265 of Terminal 2's 770 weekly international departure slots from 25 October to make room for domestic flights during Terminal 1 rebuilds. Airlines must declare which flights to cut by Tuesday 4 pm. The Civil Aviation Ministry is meeting both sides the same day.

The cuts disrupt airlines' approved winter schedules. MIAL is offering Navi Mumbai Airport, 45 kilometres away and owned by the same Adani Group parent, as an alternative. This is the first real test of Mumbai's two-airport system and raises questions about whether the safety justification aligns with commercial incentives.
