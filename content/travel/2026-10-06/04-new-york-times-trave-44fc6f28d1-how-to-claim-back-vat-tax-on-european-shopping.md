Value added tax is built into prices across Europe. As a non-EU visitor, you can reclaim it on goods you take home, though the process and thresholds vary by country.

VAT refunds matter to independent travellers because they can recover 15 to 25 percent of the purchase price on eligible items. This makes luxury goods and souvenirs notably cheaper.
