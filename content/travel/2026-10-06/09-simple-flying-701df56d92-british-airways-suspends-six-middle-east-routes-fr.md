British Airways has suspended six routes from London Heathrow to the Middle East: Abu Dhabi, Amman, Bahrain, Dubai, Riyadh, and Tel Aviv. Most are expected to resume between late October and early November as the airline switches to winter schedules, though some may not return until 2027.

The suspensions reflect the impact of the Iran conflict on aviation across the region. BA's Middle East capacity from Heathrow is now down 59 percent compared with a year ago, from 78 weekly departures to 32, affecting independent travellers planning regional connections.

When flights resume, frequencies and aircraft will be reduced. Amman drops to four weekly A320neo from daily service. Abu Dhabi will not return until winter 2027 or 2028. The A380 will no longer serve Dubai, though it may return later.
