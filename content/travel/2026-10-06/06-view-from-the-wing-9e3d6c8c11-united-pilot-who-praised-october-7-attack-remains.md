A United Airlines pilot suspended in 2023 after posting that Hamas's October 7 attack was "resistance by a brave people" has returned to flying passengers. United removed him temporarily but has not disclosed the outcome of its investigation or why he was cleared to resume duties.

The case matters because it shows how airlines assess pilot fitness when they have expressed support for violence. A flydubai copilot recently attacked his captain with an axe, intending to crash the aircraft into Tel Aviv airport, after joining specifically to access Israel routes.

United has remained silent on what prompted Mossallam's reinstatement nearly three years after the initial posts. Transparency about how the airline evaluated the concern would clarify its safety standards.
