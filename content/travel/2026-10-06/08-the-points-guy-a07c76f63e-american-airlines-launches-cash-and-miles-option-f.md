American Airlines is rolling out a cash and miles booking option for AAdvantage members on domestic flights, excluding Alaska and Hawaii. Members will use a slider to choose how many miles to redeem toward a cash fare, reducing the out-of-pocket cost. The feature will be available on aa.com and the American app in coming weeks.

This brings American in line with Delta, United and Southwest, which already offer similar tools. It could be useful for members with small mile balances that won't cover a full award ticket. The move lets members redeem miles without needing traditional award availability.

American has not disclosed the redemption value per mile, though it likely sits around 1 cent based on similar features. This trails TPG's valuation of 1.45 cents per AAdvantage mile. Only the cash portion earns miles and elite-qualifying points, so redeeming more miles could slow status progress.
