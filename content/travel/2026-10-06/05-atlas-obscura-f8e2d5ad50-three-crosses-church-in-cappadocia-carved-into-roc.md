Üç Haclı Kilise, carved into the red rock of Çavuşin valley in Turkey, is a monastic church with frescoes dating to the tenth century. The church structure itself may date back to the AD 600s. Despite erosion, paintings depicting Jesus as ruler of the world and his baptism remain visible.

The site appeals to travellers seeking places with historical depth rather than conventional attractions. Cappadocia's cave churches form a distinct travel route through Anatolia, combining geology, art history and religious heritage in one region.
