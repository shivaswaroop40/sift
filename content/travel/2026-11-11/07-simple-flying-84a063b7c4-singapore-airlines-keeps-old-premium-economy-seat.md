Singapore Airlines has confirmed that its new premium economy seat will debut only on the Boeing 777-9. The 41 Airbus A350s in its S$1.1 billion retrofit will get new first and business class, but keep the existing premium economy seat with a refresh. The airline confirmed the split on 6 November, after detailing its KrisWorld system on 22 October.

The new seat has a 16-inch 4K screen, Bluetooth audio, USB-C, 40-inch pitch, 9 inches of recline and a 19-inch width. Collins Aerospace supplies it and Factorydesign designed it. The A350 seat dates from August 2015, so it is over 11 years old. Singapore says only selected aircraft will get 4K OLED displays.

For travellers, the same cabin name will mean different products by aircraft. On long-haul routes, check the aircraft type before paying for premium economy. The 777-9 is still uncertified, with first delivery to Lufthansa targeted for 2027, so the new seat is some way off.

Singapore has not explained the choice. The article suggests the retrofit favours the cabins with the most revenue potential. It cites a past executive comment that business class sold easily on ultra-long-haul flights while premium economy needed attractive pricing. That reasoning is the author's analysis, not the airline's stated position.
