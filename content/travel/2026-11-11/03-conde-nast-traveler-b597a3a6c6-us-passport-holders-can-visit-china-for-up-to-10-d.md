China's 240-hour visa-free transit policy, introduced in December 2024, lets holders of passports from 57 countries, including the US, stay up to 10 days without a visa. The Condé Nast Traveler writer flew Paris to Shanghai, stayed five nights, then flew on to Manila, entering and leaving through Shanghai Pudong.

The rule depends on transit. You must arrive from one country and leave for a different one, so US to China and back to the US does not qualify. Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan count as separate destinations. You must also use designated entry and exit ports, and staying within permitted areas.

Airline and airport staff may not know the rule. The writer says he nearly lost his boarding at Paris-Charles de Gaulle because he had no printed proof of his onward ticket, hotels or the policy. Specialists advise carrying printed bookings, calling the airline beforehand and printing the official policy page.
