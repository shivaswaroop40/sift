Frontier Airlines has put first class tickets on sale. The product launches systemwide for flights from March 2027. The Denver-based carrier has long been an ultra low cost airline and is now trying to move upmarket in response to what customers want.

The headline of the original says the seat comes with no food, no drinks and no recline. The text provided is truncated, so it does not confirm what is included or what it costs.

First class is one of several changes. Frontier is also adding Starlink Wi-Fi and charging ports at select seats.
