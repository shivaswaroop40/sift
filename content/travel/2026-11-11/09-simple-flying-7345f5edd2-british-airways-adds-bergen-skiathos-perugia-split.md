British Airways has announced five new or returning routes for 2027. Heathrow to Bergen starts on 15 April, five times weekly, mainly on the A319. Heathrow to Skiathos starts on 20 May, three times weekly on the A320neo, and replaces a London City service. BA CityFlyer will fly London City to Perugia from 1 May, Edinburgh to Split and Glasgow to Nice from 15 May, all weekly on E190s.

Skiathos is the first time Heathrow has served the island, and it takes the airport to 12 Greek destinations. Perugia moves from Heathrow to London City, so Perugia is now served from the City airport for the first time. Bergen returns after BA flew it from 2012 to 2017. Norwegian will fly it from Gatwick, so there will be two London options.

The Scottish routes are weekend-only. Edinburgh to Split runs on Saturdays and Glasgow to Nice on Sundays outbound, using CityFlyer aircraft that would otherwise sit idle while London City is closed. That makes them useful for independent travellers in Scotland who want direct summer access to Croatia and the Riviera without changing in London.

The Bergen schedule varies by day, with departures between 7:45am and 8:40am, which the source puts down to Heathrow slot constraints. Other 2027 BA launches include Heathrow to Melbourne via Kuala Lumpur from 9 January, and Gatwick to Kilimanjaro and Zanzibar from 29 May.
