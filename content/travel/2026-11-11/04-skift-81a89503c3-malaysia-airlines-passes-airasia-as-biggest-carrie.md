Malaysia Airlines has scheduled 1.57 million seats between Malaysia and India in 2026. The AirAsia group, including AirAsia X, has 1.42 million, according to Cirium data. It is the first time since 2021 that AirAsia is not the largest operator on the route.

Malaysia Airlines barely grew, with seats up 1% on 2025. AirAsia's seats fell 21%, a loss of nearly 378,000. The whole market shrank 10%. Malaysia Airlines' share rose from 34% to 38%, while AirAsia's fell from 39% to 35%.

Rivals have not filled the gap. IndiGo cut seats by 18% and Batik Air by 9%. Air India is the only carrier described as growing strongly, though the source text cuts off before the detail. Skift's summary points to high fuel costs and scarce aircraft.
