OpenAI has acquired Odessia, an AI travel agent startup founded by Sonder co-founder Francis Davidson. He announced the deal on Tuesday and will join OpenAI to work on the future of travel. Terms were not disclosed. He did not say whether Odessia will keep running as a standalone company.

Odessia launched in June with eight staff in San Francisco and about $6 million in seed funding led by Sequoia Capital. It plans and books flights, hotels and itineraries in one conversation. It opened to the public on 14 October as a free concierge that users reach by text.

Odessia held IATA accreditation as a travel agent. That gave it access to private hotel rates and perks such as free breakfast and room upgrades. It also wrote guides to hundreds of thousands of hotels. The source text is cut off mid-sentence, so the rest of that detail is missing.

Skift's own view is that OpenAI bought the team and the founder's experience rather than revenue. It reads the deal as a sign that ChatGPT wants to do more in travel than send users to Booking.com and Expedia. That is Skift's interpretation, not something OpenAI has said.
