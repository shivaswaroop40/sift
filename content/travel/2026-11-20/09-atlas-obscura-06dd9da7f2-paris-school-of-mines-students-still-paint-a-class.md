The Promotions Gallery in Paris is tied to the School of Mines and its old "Mines staircase". The staircase was once a secret entrance that gave students access to the Paris Catacombs. That private access is no longer used, for safety reasons.

The General Inspector of Quarries still gives students permission to keep up an artistic tradition. Each year, around Sainte-Barbe on 4 December, first-year students paint a picture representing their class. Sainte-Barbe is the feast of the patron saint of miners.
