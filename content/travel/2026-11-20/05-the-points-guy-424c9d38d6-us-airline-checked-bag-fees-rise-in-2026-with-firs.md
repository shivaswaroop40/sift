The Points Guy reports that checked bag fees on major US airlines have gone up in 2026. A first checked bag typically starts at around $45 to $50 each way. Additional bags and overweight bags cost more. Most major airlines still allow one personal item and one carry-on.

Fees vary by airline, fare type, travel date and whether you pay online or at the airport. Paying online in advance can lower the cost. Low-cost carriers tend to charge the most and vary the most, and some enforce tighter limits, such as 40 pounds for a checked bag.

Basic economy fares can be more restrictive, and specialty or oversized items usually attract extra charges. The piece says elite status, premium cabin tickets or certain travel benefits can bring free bags or higher weight limits. The source text here is a short summary, so airline-by-airline figures are not given.
