Avianca LifeMiles is running a limited-time 145% bonus on purchased miles. With the bonus, members can buy miles for as little as 1.35 cents each. The source asks whether this is a good deal, but the text supplied is cut off before it gives an answer.

The author rates LifeMiles as one of the more useful programmes for redeeming miles. The reason given is the ability to book long-haul premium cabin awards on Star Alliance airlines. That makes the bonus relevant to anyone who wants to buy miles for a specific premium trip rather than collect them slowly.

There is a caveat. The author stresses that LifeMiles does not always see the same award space as other Star Alliance programmes. Check availability on your route before buying, since purchased miles are hard to turn back into cash. The excerpt does not cover the end date, the purchase cap or any comparison with other programmes.
