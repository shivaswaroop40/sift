Accor's ALL Signature subscription is run from Brazil and priced in reais, but anyone can join. Points are worth a fixed 2 euro cents each toward hotel stays. A weak real makes them cheaper in dollars. The Absolute plan costs about $1,254 for 75,000 points, or roughly €1,500 of credit. A 20% first-bill code, PREBLACK2026, runs until 20 November.

The Explorer plan is the better value per point. It costs about $693 for 45,000 points, worth €900. That is about 29.8% below redemption value, against 23.8% for Absolute. Stacking Explorer points with the 15% rate discount from the separate ALL Accor+ Explorer membership gives about 40.3% off the room price, before membership costs and lost earnings.

There are limits. Points arrive monthly, not all at once, so you cannot spend a year's balance on day one. Paying with points forfeits the roughly 8.8% earn a Platinum member would get. Stay Plus certificates and Suite Night Upgrades exclude point redemptions. Subscription status nights are capped at 30 a year.

Points can go to airlines, but it is poor value. Iberia and Flying Blue transfer 1:1, while British Airways gives half as many Avios. At Explorer prices a point costs about 1.54 cents, but moving it gives up about 2.19 cents of hotel credit. Last year's Black Friday offered 30% off annual plans, and a repeat is possible but unconfirmed.
