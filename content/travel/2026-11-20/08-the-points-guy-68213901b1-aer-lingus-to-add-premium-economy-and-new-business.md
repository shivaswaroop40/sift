Aer Lingus will retrofit 10 of its A330s with its first premium economy cabin, new lie-flat business suites and updated economy seats. Premium economy will have 28 seats in four rows, laid out 2-3-2, with 38 inches of pitch. Fares start at €700 each way, about $768. The first refitted flights are EI104 and EI105 between Dublin and New York JFK from 1 October.

Business class moves to 28 Collins Elevation suites in a 1-2-1 layout, so every passenger gets aisle access. That is two seats fewer than today's A330-300. The suites have closing doors, a 78-inch flat bed and wireless and USB-C charging. Economy stays 2-4-2, with 4K screens, Bluetooth audio and USB-C. Free Starlink Wi-Fi continues.

For transatlantic flyers, this adds a mid-priced option on a carrier that had none, and ends the staggered 1-2-2 business layout. The €700 starting fare gives a reference point when comparing with business class and other airlines' premium economy. The retrofit will come to the North America routes in stages, so the cabin you get will depend on the aircraft.

The airline has 12 A330s in total, per Planespotters.net, and it is not clear what happens to the two not being refitted. The dining plans for business class were not detailed. The source article is also upbeat in tone, so treat its verdict on the new product as expectation rather than experience.
