Riyadh hotels had one of their worst months of the year in October after the city's main airport closed. Occupancy fell 34.7 percentage points to 34.3% from 69%, according to CoStar's STR. Average daily rate dropped 29.9% to $190. Revenue per available room fell 65.1% to $65. Only March had lower occupancy, at 28.3%.

The closure followed an attack on King Khalid International Airport on Oct. 10. Saudi authorities said it killed 13 people and injured more than 300. Cirium data shows about 86% of scheduled Riyadh departures were cancelled between Oct. 8 and late October.

October and November are normally Riyadh's peak season, so the hit is larger than a similar fall in a quiet month, an STR analyst said. The source's summary line says British Airways, Cathay Pacific and others have suspended flights into the new year, and that Houthi strikes are still hitting the capital.
