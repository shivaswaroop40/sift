Čigoč is a village in Lonjsko Polje Nature Park, beside the Tišina oxbow of the Sava River. White storks build large nests on its traditional wooden houses. In 1994 the conservation foundation EuroNatur named it the first European Stork Village, which started a wider network of stork villages across Europe.

The village suits a visit built around wildlife and rural life rather than resort comforts. Wet meadows, pastures and pools surround the houses and give the birds food close to the nests. During the breeding season you can walk through and watch adults tending nests and flying out to feed.
