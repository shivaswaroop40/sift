Delta has announced its summer 2027 transatlantic schedule from the United States but has not added any new European cities, in contrast to American Airlines and United Airlines, which both revealed new routes in recent weeks. American added three new destinations, while United announced 10, its largest expansion in history.

For travellers, the absence of new Delta gateways means limited fresh options on SkyTeam for next summer, and frequencies or timings on existing routes may shift. Anyone holding Delta SkyMiles or partner awards may want to compare across the three carriers before booking, since competitors are adding the new city pairs.

The article frames the announcement as underwhelming given the typical fall window for major US carrier route reveals. It does not detail specific capacity changes, aircraft swaps or frequency increases Delta has confirmed for 2027.
