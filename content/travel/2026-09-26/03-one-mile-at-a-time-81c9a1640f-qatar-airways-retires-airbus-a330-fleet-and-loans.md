Qatar Airways has retired its Airbus A330 fleet and is sending some of its pilots to RwandAir, as the Iran conflict continues to hit Gulf airline demand. The airline has not publicly announced the move, but the fleet type has effectively been pulled from service.

The retirement matters because it shows how sharply the war in Iran has cut traffic through Doha, a hub that depends on connecting flows between regions rather than local passengers. Rerouting A330 pilots to a partner carrier in Kigali is a way to keep staff employed while reducing capacity.

For travellers, Qatar's smaller widebody lineup means fewer seat options on some long-haul routes until demand recovers, while RwandAir gains experienced crews as it grows its own network.
