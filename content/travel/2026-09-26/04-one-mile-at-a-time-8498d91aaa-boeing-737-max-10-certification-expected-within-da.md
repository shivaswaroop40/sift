The 737 MAX 10 is reportedly days away from FAA certification, according to industry signals covered by One Mile at a Time. The aircraft is the last of Boeing's MAX family still awaiting approval, with the MAX 7 certified earlier in 2024 and the 777X still pending.

The MAX 10 matters because it is the largest variant in the MAX range and offers more seats and range than the MAX 8. United Airlines is the largest customer with about 100 on order, while Alaska Airlines is also expecting deliveries. Certification unlocks years of delayed capacity for carriers that have had to extend leases and rejig fleet plans.

Certification is not the end of the road. The MAX 10 still needs an engine fix tied to the 2020 grounding before it can enter service, and pilots require additional training. Actual passenger flights are still expected to slip into 2025 rather than the rest of this year.
