Lufthansa will start the first nonstop flight between Columbus and Europe on 5 May 2027. The route links John Glenn Columbus International with Frankfurt three times weekly, on Wednesdays, Fridays and Sundays. LH407 departs Columbus at 4:20 PM and arrives at 6:15 AM the next day. Tickets went on sale on 20 October.

The service gives central Ohio its first direct link to Europe. Travellers have until now connected through another US gateway or started at a different airport. Frankfurt also opens Lufthansa's network across continental Europe, the Middle East, Africa and Asia. Three weekly flights are a cautious start, letting the airline test seasonal demand before any expansion.

The aircraft is a 287-seat Boeing 787-9 with the Allegris cabin, laid out as 28 business seats, 28 premium economy and 231 economy. Lufthansa is betting on German corporate demand. The Columbus area hosts 38 German-based companies, and Bayer has committed billions to nearby New Albany. State-backed revenue guarantees support the route, but its long-term prospects depend on mixing corporate, leisure, connecting and premium traffic.
