A video from Cancun airport shows a vacation rental owner confronting staff in Visitax vests, demanding they explain the extra charge imposed on visitors. Quintana Roo's tourist tax is officially 293 pesos, about $15.70 per person. Travellers at airport collection points report being asked for as much as 550 pesos, nearly double. The state said in December 2025 the tax would stay at 285 pesos with no increase in 2026.

The tax is not included in airline tickets, so it lands on travellers on top of the federal visitor charge, hotel taxes and city environmental fees. The official payment site recognises authorised private collectors alongside the government portal, leaving travellers to guess whether the person demanding money is legitimate and how much reaches the state rather than the processing company.

The governor says airport collection will continue, arguing there is no other way after hotel owners refused to collect the tax. Travellers can pay in advance at visitax.gob.mx, which issues a QR-code receipt. Replies to the video call the charge a "narcotax" and vow "never again to Cancun".
