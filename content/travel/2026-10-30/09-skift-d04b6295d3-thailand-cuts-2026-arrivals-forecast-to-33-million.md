Thailand has cut its 2026 forecast for international arrivals to 33 million, down from 36.7 million. The 2027 target is "at least 33 million", which carries this year's reduced ambition into next year. Hotel developers and airlines are still adding rooms and flights despite the weaker outlook.

That mismatch raises questions about the demand assumptions behind the new rooms and seats. For travellers, more capacity chasing weaker demand could mean softer rates and fares. The exposure is uneven. Bangkok's luxury hotels are outperforming, while Phuket faces a wave of openings.
