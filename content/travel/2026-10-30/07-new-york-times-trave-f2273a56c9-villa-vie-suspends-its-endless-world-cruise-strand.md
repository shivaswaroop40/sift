Villa Vie has suspended its 'endless' cruise around the world, leaving passengers stranded. The voyage was sold as a lifetime of sailing for less than the cost of living on land. The company was underfunded and could not absorb the rising cost of fuel.

The collapse is a warning about travel products sold on very long commitments. A promise to undercut the cost of living on land only holds if operating costs stay flat. Fuel is the cost that rose. Buyers of open-ended voyages are exposed to the operator's balance sheet as much as to their own itinerary.
