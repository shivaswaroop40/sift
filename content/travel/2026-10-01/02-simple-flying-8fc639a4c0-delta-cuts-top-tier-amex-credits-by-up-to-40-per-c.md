Delta reduced its American Express statement credit benefit for elite members on 1 February 2026. Diamond Medallion members saw their credit fall from $700 to $500 annually, while Platinum members dropped from $400 to $250. The Amex credit had been one of the programme's most valuable perks, effectively offsetting the cost of a yearly SkyClub membership for top members.

The change matters because it reduces the tangible financial benefit of reaching Delta's highest loyalty tiers. For independent travellers who rely on elite status perks to justify programme engagement, the devaluation directly affects the return on their spending and flying.

Delta boosted three alternative choice benefits to offset the cut, raising bonus miles to 40,000 for Diamond members and travel vouchers to $550. However, these substitutes appeal less broadly than the Amex credit, which provided direct cash value regardless of redemption preferences.
