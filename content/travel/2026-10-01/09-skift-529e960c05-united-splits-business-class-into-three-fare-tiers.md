United Airlines is introducing tiered pricing for business class on domestic and short-haul international flights. The carrier will offer three tiers for United First and United Business cabins, with the base tier at the lowest price point and higher tiers adding benefits like free seat selection and additional checked baggage.

The airline already uses tiered fares successfully on long-haul routes. Extending this model to domestic flights assumes business travellers will pay premiums for flexibility and perks even on shorter journeys, allowing United to capture more revenue from this segment.
