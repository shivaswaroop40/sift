Air Canada has removed its Montreal to Algiers service from reservation systems for summer 2026 and 2027, citing doubled fuel prices since the US-Iran conflict began. The airline originally launched this route in 2017 and had planned to resume it in June 2027 with four weekly flights before extending the suspension.

For independent travellers, this eliminates a direct transatlantic option to North Africa from Canada's main French-speaking hub. Air Algérie now operates the only nonstop service on this corridor, four times weekly, while Air Canada maintains only its Montreal to Casablanca route to Africa.

Air Canada's decision may reflect deeper profitability concerns rather than temporary fuel costs alone. The suspension of around 1% of annual seat miles across multiple routes suggests a broader network reset in response to sustained operating pressures.
