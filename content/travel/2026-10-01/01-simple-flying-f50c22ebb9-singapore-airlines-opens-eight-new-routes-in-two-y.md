Singapore Airlines has added eight destinations from its Singapore hub since 2024, including returns to Madrid after 22 years and Riyadh after 12 years, alongside new services to London Gatwick, Beijing Daxing, Sapporo, Hangzhou, Western Sydney and Brussels. All routes deploy the A350-900 widebody, with some also using the 787-10.

For independent planners, these additions matter because they expand Singapore Airlines' network across Europe, Asia and Australia. The London Gatwick route in particular shows strong demand, with frequency doubling to twice daily within 28 months, making it the carrier's third busiest European destination after Heathrow and Frankfurt.

From October 2027, Singapore Airlines will add a daily Boeing 777-300ER to Gatwick alongside the existing A350 service. This introduces first class to the route for the first time and increases premium seating by 8 per cent in premium economy and 7 per cent in business class, reflecting confidence in the route's profitability.
