Atmos Rewards, the joint loyalty programme for Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines, will let members pick their earning method from January 1, 2027. Members can choose to accumulate points based on distance flown, ticket price paid, or number of flight segments. The selection opens today.

This matters because different travellers benefit from different earning structures. Frequent short-haul flyers gain more from segment-based earning, while those on long routes prefer distance-based points. Price-based earning rewards premium cabin purchases.
