A flydubai Boeing 737 experienced a rapid descent of around 15,000 feet after an altercation broke out in the cockpit. The aircraft was recovered, but the incident came close to a catastrophic outcome.

For independent travellers, incidents involving sudden loss of altitude and crew conflict are rare but significant safety events. They affect confidence in specific airlines and can prompt regulatory changes that influence routes and operations.
