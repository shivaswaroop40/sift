An Arizona guide service has sued to overturn rules requiring non-US citizens to pay a $100 fee on top of standard entry costs at 11 national parks. The operator argues only Congress has authority to set park pricing.

Independent travellers visiting US parks from abroad now face doubled costs at popular sites. The lawsuit tests whether administrative rules can impose citizenship-based pricing without legislative approval.

The 11 parks affected are not yet specified in the source, nor the guide company's name or filing details.
