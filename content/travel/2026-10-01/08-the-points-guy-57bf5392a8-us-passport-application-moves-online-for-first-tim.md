The Trump administration has announced changes to US passport applications and design. First-time applicants will be able to apply entirely online through an AI-powered platform launching by end of 2027, replacing the current requirement to visit an authorized facility in person. The new system will pre-populate existing government data, allow applicants to upload their own photo, and provide real-time tracking of application status.

This matters to frequent travellers because it removes a friction point in getting your first passport. No more booking appointments with third-party photo services or travelling to a facility. The process becomes faster and more convenient for independent planners organising their first trip.

New passport designs arrive in 2028, featuring a more prominent Great Seal in gold and interior pages depicting US history with a flipbook-style eagle. Existing passports remain valid through their expiration dates, so no urgent action is needed.
