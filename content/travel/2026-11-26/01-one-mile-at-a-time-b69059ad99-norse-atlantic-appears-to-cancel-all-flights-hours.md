Norse Atlantic Airways, which flies only Boeing 787s, said this morning that its financial situation was critical. Hours later it appears to have cancelled all flights, leaving passengers stranded. The source text is short and cuts off, so it gives no detail on routes, numbers or any refund process.

The airline said in August 2026 that it was in a difficult financial position and was looking at a sale, merger or other arrangement. According to the report, little progress followed in the months after that announcement.
