TSA PreCheck Touchless ID is now available at more than 60 major US airports. Eligible travellers can pass through security without showing a physical ID or boarding pass. The system compares the traveller's face with the passport photo held on file.

To use it, you must opt in through your airline frequent flyer profile. That means uploading a passport photo and consenting to facial identification. Only PreCheck members, or those with PreCheck access through Global Entry, can use the lanes.

Availability varies by airline and terminal, so an airport on the list does not guarantee access. Not every carrier or terminal at a given airport takes part. Check your departure airport before relying on it.

Carry a physical ID anyway. Technical faults or an officer's request may still require traditional identification. The source summary is thin on detail, and the full airport and airline list sits in the original article.
