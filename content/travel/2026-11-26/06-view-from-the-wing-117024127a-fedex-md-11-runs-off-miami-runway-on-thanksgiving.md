FedEx flight 910 from Memphis ran off the end of runway 26R at Miami on Wednesday night and stopped in grass just inside the fence at NW 67th Avenue. Both people aboard got out safely and nobody on the ground was hurt. The MD-11 landed at 8:26 p.m. Eastern.

Eighty days earlier, an Amazon Air Boeing 767 crossed the same avenue after landing on a different runway. Five people in a van died. The source says the FedEx nose lined up with the traffic light across the street.

Miami was left with one runway. The FAA held Miami-bound flights at their departure airports and projected average arrival delays of 3 hours 34 minutes. At 10:30 p.m. the diagonal runway reopened for departures, and the south runway handled both takeoffs and landings.

Weather and equipment problems were in play. Thunderstorms closed Atlantic routes, and a communications fault with Havana was fixed by 7:43 p.m. A controller told a reporter of more than 10 severe turbulence reports. These are accounts, not findings on cause.
