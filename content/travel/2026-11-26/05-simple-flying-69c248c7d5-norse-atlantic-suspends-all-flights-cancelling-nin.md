Norse Atlantic Airways suspended all flights on Thursday 26 November, a day after saying it needed financing to meet short-term liquidity needs. It was flying seven routes, with two more due to start in December. The board says it intends to file for bankruptcy if short-term financing is not secured urgently.

The airline is in talks with investors about a possible takeover that would include financing, but it says there is no assurance a viable solution will be found. Most of the network was Thailand. Five of the seven live routes served Bangkok or Phuket, from London Gatwick, Oslo and Stockholm. Gatwick to Cape Town and Orlando were the others.

The two planned launches were Manchester to Bangkok, four times a week from December to April, and Gatwick to Phuket. All used Boeing 787-9s, with 56 premium economy and 282 economy seats. Pakistan International Airlines has reverted to its own 777s on UK routes, having used Norse's leased 787s.
