Edelweiss Air, the leisure subsidiary of SWISS, has revealed details of a new cabin for its Airbus A350-900 fleet. The aircraft were acquired from LATAM and have so far kept their original interiors. The first reconfigured jet is now expected later than the original end-of-2026 target.

For independent travellers, this matters because Edelweiss flies long haul routes from Zurich to leisure spots such as the Caribbean and Cape Town. A new business class product with updated seats and cabin finishes could change the on-board experience on fares to those destinations, as well as how Edelweiss compares with SWISS mainline and other European carriers on similar routes.

The retrofit announcement was made in January 2026. The source text is truncated, so seat format, exact launch date and which routes get the first reconfigured aircraft are not stated.
