Marriott has appointed Chris Buckner as senior vice president of loyalty, replacing Dave Flueck who retires at the end of March after 22 years. Buckner previously led JetBlue's TrueBlue overhaul in May 2023 and renegotiated its Barclays credit card deal in 2021, and most recently served as chief product officer at The Points Guy.

His track record suggests Marriott members may see earlier perks before reaching full elite status, more choice in benefits and a focus on driving credit card spending. The author flags, however, that he has also defended cutting low-use bonuses, which can hide broken delivery rather than fix it.

Bonvoy has more than 295 million members, though many are inactive or signed up without consent for a 2% member rate discount. The real challenge is enforcement: thousands of independent hotel owners deliver benefits and can profit by defecting, while Marriott's customer service has limited power to compel them, as seen in a breakfast guarantee case closed seven times before payout.

Buckner inherits new long-term US card deals with Chase and American Express announced in August, so card renegotiation is not imminent. Key priorities identified include enforcing promised benefits, clarifying what status delivers at each property, restoring upgrade value after the shift from Suite Night Awards, and waiving resort fees on award stays.
