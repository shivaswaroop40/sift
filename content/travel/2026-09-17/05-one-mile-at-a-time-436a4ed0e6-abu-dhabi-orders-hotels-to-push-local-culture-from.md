Abu Dhabi's tourism authorities are requiring hotels to introduce new cultural standards covering food, dress and guest interaction. Staff must be able to name and explain at least three Emirati dishes to guests.

For travellers, the changes will show up in hotel restaurants, where menus are expected to feature more local cuisine, and in the way staff engage with guests about the destination.

The move is part of a wider Gulf push to diversify economies away from oil and reduce reliance on transit passengers. Hotels in Abu Dhabi will need to comply as the standards are rolled out.
