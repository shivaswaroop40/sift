Chase is making two changes to the Sapphire Reserve for Business card. From 1 January 2027, holders will get four annual statement credits of up to $250 each for stays of two nights or longer at The Edit properties booked through Chase Travel, up from two credits in 2026. Maximum annual value rises from $500 to $1,000.

From 15 November 2026, the 3 points per dollar earning rate on social media and search engine advertising will be capped at $1m of eligible spend per account anniversary year. Spending above that threshold earns 1 point per dollar. The cap will first apply to spending from 15 November 2026 through the statement carrying the next annual fee, then reset yearly.

The credit boost applies only to the Business card, not the personal Sapphire Reserve. Chase says the $1m cap will not affect most cardholders, as few businesses spend above that level in the categories.

A 200,000-point welcome bonus after $30,000 spent in six months is also available, valued at $4,100 by The Points Guy's September 2026 valuation.
