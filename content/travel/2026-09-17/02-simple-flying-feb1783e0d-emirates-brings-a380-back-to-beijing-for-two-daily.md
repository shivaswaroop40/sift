Emirates will deploy a refurbished 487-seat Airbus A380 on one of its two daily Dubai–Beijing services from October 4 through November 30, then again from February 1 to March 27, 2027. The aircraft replaces a Boeing 777-300ER on flight EK306/307 and introduces Premium Economy to the route for the first time.

For travellers, this means an extra wide-body option between Dubai and Beijing Capital on most days between October and March, including a westbound sector scheduled at up to 9 hours. The other daily rotation stays on the 777-300ER, and the A380 gap over December and January reflects seasonal capacity planning rather than a permanent switch.

The move is part of a wider China rebuild: Emirates now serves five mainland cities with 49 weekly flights, up from 28 in mid-2025, and has added Shenzhen and Hangzhou since July 2025. Overall seat capacity to China has grown 88%, and Guangzhou will also move to the A380 on October 1.

One caveat: the A380 is not scheduled on the route during December and January, so anyone wanting to fly the superjumbo on this sector needs to plan around those months or consider Shanghai or Guangzhou instead.
