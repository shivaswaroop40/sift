Banyan Group, the Singapore-listed owner of the Banyan Tree brand, is acquiring a 70% stake in Cape Town-based Newmark Hotels & Reserves, with a path to full ownership. The deal values Newmark at about 756 million South African rand, or $47 million. It adds 26 hotels, lodges and private game reserves to Banyan's portfolio.

Newmark lifts Banyan's portfolio by roughly a third, to nearly 130 properties across 14 brands in 28 countries. The acquisition gives Banyan a foothold in safari and nature hospitality, a segment it did not previously cover.

The purchase follows Banyan's "asset-right" model, a mix of owning some properties while managing others for third-party owners, generating fee income rather than taking on heavy real estate. Newmark will operate as a collection brand within Banyan's portfolio.
