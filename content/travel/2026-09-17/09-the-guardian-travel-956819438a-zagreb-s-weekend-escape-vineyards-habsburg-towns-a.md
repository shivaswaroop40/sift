Weekenders from Zagreb head west into the Samobor Hills, a green, rolling landscape dotted with baroque towns, forested trails and more than three dozen wineries along the Plešivica wine road. The piece frames it as an alternative to coastal Dalmatia, firmly Habsburg Croatia rather than Venetian.

It matters because this part of Croatia is reachable in under an hour from the capital, which makes it a realistic add-on for travellers already routing through Zagreb. The story flags specific stops: Samobor for kremšnita, the Žumberak nature park for hikes, Plešivica for sparkling wine, and Rude for a cheese pastry with EU protected status.

A useful caveat: this is a personal travel piece by a single writer, so the recommendations reflect one June visit and one set of tastes. Prices quoted, such as the €118 five-course tasting menu at Michelin-starred Korak, give a concrete sense of cost.
