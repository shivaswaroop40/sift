A Japan Airlines passenger moved two business class awards to a different date and airport after JAL issued a five-minute schedule change notice. They switched from Narita to Haneda, got the new Airbus A350 with premium suites, and paid no extra fees. The rebooking happened through JAL's website with Finnair reissuing the tickets within 24 hours.

For award bookers, this matters because schedule changes often force rebooking onto the same flight or require acceptance of less desirable alternatives. JAL's system let this traveler access inventory that partner airlines usually don't expose through their normal award search. The A350 suites offer beds up to 78 inches long and 24-inch screens.

The mechanism works because the operating airline can change reservations on its own website even when another airline issued the ticket, then instruct the issuer to reprocess. No guarantee applies across all partner combinations. Other travellers have had mixed results, from seamless reissues to restricted online options.
