Southwest is revamping its Rapid Rewards program on 1 January 2027, introducing new status names and a new Platinum tier that includes an unlimited Companion Pass. Current A-List members become Silver and A-List Preferred members become Gold automatically. The transition requires no action from customers.

Most status perks like free checked bags and early boarding remain unchanged. Silver members get improved earning bonuses, rising from 25% to 40%. However, Gold members face a cut from 100% to 60% earning bonus on qualifying flights, though those earning Gold in 2026 keep the 100% rate through 2027.

Customers who buy premium fares lose significantly. Choice Extra fares drop from 14x to 10x points per dollar, and Choice Preferred from 10x to 8x. A Gold member buying Choice Extra will earn 57% fewer points than an equivalent A-List Preferred member does now. Members between Gold and Platinum can earn limited Companion Pass benefits worth three round trips at 95,000 tier qualifying points.
