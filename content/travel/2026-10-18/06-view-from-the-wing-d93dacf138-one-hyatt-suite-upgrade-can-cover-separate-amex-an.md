Hyatt members report getting a single suite upgrade award applied across separate hotel reservations, including consecutive nights booked through Chase's The Edit and American Express Fine Hotels + Resorts. A concierge worked with the hotel to keep the guest in a suite for the whole stay.
That's useful when hotel credits push you into booking one trip in pieces. You don't want to use another upgrade certificate just because the payment went through a different website.
