Delta Air Lines has written to US Representative Frank Pallone denying it engages in "surveillance pricing" based on personal data, stating no fare product targets customers with individualised prices using that data.

The carrier has separately set out a public plan to move towards individually generated offers. Former president Glen Hauenstein told investors in November 2024 that AI would help price tickets for "you, the individual," describing a multi-year move from static fare grids towards personal offers.

Delta's pricing vendor Fetcherr markets tools that use customer lifetime value, past purchase behaviour and real-time booking context to produce "segments of one" pricing. Fetcherr removed promotional language after criticism, though archived materials remain.

US air carriers fall outside the FTC's Section 5 authority, and 2014 DOT guidance did not classify personalised airline offers as inherently unlawful. State-level pricing laws are largely preempted by the Airline Deregulation Act.
