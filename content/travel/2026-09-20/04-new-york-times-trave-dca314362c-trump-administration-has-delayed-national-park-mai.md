The Trump administration has postponed maintenance projects at national parks across the country and shifted resources towards projects in Washington favoured by the president. The New York Times Travel piece details which parks and programmes have been affected by the budget disruption.

This matters for independent travellers who plan to visit US national parks, as deferred maintenance can mean closed facilities, fewer staff, deteriorating trails and reduced visitor services during trips.

The piece also covers how funding has been diverted, which helps travellers anticipate which sites may be most affected when planning routes and itineraries.
