Researchers developed a mechanism-based kinetic model for NH3-SCR over Cu-SSZ-13 using experimentally measured parameters. NO oxidation and NH3 oxidation occur on multinuclear Cu species, while standard SCR proceeds on exchanged Cu ions via Cu II/Cu I redox cycling. The model captures dual kinetic regimes at low and high temperatures.

For process design, this addresses a long-standing modelling gap in mobile aftertreatment. Reliable kinetics enable accurate prediction of NOx conversion across the operating window and inform catalyst loading decisions. The experimental constraint approach reduces the uncertainty in simulating real reactor behaviour under variable conditions.
