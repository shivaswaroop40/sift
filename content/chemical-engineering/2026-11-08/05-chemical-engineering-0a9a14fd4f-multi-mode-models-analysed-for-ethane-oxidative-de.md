Chemical Engineering Journal has published an analysis of multi-mode models for a monolith reactor carrying out the homogeneous oxidative dehydrogenation of ethane to ethylene. The chemistry is homogeneous, meaning it proceeds in a single phase rather than on a solid catalyst. The available text stops at the title, so the models examined and their results cannot be reported here.

The topic matters for ethylene production and reactor scale-up. Ethylene is made today mainly by steam cracking, and oxidative dehydrogenation of ethane is one proposed alternative. Model choice shapes predicted conversion, selectivity and heat management, and multi-mode formulations trade detail against computational cost. This entry is a pointer to the paper rather than a report of its findings.
