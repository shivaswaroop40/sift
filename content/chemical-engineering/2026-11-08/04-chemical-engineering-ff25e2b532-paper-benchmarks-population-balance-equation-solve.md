Chemical Engineering Science has published a benchmarking study of numerical solvers for population balance equations. The paper evaluates solvers on speed, accuracy and reliability, and takes the perspective of engineering applications rather than purely mathematical test problems. Only the title is available in this notice, so the solvers tested and their rankings are not stated here.

Population balance equations track how particle populations evolve in operations such as crystallisation, granulation and polymerisation. Solver choice affects simulation cost and the confidence one can place in predicted size distributions during design and scale-up. A benchmark framed for engineering use can guide that choice, though the comparisons themselves sit in the full paper.
