The paper presents a CFD design study of the unit cell of a prismatic mesostructured reactor aimed at intensifying secondary flow. The geometry uses internal mesostructures to drive mixing and heat or mass transfer within each repeating cell.

Secondary flow intensification matters because it can improve transport rates in catalytic or reactive channels without increasing pressure drop dramatically. For process engineers, this offers a route to more compact, higher-performance reactor hardware.

The work is at the CFD design stage, with no experimental validation reported in the available text. Performance claims are therefore limited to what the simulations show.
