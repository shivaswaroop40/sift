A study in Chemical Engineering Science investigates heat transfer and hydrogen production in ammonia decomposition reactors using computational fluid dynamics coupled with machine learning. The work aims to optimise reactor performance for hydrogen generation from ammonia.

For chemical engineers designing hydrogen carrier systems, the coupling of CFD with machine learning offers a route to map operating conditions against reactor geometry and thermal behaviour. This matters because ammonia decomposition is a candidate pathway for storing and transporting hydrogen, where endothermic reaction management controls overall efficiency.

The paper claims improved performance prediction through the combined approach, though the abstract provides limited quantitative detail on conversion, temperature gradients, or specific optimisation gains achieved.
