A study in Chemical Engineering Journal reports MgO/Ni–Ce–Al catalysts with tunable redox–basic interfaces for super-dry reforming of methane. Leaching-assisted preparation is used to adjust the balance between metal and oxide sites.
The work targets dry reforming of methane, a reaction that converts CO2 and CH4 into syngas. Controlling the interface is presented as a way to influence activity and stability under demanding reforming conditions.

For a process engineer, the interest is whether such interface tuning can give a measurable gain in conversion or coking resistance at realistic temperatures. The paper provides a route to tailor acid–base and redox properties of Ni-based reforming catalysts.
