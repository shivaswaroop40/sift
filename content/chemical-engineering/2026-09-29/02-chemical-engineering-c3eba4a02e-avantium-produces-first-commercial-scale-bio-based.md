Avantium has produced the first batch of furandicarboxylic acid (FDCA) at its FDCA Flagship Plant in Delfzijl, the Netherlands, described as the world's first commercial-scale FDCA facility. All major process units have been successfully commissioned, and the company expects commercial sales of FDCA to begin by the end of 2026.

The milestone validates Avantium's proprietary YXY technology under commercial operating conditions. FDCA is the monomer for poly(ethylene furanoate), or PEF, a bio-based alternative to PET. Validating the process at scale supports both Avantium's own product sales and ongoing licensing discussions with prospective partners worldwide.

Next steps include a product qualification programme, transition to continuous operations, and ramp-up to full design capacity over 12 to 24 months. The company will need to meet specifications for customers during this period before scaling further.
