Researchers report a scaled-up flash vacuum pyrolysis route to benzocyclobutene, a strained four-membered ring used in polymer and pharmaceutical chemistry. The paper describes reactor modifications and operating conditions that move the synthesis beyond laboratory scale.

Process safety and selectivity matter here. Flash vacuum pyrolysis operates at short residence times and high temperatures, which limits side reactions but creates challenges in heat transfer and throughput. Demonstrating scale-up addresses a common bottleneck between lab discovery and pilot production.
