MAIRE subsidiary Tecnimont USA has signed a Front-End Engineering Design (FEED) contract with Argent LNG for a 25 million tons per annum LNG export facility at Port Fourchon, Louisiana. The scope covers FEED engineering, FERC permitting support, project execution planning, schedule and cost estimation, and safety and reliability studies.

The contract builds on a cooperation agreement signed in January 2026 that named Tecnimont as engineering and technology integrated services provider. Following FERC approval, FEED completion, and final investment decision, the project is expected to move into the EPC phase. First cargo is targeted for 2030.

For engineers, the scope is broad: process design for large-scale gas treatment and liquefaction, integration of multiple technology packages, and modular design considerations. The facility is sited in a region marketed as hurricane-resilient, which will shape layout, safety studies and reliability targets.
