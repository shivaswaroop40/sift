The paper presents a coarse-grained CFD-DEM framework that uses the simplified Johnson-Kendall-Roberts (SJKR) contact model to simulate cohesive powder fluidisation in metal powder energy systems.

It matters to chemical engineers because accurate modelling of cohesive powders affects design and scale-up of fluidised-bed reactors used in metal-based energy processes, where particle-particle adhesion drives agglomeration and defluidisation.

The authors claim the coarse-graining approach reduces computational cost relative to full-resolution DEM while preserving the cohesive response, though the abstract provides no quantitative comparison.

Process designers working on metal powder fluidisation can use this framework to screen operating conditions before running expensive physical trials.
