Johnson Controls has released the Sabroe HitemHP line of industrial high-temperature heat pumps, built in Europe and aimed at sectors such as pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, advanced manufacturing, and food and beverage that need process heat above 100°C.

In one customer installation, the company reports the system reached up to three times the energy efficiency of an electric boiler, cut energy costs by over 91%, reduced annual water costs by up to 30%, and lowered on-site CO2 emissions by up to 82% when paired with waste heat from a refrigeration plant.

The range uses ultra-low-GWP butane and iso-butane refrigerants to align with upcoming EU regulation, and is built around the Sabroe SMC single-stage reciprocating compressor, which the company says lifts reliability and energy performance under part-load and extends service intervals by nearly 50%.

Capacity is turndown-able from 100% to 18%, and under part-load the unit is reported to deliver up to 20% higher coefficient of performance than at full load. Units are factory-assembled and configured to order to shorten lead times and installation.
