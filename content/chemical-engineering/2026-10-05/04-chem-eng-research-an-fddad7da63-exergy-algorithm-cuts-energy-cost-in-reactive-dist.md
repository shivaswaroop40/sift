Researchers developed an algorithm that uses exergy analysis to optimise reactive distillation columns, testing it on ethylene oxide hydrolysis and cumene production. The method identifies where energy is wasted in the system and adjusts operating conditions to reduce losses.

For process design and operations, this matters because reactive distillation already couples reaction and separation, but energy intensity remains high. An exergy-based approach finds the thermodynamic sweet spot more precisely than traditional methods, potentially lowering both utility consumption and capital cost.

The validation on two distinct industrial reactions suggests the algorithm could apply across reactive distillation applications, though the paper itself does not disclose the magnitude of energy savings achieved.
