Researchers combined reaction engineering methods with droplet shrinkage kinetics into a CFD-DPM framework for spray drying. The model tracks how solute-containing droplets shrink and dry during the process, incorporating both mass transfer and kinetic behaviour.

Spray dryers are widespread in pharmaceutical and food industries for powder production. Accurate droplet behaviour prediction affects residence time, drying efficiency, and final product quality, making better simulation tools valuable for process design and scale-up.
