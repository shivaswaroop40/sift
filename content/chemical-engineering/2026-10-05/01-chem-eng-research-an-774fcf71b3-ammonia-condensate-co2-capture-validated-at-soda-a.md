Researchers tested ammonia-condensate CO₂ capture integrated with Solvay soda ash production at industrial scale. The work validates capture performance and establishes energy requirements for larger deployment.

Soda ash plants emit significant CO₂. Integrating capture into existing Solvay process loops reduces retrofit costs and uses waste heat already present. This matters because it shows how to add carbon removal to established chemical manufacturing.

The study provides scale-up data needed for commercial deployment. Energy demand and process interactions become concrete at pilot or demonstration scale rather than laboratory conditions.
