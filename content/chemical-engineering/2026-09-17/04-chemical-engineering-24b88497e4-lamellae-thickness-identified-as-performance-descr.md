Researchers report that lamellae thickness in biaxially oriented polypropylene (BOPP) capacitor films is a key structural descriptor for performance at high temperature and high voltage, validated at industrial scale.

The work links a polymer morphology metric to dielectric performance under demanding conditions, giving process and film designers a measurable target rather than relying on bulk properties alone.

The study is framed as industrial-scale validation, suggesting the relationship holds beyond laboratory samples, though the source text does not give specific numerical gains or test conditions.

For practitioners, the result points to lamellae thickness as a candidate control variable in BOPP film production and a potential screening parameter for high-temperature, high-voltage capacitor grades.
