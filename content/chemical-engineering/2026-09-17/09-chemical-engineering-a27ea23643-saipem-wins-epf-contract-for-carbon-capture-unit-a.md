Saipem has been awarded an Engineering, Procurement and Fabrication contract by Ren-Gas for the carbon capture unit at the Tampere Power-to-Gas project in Finland. The scope covers the first full-scale unit based on Saipem's CO2 Solutions technology and Bluenzyme 200 solution, including engineering, fabrication, and advisory services through commissioning and performance testing.

The capture unit will be integrated with Ren-Gas's e-methane facility in Tampere, planned to produce around 200 GWh per year of renewable synthetic methane for heavy-duty road transport in Germany via existing gas infrastructure. The contract takes effect only after Ren-Gas reaches its final investment decision on the project.

The award follows Ren-Gas's December 2024 selection of Saipem's Bluenzyme technology for post-combustion capture at the site. The project is positioned as part of a wider effort to link carbon capture with Power-to-X applications and decarbonise transport fuel supply chains in Northern Europe.
