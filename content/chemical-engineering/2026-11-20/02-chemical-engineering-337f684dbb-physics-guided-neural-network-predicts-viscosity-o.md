A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal describes a physics-guided neural network for predicting the viscosity of organic liquid mixtures. The model uses an aggregation scheme inspired by the McAllister multibody approach. The stated aim is to generalise to components not seen during training, including in strongly non-ideal mixtures.

Mixture viscosity is hard to predict where interactions are strong, and empirical mixing rules often fail there. A model that handles new components without refitting would help with solvent selection, pump and heat exchanger sizing, and screening of candidate mixtures before measurement.

The source text supplied is only the title. No accuracy figures, dataset size, component classes or benchmark comparisons are available. The claim of generalisation to unseen components cannot be assessed from what is given, and it should be checked against the paper's validation method.
