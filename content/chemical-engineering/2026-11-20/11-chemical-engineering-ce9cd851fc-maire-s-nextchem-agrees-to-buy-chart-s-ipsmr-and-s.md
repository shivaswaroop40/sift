Nextchem, part of Italy's Maire, has signed a binding agreement to buy Chart Industries' mid-scale IPSMR and small-scale LNG process technologies for $220 million in cash. Chart is owned by Baker Hughes. The deal covers IPSMR, IPSMR+, the early-stage IPDMR, over 90 patents, trademarks and a team of 12 engineers and commissioning specialists.

IPSMR is a pre-cooled single mixed-refrigerant process for trains of about 0.5 to 2 MTPA, with a sweet spot of 1.4 to 2 MTPA. IPDMR is meant to extend this to 2 MTPA and above 4 MTPA. It has about 14 MTPA of operating references, 57 MTPA under construction and about 90 MTPA selected for pre-FID projects.

For anyone choosing a liquefaction process, a new licensor owner changes the supplier landscape for mid-scale and modular trains. Chart will keep supplying cold boxes under a 10-year agreement. Nextchem moves from gas treatment and purification into liquefaction licensing, and can engage clients earlier in project development.

Maire expects the licensing business to earn at least $25 million EBITDA in 2027, at a high-single-digit multiple. Banca BPER has underwritten the funding. Closing depends on regulatory approvals, expected by year-end or early January 2027. IPDMR is still early-stage, so its larger-scale claims are unproven.
