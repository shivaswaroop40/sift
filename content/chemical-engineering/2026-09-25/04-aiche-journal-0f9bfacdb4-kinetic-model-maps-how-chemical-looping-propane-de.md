Researchers report a macroscopic kinetic model for chemical looping oxidative propane dehydrogenation (CL-ODH) over a VCeAl oxygen carrier, separating lattice oxygen-mediated oxidative dehydrogenation from catalytic propane dehydrogenation on reduced sites.

Across 470 to 515 °C, the system shifts gradually from oxidative dehydrogenation at higher temperatures to catalytic dehydrogenation at lower temperatures, because oxidative dehydrogenation has the lower activation energy of the two pathways.

Higher propane pressure depletes lattice oxygen faster and pushes the reaction into the catalytic dehydrogenation regime, meaning pressure choice directly controls which pathway dominates and how much oxygen carrier is needed.

The authors state the model can support scale-up by quantifying selectivity and oxygen carrier conversion, though only the VCeAl system at 470–515 °C is covered.
