A new simulation framework combines electron transfer at the electrode-electrolyte interface with charge and mass transport in the electrolyte, published in J. Chem. Phys. 163, 164113. It treats structural, thermodynamic and statistical properties of the electrical double layer at non-ideal conducting interfaces in a consistent way.

The model reproduces Tafel and Butler-Volmer kinetics and overpotential behaviour with few ad-hoc assumptions, and yields stationary non-equilibrium quantities such as entropy production. An explicit extension adds the effect of fluctuating electric fields on the electron transfer rate.

The work targets comprehensive modelling of basic electrochemical devices such as electrolytic cells and batteries. For practitioners, the value is a single framework that ties interface structure to macroscopic kinetic laws without separate post hoc corrections.
