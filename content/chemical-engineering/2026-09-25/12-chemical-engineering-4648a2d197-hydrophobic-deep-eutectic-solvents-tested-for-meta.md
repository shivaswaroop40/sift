The study examines neutral coordination-type hydrophobic deep eutectic solvents (DES) for recovering strategic metals from spent battery leachate, positioning the work within battery recycling and circular economy goals.

For practising chemical engineers, hydrophobic DES offer a water-immiscible extraction route that could sidestep volatile organic solvents and reduce downstream waste, though the abstract gives no yields, selectivities or partition coefficients.

The paper claims sustainable recovery of strategic metals, but without disclosed metal targets, equilibrium data or stripping steps the practical performance and scale-up prospects remain unclear from the abstract alone.
