UK water regulator Ofwat has provisionally approved a £3.4bn investment package for projects led by water companies to improve and safeguard national infrastructure.

A share of the funding is earmarked for PFAS treatment, addressing persistent per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances that are difficult to remove by conventional water treatment and increasingly subject to tightening regulatory limits.

Separate allocations cover infrastructure to support data centres, which require large volumes of process and cooling water and place additional demand on existing supplies.

The figure sits within Ofwat's wider five-year price determination, so water companies will recover the spend through customer bills subject to final sign-off.
