A framework for scheduling LNG terminals combines a Wasserstein distributionally robust optimisation approach with physics-informed models, addressing uncertainty on both the supply and demand sides. It also incorporates asset health management across multiple timescales.

The method aims to give terminal operators a scheduling tool that stays feasible when arrival and offtake forecasts deviate, while tracking equipment condition to schedule maintenance without halting throughput.

The paper is an abstract-level report with few quantitative results in the source text, so claimed gains over conventional scheduling approaches are not stated here.
