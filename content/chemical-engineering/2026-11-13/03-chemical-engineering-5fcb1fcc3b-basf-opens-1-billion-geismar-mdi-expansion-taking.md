BASF has inaugurated its new MDI plant at the Verbund site in Geismar, Louisiana. The project cost about $1 billion and is the company's largest wholly owned investment in North America. It almost doubles annual MDI capacity at the site to roughly 600,000 metric tons. BASF says it finished on time.

The work ran in three phases from 2018. A new MDI synthesis unit started up in 2020, and several upstream units were expanded in 2021. The final phase added new upstream units and a splitter. BASF also raised aniline capacity at Geismar, which supplies agricultural chemicals, rubber, and dye and pigment customers.

For practitioners, this is a long phased build tied closely to an integrated site. Aniline, the MDI precursor, is made on site, and the extra aniline gives BASF material for other markets as well. The source gives no unit-level technical details. BASF cites construction, appliances, automotive, footwear and furniture as end uses for the polyurethanes made from MDI.
