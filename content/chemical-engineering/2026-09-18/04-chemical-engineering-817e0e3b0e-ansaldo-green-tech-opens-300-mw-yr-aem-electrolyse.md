Ansaldo Green Tech has completed a high-volume production line for anion exchange membrane (AEM) electrolyser stacks at its Genoa facility, with capacity exceeding 300 MW per year. The line is part of a turnkey hydrogen plant project backed by more than $300 million of public funding through Italy's PNRR and the EU IPCEI Hydrogen programme. Commissioning and optimisation will continue before industrial production starts.

For process engineers sizing green hydrogen systems, the line offers a new vendor with a modular range spanning a 1 MW containerised unit producing about 500 kg of hydrogen per day up to multi-MW skid-mounted systems. Ansaldo Green Tech says its AEM stacks are built to follow variable renewable input and deliver pressurised hydrogen directly.

The claim that AEM units handle load swings efficiently and output pressurised gas comes from the company; independent performance data is not given in the announcement. Capacity above 300 MW/yr signals genuine industrial scale rather than pilot volumes, but actual stack output, degradation rates and hydrogen purity remain to be demonstrated once the line runs.
