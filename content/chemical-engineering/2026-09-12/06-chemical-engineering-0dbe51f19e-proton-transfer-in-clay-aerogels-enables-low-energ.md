A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal describes proton transfer effects in clay aerogels. The authors apply acid-site-catalyzed regeneration technology to direct air capture, with the aim of low-energy-consumption sorbent regeneration. Proton transfer is highlighted as the underlying mechanism in the title.

Regeneration energy is a key cost in direct air capture. If acid-site catalysis in clay aerogels reduces that energy demand, it could improve the economics of DAC processes. The title gives no quantitative results, so the claimed benefit is not yet verified.
