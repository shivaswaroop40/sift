A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal proposes a predictive framework for selecting nanofiltration membranes to control disinfection by-products in drinking water treatment. The framework is described as predictive, meaning it aims to forecast membrane performance from feed and membrane properties rather than relying only on trial and error.

For engineers working on potable water treatment, this matters because nanofiltration is a recognised barrier against DBP precursors, but membrane selection is often case-specific. A validated predictive framework could reduce pilot testing and speed up design decisions. The available text is only the title, so no numerical results are reported here.
