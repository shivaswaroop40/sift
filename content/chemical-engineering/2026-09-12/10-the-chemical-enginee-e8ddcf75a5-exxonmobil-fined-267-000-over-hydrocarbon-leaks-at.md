ExxonMobil has been ordered to pay £267,000 ($361,000) after multiple hydrocarbon leaks at its ethylene plant in Mossmorran, Scotland. The penalty follows what the original report describes as woeful maintenance at the site. The leaks prompted enforcement action, though the specific dates and substances are not given in the brief item.

For process engineers, the case is a reminder that leak integrity depends on maintenance quality, not just design. Hydrocarbon releases at ethylene plants can create fire, explosion and environmental risks. The fine is modest relative to company revenues, but the public record and regulatory scrutiny can drive further costs. The brief report gives no technical detail on the leak mechanisms.
