S-Oil has reached final investment decision for Axens' Liquid Phase Xylene Isomerization (LPI) unit at its Aromatics Complex #1. The project follows a licence agreement signed in late 2024. Start-up is scheduled for Q2 2027. Axens will provide the technology licence, process design package and technical services for execution and start-up.

The technology is owned by ExxonMobil Holdings and licensed by Axens. It has been implemented in several aromatics complexes worldwide. By balancing liquid-phase and vapour-phase isomerization units, it delivers substantial energy savings across the xylenes loop and improves complex economics. S-Oil says the investment strengthens its petrochemicals competitiveness and supports growing paraxylene demand with lower emissions.

The announcement gives no capacity, investment value or expected savings figures. The claimed benefits rest on Axens' process optimisation and catalyst improvements, which the company says have continued across commercial installations.
