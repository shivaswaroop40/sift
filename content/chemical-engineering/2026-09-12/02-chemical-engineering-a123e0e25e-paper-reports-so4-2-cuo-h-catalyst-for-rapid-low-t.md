This paper reports a facile preparation route for a SO4^2-/CuO-Hβ composite catalyst. The catalyst is claimed to be efficient and stable for regenerating CO2-loaded amine solvents at low temperature and at a rapid rate. The title gives no quantitative performance data.

For process engineers, low-temperature regeneration of CO2-loaded amine solvents could cut the energy penalty of carbon capture. A stable solid acid catalyst that accelerates stripping at lower temperatures would reduce reboiler duty and solvent degradation. The specific SO4^2-/CuO-Hβ formulation is the key detail, but the paper's claims need checking against the full experimental results.
