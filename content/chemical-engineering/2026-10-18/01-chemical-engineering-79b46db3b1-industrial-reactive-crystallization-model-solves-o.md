Researchers coupled reaction kinetics, thermodynamics, and population balance modelling to diagnose and fix oiling out in a manufacturing reactive crystallization process. The integrated model predicted the conditions causing product separation as liquid droplets rather than crystals.

Oiling out degrades product quality and recovery. Plant operators need reliable methods to identify root causes and adjust parameters. This work demonstrates how simulation can prevent yield loss without expensive pilot-scale trials.

The study shows that population balance modelling, combined with accurate thermodynamic and kinetic data, can predict phase behaviour during crystallization. This approach allows engineers to optimize cooling rates and feed timing to avoid oiling out at scale.
