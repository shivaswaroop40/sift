Researchers have built a computational model to predict how amino acids and salts dissolve in concentrated nutrient media for Chinese hamster ovary cell culture. The framework combines modified UNIFAC thermodynamics with Debye–Hückel ionic corrections and equilibrium constraints for complexation and precipitation.

Biomanufacturing intensity demands very high nutrient densities, where solubility limits become process bottlenecks. This model lets process chemists screen media formulations and pH adjustments before running experiments, reducing trial cycles in basal and feed media design.

The parameters were fitted against amino-acid binary and ternary data, then tested on multicomponent CHO media case studies to show how composition and pH affect what remains dissolved.
