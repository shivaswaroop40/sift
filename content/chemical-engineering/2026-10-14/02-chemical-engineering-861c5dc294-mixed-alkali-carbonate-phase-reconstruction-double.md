Researchers treated α-spodumene ore with a mixture of sodium and potassium carbonate at 800°C. This reconstructed the mineral phase and released lithium more readily than conventional roasting. The method achieved significantly higher extraction rates from the ore.

Spodumene is the primary lithium source for battery production. Direct leaching of spodumene is inefficient; most processes use energy-intensive roasting. A phase-reconstruction step that improves leaching performance reduces the thermal load and chemical demand.

The mixed alkali approach differs from single-alkali treatments. The combination changes how the mineral breaks down, improving accessibility of lithium ions. This matters for cost and energy use in emerging lithium supply chains.
