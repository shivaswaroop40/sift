A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal presents a high-fidelity, multi-scale model of proton exchange membrane (PEM) fuel cells. The title says it includes a systematic procedure for calibrating model parameters. It also says the model is used to investigate how materials and cell structure affect performance.

The source text contains only the title. No abstract, results, parameter values, validation data or performance figures are available, so the size of any effect cannot be stated here.

For practitioners, parameter calibration is often the weak point of fuel cell models. A systematic method could make such models more usable for design and scale-up. Whether this one does so cannot be judged from the title alone.
