A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal examines Al2O3-stabilised CaO sorbents for carbonation. It sets out to map the operating window in temperature and CO2 concentration. The focus is the balance between faster carbonation kinetics and overall carbonation efficiency.

Only the title is available. No abstract, data, sorbent composition, temperature range or CO2 partial pressures were provided, so the findings cannot be stated.

The title suggests a trade-off. Conditions that speed up carbonation may reduce how much of the CaO is converted. For calcium looping design, that would bear on choosing carbonator temperature and CO2 partial pressure. This is an inference from the title, not a reported result.
