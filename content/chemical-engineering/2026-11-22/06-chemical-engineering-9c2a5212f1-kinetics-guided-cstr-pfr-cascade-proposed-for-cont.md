A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal describes a multistep continuous synthesis of quinizarin derivatives. The route uses a cascade of a continuous stirred-tank reactor (CSTR) and a plug-flow reactor (PFR), with evaporation assisting the process. The title says the design is guided by reaction kinetics.

The source text gives only the title. It does not report yields, residence times, temperatures, solvents or the specific derivatives made. It does not say what the evaporation step removes or how the stages are coupled. Any claim about performance would be a guess.
