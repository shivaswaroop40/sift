Chemical Engineering Science has published a study comparing flow-field architectures for proton exchange membrane water electrolysers. The work uses a multiphysics model and a multi-criteria assessment to evaluate the designs. Only the title is available to this digest, so no results, geometries, or performance figures can be reported.

Flow-field layout governs water distribution, gas removal, and pressure drop in an electrolyser cell. It also affects current distribution and cell voltage. A multi-criteria assessment matters here because gains in one metric often cost another, for example lower mass-transport losses against higher pumping power. Designers choosing a flow plate need that trade-off made explicit.
