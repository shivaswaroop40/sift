The paper uses coupled CFD and discrete phase modelling to study how sand-laden liquid erodes a packer mandrel under high-temperature flow. Erosion patterns are mapped across the mandrel geometry under varying solid loadings and flow rates.

Such erosion is a recurring failure mode in oil and gas completions, where sand production and high downhole temperatures combine. The findings help designers identify wear hotspots and set velocity or sand concentration limits to extend mandrel life.
