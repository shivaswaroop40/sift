Chemical Engineering Science has published a paper titled "Barrier-dependent reaction-diffusion coupling in electroreduction of carbon dioxide to formic acid". The title states that the coupling between reaction and diffusion in this system varies with a barrier. The available text gives no abstract, so the identity of the barrier and any supporting results are not reported here.

The subject matters for electrolyser design. Formic acid is a common product of CO2 electroreduction, and performance at useful current densities is often set by the interplay of electrode kinetics and species transport rather than kinetics alone. If the paper quantifies how a barrier changes that interplay, it could inform separator choice and operating limits. That cannot be confirmed from the title alone.
