A paper in Chemical Engineering Research and Design sets out a phase-wise method for parameterizing mechanistic fermentation models. The work uses industrial-scale data rather than laboratory measurements. It links the three coupled variables at the core of fermentation kinetics, growth, substrate utilization, and product formation, within one mechanistic structure fitted phase by phase.

For engineers running fermenters at scale, model parameterization is often the bottleneck. Plant data are noisy and a single global fit can smear errors across a whole batch. A phase-wise fit offers a structured route to isolate growth kinetics from product formation, which matters for scale-up, control design, and yield optimisation. The reliance on industrial-scale data, rather than lab experiments, is the notable part of the scope.
