A study in Chemical Engineering Journal reports an electrodialysis stack that regenerates KOH-based absorbent saturated with CO2 in a single pass. The stack performs gas-liquid separation internally. CO2 released during regeneration is therefore handled inside the unit rather than in external degassing equipment. The work targets regeneration in alkaline capture, where KOH takes up CO2 from gas streams.

Regeneration is the main energy cost in alkaline capture and usually needs separate stripping or degassing hardware. Electrodialysis drives the separation with electricity rather than steam, which suits electrified sites and intermittent renewable supply. Internal gas-liquid separation could remove an external degasser from the flowsheet and cut the equipment count. Single-pass operation also avoids recirculating solvent through the stack.
