Chemical Engineering Science has published a case study on the coordinated retrofit of a chemical process and its refrigeration utility system. The subject is a TFE unit. The title indicates that the process retrofit and the refrigeration utility retrofit are handled together, rather than the utility being reworked after the process changes. No abstract or results accompany the available text.

The topic matters because refrigeration is often a major utility load in low-temperature chemical processes. A process retrofit changes heating and cooling duties, which shifts the demand placed on the refrigeration system. Treating both retrofits as one design problem can expose interactions that a sequential approach would miss, such as refrigeration equipment left poorly matched to the new duties.
