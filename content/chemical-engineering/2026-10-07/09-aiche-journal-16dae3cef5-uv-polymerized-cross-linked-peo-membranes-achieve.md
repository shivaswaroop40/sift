Researchers synthesised soluble cross-linked poly(ethylene oxide) using intermittent UV irradiation to control free radical polymerization. They coated this onto a 45 nm selective layer, achieving CO2 permeance of 3074 gas permeation units with CO2/N2 selectivity of 38.3.

This addresses a known gap: cross-linked PEO is effective for CO2 capture but difficult to process into thin films via solution methods. The UV strategy controls polymerization uniformity, enabling defect-free coatings without conventional synthesis constraints.

The membrane showed operational stability and scalability potential, indicating a path toward continuous manufacturing for carbon capture plants.
