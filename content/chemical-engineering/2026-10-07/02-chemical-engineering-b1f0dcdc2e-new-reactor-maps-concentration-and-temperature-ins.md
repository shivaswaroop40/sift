Researchers built a single pellet profile reactor that directly measures concentration and temperature gradients within a porous catalyst pellet. The device provides spatial maps of reaction conditions at micrometre resolution.

For process design, this addresses a real constraint: predicting pellet performance requires knowing whether diffusion or reaction limits conversion across the particle. The new data can validate or refine diffusion-reaction models used in commercial reactor simulations.

Scale-up relies on accurate effectiveness factors. Better measurement of internal concentration and temperature profiles improves confidence in extrapolating lab kinetics to industrial-scale fixed beds.
