A paper in Chemical Engineering Research and Design presents a dynamic model of dehydration by pervaporation for multicomponent esterification mixtures. The model is built in Aspen Custom Modeler. The title states that it has been validated. The source text gives only the title, so the validation data and results are not available here.

Esterification is equilibrium limited, and removing water drives conversion. Pervaporation is a known way to do this, often in a hybrid with reaction or distillation. A dynamic model, rather than a steady-state one, could help with start-up, disturbances and control design for such units.

The source gives no detail on membrane type, the esters studied, the model structure or how well it fits experimental data. Any claim about accuracy or usefulness for scale-up cannot be judged from the title alone.
