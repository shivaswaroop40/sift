A paper in Chemical Engineering Research and Design describes an open-source model of palladium-silver membrane reactors for hydrogen production by steam methane reforming. The title is the only text available, so the model's structure, validation data and results are not stated here.

Membrane reactors combine reaction and hydrogen separation in one unit. Removing hydrogen through the Pd-Ag layer shifts the equilibrium, which can allow lower operating temperatures or higher methane conversion. An open model lets engineers inspect the assumptions and reproduce the results instead of relying on proprietary code.
