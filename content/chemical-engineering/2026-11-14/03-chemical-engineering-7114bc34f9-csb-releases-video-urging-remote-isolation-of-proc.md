The US Chemical Safety Board has released a safety video called "RIPE for Change". It is based on the board's 2024 study on remote isolation of process equipment. It features Chairperson Steve Owens and Director of Recommendations Charles Barbee.

The video covers three 2019 incidents: the ITC terminal fire in Deer Park, the PES refinery fire and explosions in Philadelphia, and the TPC Group fire and explosions in Port Neches. The CSB found that the fires burned from several days to over a month. It says this was partly because the sites had no remote isolation equipment. All three relied on manual or locally operated valves that staff could not reach safely during the events.

Barbee says good industry guidance has existed for years but has not been followed consistently. The CSB wants industry standards and federal regulations to require remote isolation where appropriate. Its recommendations are directed at API, EPA and OSHA. Owens urges facilities to assess remote isolation now, even without a formal requirement, and to install it where workers can operate it safely.
