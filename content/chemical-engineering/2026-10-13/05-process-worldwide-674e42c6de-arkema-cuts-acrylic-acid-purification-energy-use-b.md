Arkema has commissioned a new acrylic acid purification unit at its Carling site in France, backed by a 150 million euro investment. The process cuts energy consumption by 25 percent and reduces site carbon intensity by 20 percent, while cutting solvent use significantly.

For process engineers, this matters because acrylic monomers feed into coatings, adhesives and water treatment. The improvements show how retrofit purification can deliver substantial efficiency gains without replacing downstream capacity. The project signals competitive pressure on European producers to decarbonize.

The work was partly funded by France 2030 and EU grants. Arkema plans cumulative decarbonization investment of 400 million euros through 2030 across its portfolio.
