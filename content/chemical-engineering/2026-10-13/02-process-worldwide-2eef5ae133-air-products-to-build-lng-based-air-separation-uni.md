Air Products has signed a definitive agreement to design, build and operate an LNG-based air separation unit at Pengerang in Johor, Malaysia for a joint venture led by Petronas Gas and Dialog Group. The facility will produce more than 600 tonnes per day of liquid oxygen, nitrogen and argon, coming onstream in early 2027.

For process engineers, this is Air Products' fifth LNG-based ASU in Asia and extends production capability in Southern and Central Malaysia. The plant leverages cold energy from LNG regasification to liquefy air, improving energy efficiency and cutting production emissions compared to conventional air separation.

The facility will supply merchant gases to electrical, electronics, petrochemical, aerospace and manufacturing sectors across the region. The energy-efficient design allows customers to reduce their industrial gas supply emissions.
