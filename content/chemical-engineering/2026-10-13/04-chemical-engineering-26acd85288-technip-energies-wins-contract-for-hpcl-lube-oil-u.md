Technip Energies has been awarded an EPC contract by HPCL to build a 550-kiloton-per-annum integrated hydrocracker and catalytic dewaxing unit, plus an 850-kiloton-per-annum solvent deasphalting unit at the Mumbai Refinery. The project will modernize lube oil base stock production and upgrade heavy refinery streams.

For process engineers, this matters because it shows how major refiners are retrofitting existing plants to improve yield from marginal streams. Converting heavier feedstocks into higher-value lube base stocks and specialty products increases per-barrel revenue without new crude processing capacity.

The project integrates two technologies within an operating refinery, a common constraint in revamp work. Technip's 20-year relationship with HPCL suggests prior experience with the site layout and operational challenges.
