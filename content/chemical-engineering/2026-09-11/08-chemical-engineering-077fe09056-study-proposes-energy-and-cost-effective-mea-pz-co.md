A study in Chemical Engineering Science reports an advanced design, optimisation and analysis of CO2 capture using a monoethanolamine and piperazine solvent blend in coal-fired power plants. The work targets energy- and cost-effective operation. No numerical results are given in the available text.

For process engineers, the interest is in reducing the energy penalty and cost of post-combustion capture. MEA/PZ blends are already used in absorption-stripping systems, and design choices such as solvent concentration, intercooling and stripper configuration can shift performance. The paper's contribution is the optimisation approach, but the abstract provides no specifics.
