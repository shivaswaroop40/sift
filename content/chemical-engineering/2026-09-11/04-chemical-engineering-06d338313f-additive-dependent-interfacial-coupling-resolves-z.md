A study in Chemical Engineering Science reports a way to resolve the compatibility–performance tradeoff in ZIF-8/PIM-1 mixed-matrix membranes for propylene/propane separation. The approach relies on additive-dependent interfacial coupling, meaning the interface between the ZIF-8 filler and the PIM-1 matrix is tuned by adding specific additives.

Mixed-matrix membranes often face a tradeoff: good particle-polymer compatibility can come at the cost of separation performance, or vice versa. If the additive route works, it would let process engineers tailor membrane interfaces without sacrificing selectivity or permeability. Propylene/propane separation is energy-intensive, so better membranes could cut distillation load.

The source is the paper title only. No permeation data, additive identities or comparison with existing membranes are given in the abstract. The claim is that the tradeoff is resolved, but the magnitude and practical conditions remain unspecified.
