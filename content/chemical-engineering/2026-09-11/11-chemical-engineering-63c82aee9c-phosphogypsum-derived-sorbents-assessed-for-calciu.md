A study in Chemical Engineering Journal reports on phosphogypsum-derived sorbents for calcium looping CO2 capture. The work covers cyclic performance, reaction kinetics, and a techno-economic assessment of the sorbents.

Calcium looping is a post-combustion capture route that relies on cheap, durable sorbents. Phosphogypsum is a waste by-product from fertiliser production, so using it as a precursor could cut sorbent costs and address waste disposal. The title gives no numerical results, so the actual performance and cost figures are not yet available from this summary.
