The paper describes a phase-wise parameterization approach for mechanistic fermentation models. It uses industrial-scale data to link growth, substrate utilization, and product formation. The method fits model parameters in stages rather than all at once, which is intended to improve accuracy and identifiability under industrial conditions.

This matters for process engineers because fermentation models are often fitted to lab data and fail at industrial scale. Phase-wise fitting can make models more reliable for design and operations, and can help identify which parameters are best constrained by which data.

The title gives no quantitative results, so the claims are limited to the proposed methodology. No specific fermentation system or product is named. The paper appears in Chemical Engineering Research and Design. The findings are a methodological description rather than a validated outcome.
