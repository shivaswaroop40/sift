A new article in Chemical Engineering Research and Design addresses reactive operability limits in stress-driven viscoelastic channel flow. The title names three constraints: thermal runaway, cooperative erosion, and a cold-state admissibility ceiling. The source text is only the title, so no results or methods are available.

For a practitioner, the title indicates that safe operation of such flows depends on avoiding both high-temperature runaway and erosion, while also respecting a minimum cold-state condition.
