A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal describes an AI-driven method for identifying novel working fluids. Its title frames a pipeline that runs from molecular generation to process-relevant performance. On that reading, candidate molecules are generated computationally and then scored on measures that matter at process scale, not on molecular properties alone. The stated outcome is novel working fluid candidates.

Working fluid choice sets cycle efficiency, operating pressures and heat exchanger sizing in power and refrigeration duty. Generative molecular tools can propose very large candidate lists, but ranking those candidates against process metrics is the hard step. A method that carries generated molecules through to process-relevant scores would give designers a wider, better-screened pool than property tables or manual shortlisting provide.
