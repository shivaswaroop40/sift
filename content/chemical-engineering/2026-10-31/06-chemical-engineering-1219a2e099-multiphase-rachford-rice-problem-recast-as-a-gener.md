A paper in Chemical Engineering Science reformulates the multiphase Rachford-Rice problem as a generalized eigenvalue problem. The formulation is then applied to flash calculations built on equations of state. The available source is the title alone, so no results, benchmarks or performance claims can be reported here.

Rachford-Rice equations set the phase amounts and compositions in every flash calculation inside a flowsheet simulator. Multiphase cases, such as three-phase vapour-liquid-liquid splits, are where standard iterative solvers struggle, with negative phase amounts and multiple roots. Recasting the problem as an eigenvalue problem suggests a different mathematical route to those splits. Whether it improves convergence or reliability is not stated in the source.
