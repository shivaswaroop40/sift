Environmental campaigners have filed a legal claim against the GB Health and Safety Executive (HSE). The claim targets a new law that provides fast-track market approval for chemicals. The report is brief. It does not name the campaign groups, identify the legislation, or set out the legal grounds for the challenge.

The case matters to anyone placing chemicals on the GB market. A ruling against the HSE could change or delay the fast-track route. That would affect approval timelines and compliance planning for new products. Until the court decides, the route carries legal uncertainty.
