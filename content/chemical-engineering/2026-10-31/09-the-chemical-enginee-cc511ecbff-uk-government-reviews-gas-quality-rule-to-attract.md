The UK government is reviewing a legal gas quality requirement. The stated aim is to improve the country's attractiveness to LNG exporters abroad. A report has confirmed the review. The requirement sits in law. No scope, timetable or preferred outcome has been set out in the confirmation.

Gas quality requirements decide which LNG cargoes UK terminals can accept and how imported gas enters the network. A review aimed at attracting exporters points toward widening what can be accepted. That carries the familiar trade-off between supply flexibility and the gas quality delivered to end users. Terminal and network engineers would feel any change.
