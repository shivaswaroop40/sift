Topsoe has licensed its kerosene hydrotreating technology to BPCL for a revamp of the Mumbai Refinery's 4,000-metric-ton-per-day hydrotreater. The unit will co-process 5% used cooking oil with conventional feedstock to produce sustainable aviation fuel without building new infrastructure. Commissioning is expected by the end of 2026.

For refiners, this approach offers a practical route into SAF production using existing equipment and flexible feedstock blending. India has committed to SAF blending targets of 1% by 2027, 2% by 2028 and 5% by 2030 to meet CORSIA compliance. Co-processing allows operators to adjust renewable and conventional feedstock ratios as supply and economics shift.
