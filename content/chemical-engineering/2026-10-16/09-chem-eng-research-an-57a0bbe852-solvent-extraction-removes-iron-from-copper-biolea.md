Researchers used di(2-ethylhexyl)phosphoric acid and tributyl phosphate to extract iron from copper bioleach solutions. The method selectively removes iron while leaving copper in solution, avoiding the solid residue produced by gypsum precipitation.

For copper operations, this matters because gypsum precipitation generates large volumes of waste requiring disposal. Solvent extraction offers a residue-free path, though the choice between methods depends on capital cost, reagent recovery, and site constraints.
