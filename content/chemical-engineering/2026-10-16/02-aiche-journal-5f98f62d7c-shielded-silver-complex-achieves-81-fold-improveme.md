Researchers immobilized silver ions in ethylene glycol to create a selective extractant for olefin-alkane separation. The hydrogen bonding network of ethylene glycol blocks nonpolar alkanes while retaining silver's π-complexation with olefinic bonds. The hexene/hexane performance index reached 81, one to two orders of magnitude above conventional solvents.

This matters because olefin-alkane separation is industrially significant but difficult with standard silver-based extraction. Single-stage enrichment of Fischer-Tropsch oils achieved over 98 wt.% olefins with good solvent recyclability. A coupled extraction-distillation process yielded 98% olefin and 99.9% alkane products while reducing energy consumption by 84.7%.
