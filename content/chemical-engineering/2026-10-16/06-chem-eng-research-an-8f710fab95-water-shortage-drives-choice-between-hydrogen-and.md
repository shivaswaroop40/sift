Researchers optimized an integrated system pairing high-temperature steam electrolysis (HTSE) with solar desalination for green hydrogen production. The study used techno-economic modelling to find the best balance when water availability is limited, accounting for capital and operating costs across both processes.

A practising chemical engineer designing hydrogen plants in water-stressed regions will need to choose between prioritizing hydrogen output or fresh water production. This work quantifies that trade-off and shows how it shifts with capital constraints, solar resource, and local water scarcity. The results inform process feasibility before detailed design begins.
