MOL Group has completed reconstruction of the AV3 crude oil distillation unit at its Százhalombatta Danube Refinery, which was damaged by a fire in October 2025. The 18 billion forint project included demolition and rebuilding of the reinforced concrete structure, installation of 27 new high-capacity pumps, replacement of 8 km of piping, and laying of 100 km of electrical cable.

The work finished on schedule. Next steps are flushing, pressure testing, functional tests of process control and safety systems, and connection of auxiliary power supplies, with crude oil introduction to follow. Production will resume step by step through October, returning the refinery to full capacity.

For practitioners, the scale of the rebuild is notable: a single atmospheric distillation unit rebuild touched concrete structures, rotating equipment, piping, cabling and control systems in one coordinated outage. MOL also flagged the regional fuel supply context, citing competition for alternative crude sources linked to the Iran conflict and broader energy market disruption.

The article provides only MOL's own statements. Independent confirmation of restart progress or capacity uplift is not yet available.
