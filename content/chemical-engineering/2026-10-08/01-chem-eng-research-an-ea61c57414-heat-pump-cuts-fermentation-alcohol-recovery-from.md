Researchers redesigned a three-alcohol separation from fermentation broth, replacing seven distillation columns with two dividing-wall columns (DWCs) and adding a heat pump. The work moved from conceptual flowsheet to energy-optimized design.

Fermentation recovery consumes large energy for distillation. Dividing-wall columns reduce capital and duty by combining separations; heat pump integration recovers waste heat. For a practitioner, this shows a concrete route to lower operating cost and smaller footprint on existing multicomponent alcohol separations.

The study optimizes for both equipment size and energy input, addressing the practical constraint that column choice affects both capital and running expense.
