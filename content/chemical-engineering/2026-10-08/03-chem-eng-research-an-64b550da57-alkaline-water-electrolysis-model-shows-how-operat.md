Researchers developed a dynamic model of industrial alkaline water electrolysis to predict how changes in cell voltage, current density, and operating pressure affect hydrogen and oxygen purity and overall energy consumption.

For process engineers, this matters because hydrogen purity directly impacts downstream storage and use, while energy efficiency determines operating cost. The model lets you explore trade-offs between purity and efficiency without trial-and-error on pilot kit.

The paper is computational; it does not report experimental validation or industrial trial results yet, so implementation on existing cells requires verification against your own conditions.
