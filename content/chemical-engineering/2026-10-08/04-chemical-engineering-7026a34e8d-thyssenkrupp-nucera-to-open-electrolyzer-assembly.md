Thyssenkrupp nucera is building a 7,500-square-meter central assembly hub near Porto, Portugal, to manufacture alkaline electrolyzer cells. Construction starts winter 2026, with production commencing 2028. The company is investing €10 million to €14 million, supported by €3.7 million Portuguese government funding.

Centralised, semi-automated assembly increases capacity and reduces per-cell build times compared to decentralised customer-operated workshops. Customers no longer need their own assembly infrastructure, lowering planning and resource burdens. Ready-to-install cell packages also reduce complexity and downtime at project sites.

The hub serves European electrolysis projects and will initially supply cells for Moeve's Onuba project in Spain. Consolidated assembly with parallel processing enables faster throughput and better service support for ongoing operations and cell replacement.
