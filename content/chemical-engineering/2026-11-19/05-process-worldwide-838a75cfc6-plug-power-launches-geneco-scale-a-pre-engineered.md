Plug Power has launched Geneco Scale, an integrated PEM electrolyzer plant for projects of about 100 MW and above. It is built from standardised 20 MW blocks with balance-of-plant included, in an outdoor-ready, factory-tested modular layout. Arcadia eFuels is the first customer, for the 280 MW Project Arc in Texas, where Plug has finished the technical evaluation and FEED study.

The aim is to move integration work from the EPC contractor to the OEM. Plug says a mature FEED-level reference design cuts project-specific engineering and supplier interfaces. Process skids and cooling equipment are stacked over several levels, targeting under one acre per 100 MW. Plug would act as the single responsible OEM for the whole plant.

Plug also says the design follows oil-and-gas process-safety standards and reduces on-site hydrogen inventory. No capex figures, efficiency numbers or delivery schedules are given, so the cost and safety claims are unquantified. Project Arc is meant to make about 23.2 million gallons a year of e-SAF. The company cites more than 320 MW of PEM capacity shipped to date.
