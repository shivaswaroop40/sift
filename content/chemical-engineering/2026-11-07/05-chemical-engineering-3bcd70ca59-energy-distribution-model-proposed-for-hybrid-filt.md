A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal presents an energy distribution analysis model for a hybrid filtration bed gasification system. The application is co-gasification of coal with biomass waste to produce syngas. The title indicates the model accounts for how energy is distributed through a system that combines gasification with a filtration bed.

Energy distribution analysis shows where heat and work end up in a gasification plant. That information drives heat recovery design and loss reduction. Co-gasification replaces part of the coal feed with biomass waste, which shifts the energy balance with the blend ratio. A model covering the filtration bed as well as the gasifier would support feed and design decisions.
