A paper in Chemical Engineering Research and Design presents an integrated reactor modelling framework for light naphtha isomerization. The framework couples reaction kinetics, chemical equilibrium and phase equilibrium within a single model. The stated aim is to represent the reactor more completely than approaches that treat these phenomena separately.

For engineers who model or operate isomerization units, the coupling is the practical interest. Kinetic models alone can give misleading conversion predictions when equilibrium limits or vapour-liquid behaviour shift with temperature and pressure. A framework that solves all three together could give more dependable predictions of reactor performance across operating conditions and feed changes.
