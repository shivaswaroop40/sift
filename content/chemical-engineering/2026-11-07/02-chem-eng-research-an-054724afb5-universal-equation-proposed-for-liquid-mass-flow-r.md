A paper in Chemical Engineering Research and Design proposes a universal equation for liquid mass flow rate in airlift pumps. The title indicates the equation is meant to hold across flow regimes, rather than requiring a separate correlation for each regime. The available text is the title alone, so the derivation, the range of conditions tested and the accuracy claimed cannot be reported here.

For practitioners, the appeal is straightforward. Airlift pump design usually rests on correlations tied to a particular gas-liquid flow regime, so the designer must first establish which regime applies. One equation covering all regimes would remove that step and simplify sizing and rating work. Treat this as an alert to the paper rather than a verdict, since no results appear in the source.
