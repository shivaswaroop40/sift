A review in Chemical Engineering Science surveys nickel-based catalysts for the selective semi-hydrogenation of alkynes and alkadienes, a key step in purifying olefin streams.

For chemical engineers, the work consolidates catalyst design strategies and selectivity controls relevant to polymer-grade ethylene and propylene production, where acetylene and diene impurities must be removed without over-hydrogenation to the paraffin.

The review covers nickel's lower cost versus palladium, alongside performance trade-offs in selectivity and operating conditions. It is presented as a review article, so primary experimental data are not included.
