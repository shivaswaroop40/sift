The work evaluates pelletised UTSA-16, a zirconium-based metal-organic framework also marketed as ProMOF 9100, for biogas upgrading using vacuum swing adsorption.

UTSA-16 has previously shown selectivity for CO2 over CH4 in powder form. Pelletisation is needed for practical adsorber beds but can reduce capacity and kinetics, so the paper reports how shaped pellets perform under repeated vacuum-pressure cycles.

The source provided is a title only, with no abstract details on working capacity, purity, recovery or cycle conditions.
