The paper presents a data-driven framework that corrects errors in coarse-mesh CFD simulations of a mechanical flotation cell and predicts flow fields rapidly. It addresses the trade-off between computational speed and accuracy that has long limited CFD use in flotation modelling.

For process engineers, the approach could enable faster evaluation of flotation cell designs and operating conditions without running full high-resolution simulations. This matters when screening many configurations or integrating flow-field predictions into larger process models.

Only the title is available, so the specific machine learning method, training data, and reported accuracy gains cannot be confirmed. Readers should check the full paper for details on generalisability across cell sizes and operating regimes.
