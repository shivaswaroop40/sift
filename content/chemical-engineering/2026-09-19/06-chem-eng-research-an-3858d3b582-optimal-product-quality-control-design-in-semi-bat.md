A study in Chemical Engineering Research and Design proposes a control design method for semi-batch reactive crystallization based on an input authority profile. It targets optimal product quality control in this class of batch processes.

Reactive crystallization is central to producing many solid-form chemicals and pharmaceuticals, where crystal size distribution and purity drive downstream performance. The input authority profile approach matters because it gives engineers a structured way to tune manipulated variable ranges for quality objectives rather than relying on heuristic set-point tracking.

The paper focuses on design methodology for batch operation rather than a specific industrial case study, so direct plant-level performance numbers are not reported.
