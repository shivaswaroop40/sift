The paper presents a cycle design and surrogate-based multi-objective optimisation for magnetic induction swing adsorption applied to post-combustion CO2 capture. It targets electrified operation, replacing thermal swing with induction heating to regenerate the sorbent.

The work matters because induction heating can shorten cycle times and improve energy targeting relative to conventional temperature swing adsorption, which dominates today's capture cost. The surrogate-based approach lets designers explore trade-offs between energy use and productivity without running full process models each iteration.

Specific performance figures are not reported in the available text, so the practical separation energy and recovery remain unstated. Readers will need the full paper for cycle conditions, sorbent choice and the Pareto front details.
