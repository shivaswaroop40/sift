Researchers applied a machine learning-assisted timing regulation strategy to gas blowing refining of silicon melt, aiming to remove titanium more selectively. The work targets titanium rather than relying on bulk impurity removal across the refining window.

For solar-grade and electronic-grade silicon producers, cutting titanium early or at the right point could reduce slag use, shorten refining cycles and lower energy demand per batch. The timing-based approach suggests process control rather than larger reagent inputs as the lever.

The source is an abstract, so reported removals, melt compositions and model inputs are not shown here. A practitioner would need the full paper to judge whether the timing gains justify the added sensor and control infrastructure on a production line.
