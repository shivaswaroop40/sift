The work derives a quantitative link between cyclic voltammetry (CV) and electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) models for TiO2 film electrodes, allowing parameters from one technique to predict responses in the other.

For practitioners, this means CV and EIS data on the same TiO2 film can be interpreted through a single equivalent circuit, reducing duplicated characterisation effort and helping reconcile kinetic and capacitive information from the two methods.

The paper presents the mathematical derivation and compares fitted parameters against experimental CV and EIS curves. The exact goodness of fit and parameter ranges are not given in the available text, so the strength of the correlation on real electrodes should be checked in the full article.

The study is limited to TiO2 film electrodes, so transferability to other oxide systems or thicker layers is not established.
