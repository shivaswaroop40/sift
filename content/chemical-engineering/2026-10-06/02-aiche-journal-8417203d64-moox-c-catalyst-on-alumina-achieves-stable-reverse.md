Researchers made MoOx-C/Al2O3 catalysts using dielectric barrier discharge plasma. The 1:1 composition reached 251 mmol CO per gram catalyst per hour with less than 1% deactivation over 100 hours at 600°C, without requiring pretreatment.

RWGS catalysts must balance CO2 activation, H2 activation, and rapid CO desorption. The Mo-C/Ov interface achieves this balance, making it attractive for process design where catalyst stability and activity directly affect operating costs and turnover.

Isotope labeling showed dynamic carbon exchange between interfacial carbon and reactants drives CO formation. This mechanism suggests a design rule for future RWGS catalysts: stable Mo-C interfaces with oxygen vacancies support long-term operation.
