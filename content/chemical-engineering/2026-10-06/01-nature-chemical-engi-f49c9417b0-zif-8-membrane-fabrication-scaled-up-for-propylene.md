Researchers have developed a micro-space transformation process for making zeolitic imidazolate framework membranes that separates propylene from propane. The work includes the technical and scale-up data needed to move the technology from laboratory to industrial production.

Propylene and propane separation is capital-intensive in refining and petrochemistry. Metal-organic framework membranes offer a lower-energy alternative to distillation, but lab demonstrations have not yielded the engineering detail for deployment. This study provides the missing scale-up pathway.

ZIF-8 membranes have shown separation potential in controlled conditions. The new fabrication process and accompanying data address the practical barriers to manufacturing at commercial scale.
