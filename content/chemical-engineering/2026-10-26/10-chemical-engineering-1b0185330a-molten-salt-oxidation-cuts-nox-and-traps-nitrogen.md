Molten salt oxidation of waste anion exchange resin reduces NOx emissions and immobilizes nitrogen in the solid residue. The study identified mechanisms for nitrogen retention during thermal treatment at high temperature in a molten salt medium.

For process design and waste treatment, this matters because ion exchange resins are widely used in water treatment and chemical processing. Managing both NOx air emissions and solid nitrogen content is a regulatory and operational concern in thermal disposal routes.

The paper offers mechanistic detail on how nitrogen behaves in molten salt conditions, which guides selection between oxidation temperatures, salt chemistry, and post-treatment of ash.
