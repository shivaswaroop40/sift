Researchers coupled reaction kinetics, thermodynamics, and population balance equations to model oiling out in an industrial reactive crystallization process. The model identified the root cause and tested remedies using plant data.

Oiling out—where an intermediate liquid phase separates instead of crystallizing—damages product quality and throughput. Practitioners designing or operating reactive crystallizers need predictive tools to prevent this costly defect during scale-up or process changes.

The coupled modelling approach allows engineers to diagnose oiling out without expensive trial-and-error trials, and to evaluate fixes by simulating changes to temperature, feed rate, or reactant proportions before implementation.
