A team has demonstrated CO₂ capture from ammonia condensate streams at industrial scale, integrated into a Solvay soda ash production facility. The work moves the concept from laboratory validation through to operational validation at a scale relevant to commercial deployment.

For process engineers, this addresses an industrial CO₂ stream that is often vented. Integrating capture into existing soda ash plants avoids new infrastructure while recovering a byproduct. The integration directly impacts energy balance and operating costs of the host process.

The study examines the energy requirements and practical constraints of scaling up the capture step alongside the established Solvay process, identifying the technical hurdles between bench demonstration and full industrial operation.
