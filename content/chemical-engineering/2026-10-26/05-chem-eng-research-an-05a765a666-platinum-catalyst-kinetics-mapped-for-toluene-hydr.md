Researchers studied the kinetics of toluene hydrogenation over a platinum-alumina catalyst. This reaction converts toluene to methylcyclohexane, a step in the liquid organic hydrogen carrier cycle used for hydrogen storage and transport.

For process design and scale-up, understanding the rate equations and activation energy is essential. The work provides kinetic parameters needed to model reactors and optimise conditions for the hydrogenation stage of LOHC systems.
