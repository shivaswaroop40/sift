A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal proposes recovering qualified CH4 from low-grade unconventional gas by vacuum swing adsorption (VSA) coupled with a secondary post-adsorption step. The title claims the route avoids methane slip. The source text gives only the title, so no data are available.

Methane slip is a recurring problem when upgrading dilute streams such as coal-bed or similar unconventional gas. Single-stage VSA typically has to trade product purity against recovery, and lost methane is both wasted product and a greenhouse gas emission. A second adsorption stage aimed at that loss would address a real design constraint.

The source does not give the adsorbent, feed composition, purity, recovery figures, energy use or whether the work is experimental or simulated. The claim of slip-free recovery cannot be assessed from the title alone, and the full paper is needed to judge it.
