The Department for Energy Security and Net Zero will change how the next renewables auction works. Developers will place project bids without knowing how much funding is available. The department says the aim is to bring down consumer energy costs.

The source text gives no further detail. It does not say which auction round is affected, how the budget will be set, or when the change takes effect. It also gives no estimate of the savings expected.

For developers and project engineers, the change alters bidding strategy. Without a known budget ceiling, bidders cannot tune prices to the money on offer. Whether this lowers strike prices or deters marginal projects is not addressed in the text available.
