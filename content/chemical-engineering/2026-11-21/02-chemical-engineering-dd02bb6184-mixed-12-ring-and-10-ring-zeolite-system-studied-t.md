A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal describes a mixed zeolite system that combines 12-membered-ring (12MR) and 10-membered-ring (10MR) zeolites. The stated aim is to steer the product distribution of catalytic cracking toward ethylene and propylene.

The source text gives only the title. No yields, selectivities, feedstock, temperatures or catalyst names are available, so the size of any shift cannot be stated.

The title points to a familiar design idea. Larger 12MR pores can handle bulky feed molecules, while 10MR pores favour light olefins. Whether the paper uses that division of labour, and how it compares with a single-zeolite catalyst, is not stated in the text provided.
