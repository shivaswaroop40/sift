Researchers applied isothermal-clamped flash Joule heating to flame-retardant electronics waste plastics. The process creates a uniform high-temperature zone in seconds at ambient pressure. It recovered high-purity antimony and bromine as hydrogen bromide whilst suppressing toxic brominated emissions.

The method matters because antimony is a valuable commodity chemical and bromine recovery adds further economic return. Conventional e-waste treatment releases toxic brominated compounds. This approach handles both the valuable metal and the hazardous halogen in one step.
