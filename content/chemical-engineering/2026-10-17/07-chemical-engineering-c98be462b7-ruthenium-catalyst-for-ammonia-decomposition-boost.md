Researchers mixed ruthenium catalyst with zeolite-templated carbon (ZTC) to improve ammonia decomposition. The carbon phase enhanced hydrogen spillover, a process where hydrogen atoms migrate from the metal catalyst to the support material, improving overall conversion rates and catalyst activity.

For process design, ammonia decomposition is central to hydrogen production from a renewable feedstock. Better catalysts reduce energy demand and capital cost. Hydrogen spillover mechanisms offer a tuning lever for separation and reaction engineering.

The improvement came from physical mixing rather than chemical synthesis, suggesting simpler manufacturing routes. The study claims the ZTC support material provides high surface area and favourable pore structure for hydrogen migration.
