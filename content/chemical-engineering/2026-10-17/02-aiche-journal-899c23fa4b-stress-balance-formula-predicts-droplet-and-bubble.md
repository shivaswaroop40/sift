A new critical Weber number formula, Wec=1+Ca, has been validated for predicting when droplets and bubbles break apart in turbulent flow. The formula combines interfacial stress and viscous forces in a three-way balance, making the breakup threshold scale linearly with capillary number. It works for viscosity ratios up to at least 100.

For process design, this matters because emulsification and atomization depend on controlling droplet and bubble size. The formula removes the need for regime-specific empirical corrections by linking breakup directly to fluid properties. A single model now covers both low-viscosity and high-viscosity systems without switching between approaches.

The validation covers both experimental data and simulations across the viscosity range, though the authors state the range tested extends to viscosity ratio 100 and note applicability to moderate internal viscosity.
