Topsoe and Sasol have signed a licence and engineering agreement with Alby PtX AB for a planned e-SAF facility in Alby, Sweden. They will supply their integrated gas-to-liquid package, G2L. It combines Topsoe's eREACT synthesis gas technology and hydroprocessing with Sasol's low-temperature Fischer-Tropsch (LTFT) process.

The plant is designed to convert renewable hydrogen and captured carbon into aviation fuel. eREACT makes the synthesis gas. LTFT and hydroprocessing then upgrade the synthetic hydrocarbons. The source gives a capacity of about 66,000 metric tons of e-SAF a year.

The deal follows similar agreements between the two companies and HDF Energy, Bharat Petroleum and Allied Biofuels. For process engineers, it shows a recurring route to e-SAF: electrolytic hydrogen and captured CO2, then Fischer-Tropsch, then upgrading. The agreement covers licensing and engineering. The source does not state a final investment decision, a start-up date or a cost, so the project remains at a planning stage.
