A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal applies bipolar membrane electrodialysis to ocean alkalinity enhancement. It examines the feasibility of alternative process configurations and the pathways for CO2 injection. The source text provides only the title, so no results, performance figures or conclusions can be reported here.

The topic sits where electrochemical separations meet marine carbon dioxide removal. Bipolar membrane electrodialysis generates acid and base streams from salt solutions using bipolar membranes. Ocean alkalinity enhancement aims to raise seawater alkalinity so the ocean takes up more CO2. Configuration choice and CO2 handling are the practical questions that govern energy use and cost at scale.
