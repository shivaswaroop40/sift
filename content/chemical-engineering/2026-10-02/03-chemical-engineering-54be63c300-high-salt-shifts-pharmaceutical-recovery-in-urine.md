Researchers found that increasing salt concentration in urine switches the capture mechanism for pharmaceutical compounds from chemisorption to hydrophobic partitioning. This mechanistic shift enables selective recovery of target drugs from the complex matrix.

For process design, this matters because salt-driven partitioning offers a path to recover pharmaceuticals and nutrients from urine without modifying sorbent chemistry. The transition happens at saline levels relevant to real wastewater, making the approach practical for urine resource recovery systems.
