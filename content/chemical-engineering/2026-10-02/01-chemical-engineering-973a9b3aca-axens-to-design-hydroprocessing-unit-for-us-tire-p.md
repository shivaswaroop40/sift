Axens has been selected to provide the process design package for a hydroprocessing unit that will process 100% tire pyrolysis oil at a US facility. The unit will produce low-sulfur fuel with a maximum sulfur content of 500 ppm. The project is in FEED phase, with final investment decision expected in 2027.

The selection reflects Axens' experience across more than 300 hydroprocessing references and proven capability in upgrading alternative feedstocks. The company has particular expertise in managing impurities from tire pyrolysis oil, a key challenge in this conversion pathway.

For process engineers, this represents a concrete application of hydroprocessing to circular-economy feedstocks. Tire pyrolysis oil contains heteroatoms and unstable compounds that require tailored catalyst selection and reactor design, making the technical choices in this design package relevant to similar waste-to-fuel projects.
