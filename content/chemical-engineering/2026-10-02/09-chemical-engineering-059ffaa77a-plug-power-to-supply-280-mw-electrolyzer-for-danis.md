Plug Power has signed a supply agreement to deliver 280 MW of GenEco electrolyzers to Arcadia eFuels for Project ENDOR in Denmark. The facility will produce roughly 110 tons of renewable hydrogen daily, which Arcadia will combine with captured CO2 to make synthetic aviation fuel for standard aircraft.

For process engineers, this represents a major scaling of electrolysis coupled to e-fuels production under the EU's ReFuelEU mandate requiring rising shares of sustainable aviation fuel at European airports. The agreement signals commercial viability of large-scale hydrogen-from-power-to-fuel pathways.

The deal includes a broader strategic partnership positioning Plug as preferred supplier for four additional Arcadia projects totalling over 1 GW of electrolyzer capacity across Europe and the Americas, indicating sustained growth in this segment as aviation decarbonisation mandates take effect.
