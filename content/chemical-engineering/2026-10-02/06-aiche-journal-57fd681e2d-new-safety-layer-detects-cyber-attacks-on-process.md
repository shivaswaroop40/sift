Researchers developed a framework that adds protection to chemical process control systems by running digital twins of the controller and process in parallel. The system compares trusted measurements against predicted behaviour and classifies risk as Safe, Possible Critical, or Safety Critical using safety margin metrics. Tests on a stirred-tank reactor and heater showed it detects attacks that falsify data or alter control actions, then either mitigates or triggers safe shutdown.

For process engineers running critical operations, cyber attacks that compromise sensors or controllers can push plants toward dangerous states without obvious warning. This approach creates an independent safety layer that works with existing systems, offering detection and automatic shutdown without redesigning core control logic.
