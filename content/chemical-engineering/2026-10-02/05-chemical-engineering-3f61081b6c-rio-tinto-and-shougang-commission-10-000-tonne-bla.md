Rio Tinto and Shougang have commissioned an industrial-scale carbon capture facility at Shougang's Jingtang base in China. The facility processes up to 3,000 cubic metres of blast furnace gas per hour and captures 10,000 tonnes of CO₂ annually. It follows a smaller pilot facility commissioned in 2024 as part of a phased development programme.

For steel producers, the milestone shows carbon capture on blast furnace gas is moving from bench to production scale. Using waste heat from existing operations could lower capture costs and support wider deployment across mills. Rio Tinto and Shougang are exploring ways to convert captured CO₂ into syngas for recycling back into steelmaking, reducing fresh carbon demand.
