Researchers used average metal-oxygen bond energy as a single descriptor to predict perovskite cathode performance in CO2 electrolysis. The approach reconciles the known trade-off between activity and stability that constrains cathode design. The study appears in Chemical Engineering Journal.

For process engineers designing electrochemical reactors, this descriptor offers a quantitative tool to screen perovskite compositions without building each one. The ability to balance activity against durability is central to scaling CO2 electrolysis beyond lab demonstrations.
