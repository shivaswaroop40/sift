Ineos has halted production at two acetic acid facilities in Humberside and will idle a third within days, citing gas prices it described as ridiculously high. The shutdown comes despite a £30m upgrade completed at the sites last year.

The move removes a significant share of UK acetic acid capacity at short notice, affecting downstream users in coatings, adhesives and pharmaceutical intermediates. It signals that high feedstock costs are now forcing operational decisions even at recently invested plants.

Engineers at the sites were described as distressed by the sudden closure. The episode highlights how exposure to European gas markets can override the benefits of capital investment in energy-intensive petrochemicals.

Details on restart timing and any impact on supply contracts were not given in the announcement.
