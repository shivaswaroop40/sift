Ina has started up a new Delayed Coker Unit at its Rijeka Refinery in Croatia, feeding feedstock on 1 September 2026 and reaching about 70% of design capacity with all key products on spec.

The DCU lets the refinery process heavy residues into higher-value products, raising diesel yield from the same crude by up to 30% and removing the need to import vacuum gas oil. It also widens crude flexibility for heavier grades.

The Rijeka Refinery Upgrade Project cost nearly €700 million, part of €1.3 billion spent by Ina and MOL Group over 12 years on refining and logistics. Stable operation is targeted by the end of 2026, followed by final performance tests.
