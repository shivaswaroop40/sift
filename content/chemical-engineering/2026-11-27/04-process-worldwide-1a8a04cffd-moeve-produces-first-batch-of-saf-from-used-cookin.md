Moeve has produced a first batch of sustainable aviation fuel from used cooking oil at its San Roque Energy Park in Spain. The company says this shows the site can take a new renewable feedstock into existing industrial assets. It plans to keep producing SAF at the site after the first batch.

To do this, San Roque adapted its facilities to receive and process the oil. The team checked that the unit could process it correctly. It also changed its quality controls, analysis and traceability systems to track the renewable component from entry to the final SAF.

The release is a press statement and gives no detail on volumes, process route, yields or unit configuration. It does not say which existing units were used or what modifications were needed. The claim of up to 90 % lifecycle CO2 reduction is a general figure for such fuels, not a result for this batch.
