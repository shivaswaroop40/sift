Nextchem, part of Maire, has signed a binding agreement through Nextchem USA to acquire Chart Industries' mid-scale IPSMR and small-scale LNG process technologies for 220 million dollars in cash. Chart is a wholly owned subsidiary of Baker Hughes. The deal covers IPSMR, IPSMR+, early-stage IPDMR, over 90 patents, know-how, trademarks and a team of 12.

IPSMR is a pre-cooled single mixed-refrigerant process for 0.5 to 2 MTPA per train, with a sweet spot of 1.4 to 2 MTPA. IPDMR is meant to reach roughly 2 to over 4 MTPA. Chart and Nextchem cite high thermodynamic efficiency and a compact layout, suited to modular multi-train and floating LNG. These are company claims, not independent figures.

The reference base is sizeable. IPSMR has about 14 MTPA of operating references and about 57 MTPA under construction, of which 30 MTPA is residual backlog. About 90 MTPA of pre-FID projects have selected it. A 10-year cold-box supply agreement with Chart supports IPSMR projects.

The move takes Nextchem from gas treatment and purification into LNG liquefaction licensing. Maire expects to engage clients earlier in project development and then offer engineering, procurement and execution. The pre-FID volume is only an opportunity set, as those projects may not proceed.
