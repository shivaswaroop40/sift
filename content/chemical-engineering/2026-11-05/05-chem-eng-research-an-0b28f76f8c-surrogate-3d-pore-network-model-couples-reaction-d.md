Chemical Engineering Research and Design has published a paper on surrogate three-dimensional pore-network modelling of a spherical porous catalyst particle. The model couples intraparticle reaction and diffusion with heat transfer. Only the title was available at the time of writing, so no results or numbers can be reported here.

Pore-network models represent pellet structure directly rather than averaging it into an effective diffusivity. The heat-transfer coupling matters most for exothermic reactions, where internal temperature gradients change rates and selectivity. The surrogate label suggests the authors aim to cut the cost of full three-dimensional network solutions, which are costly to run inside reactor-scale models.
