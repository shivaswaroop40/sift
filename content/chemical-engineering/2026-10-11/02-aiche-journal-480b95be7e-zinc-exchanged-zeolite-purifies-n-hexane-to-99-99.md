Researchers developed a zinc-doped LTA zeolite (0.05Zn-LTA) with hierarchical pores that selectively adsorbs n-hexane while rejecting branched and cyclic isomers. The material shows n-hexane diffusivity two orders of magnitude higher than standard LTA, with uptake of 155 mg/g at 298 K and stable performance over cycles.

For electronics applications, n-hexane must exceed 99.99% purity, a separation difficult with conventional methods. The inverse adsorption strategy targets bulk isomers instead, leaving pure n-hexane. A four-stage pressure swing process upgraded feed purity from 99.1% to 99.9929% with 96% recovery and 1.05 mol/kg/h productivity.
