Researchers studied the decomposition of ammonia over nickel and cobalt catalysts to produce hydrogen. They developed kinetic models that directly informed reactor design choices, linking catalytic performance to equipment scale and configuration.

Ammonia decomposition offers a hydrogen source that sidesteps steam reforming of methane. Understanding reaction kinetics on these metals is essential for scaling cracking units in existing or new infrastructure.

The work shows how rigorous kinetic analysis can reduce design uncertainty when moving from catalyst screening to equipment specification.
