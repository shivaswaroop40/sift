Claros Technologies and Daikin America have agreed to install a full-scale ClarosTechUV system at Daikin's Decatur, Alabama facility. The technology destroys per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances on-site during normal manufacturing operations, targeting long-chain, short-chain and ultra-short fluorine chemistries including trifluoroacetic acid.

For process engineers, this represents a shift from managing PFAS waste streams to permanent destruction at source. The integrated system combines UV-based destruction with continuous analytical testing, allowing manufacturers to meet strengthening global PFAS regulations without off-site transport.

The installation follows a multi-phase collaboration, from pilot trials through commercial optimisation. Daikin and Claros have established a long-term strategic relationship that may extend the technology across Daikin's global operations.
