Researchers engineered a catalyst combining palladium and MXene on a gallium-zinc-zirconium oxide support for CO2 hydrogenation to methanol. The Pd-MXene interface creates a hydrogen-supply pathway that enhances reaction rates and selectivity.

The approach addresses a practical bottleneck in CO2 utilisation. Controlling hydrogen transfer at catalyst interfaces improves atom efficiency and reduces unwanted side reactions, both critical for cost-competitive methanol synthesis at scale.
