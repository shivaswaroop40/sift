Researchers crystallized nickel sulfate and lithium sulfate directly from acidic leachate of recycled lithium-ion batteries, avoiding solvent extraction steps. The method produces battery-grade materials in a single stage from the dissolved cathode material.

For process engineers, this simplifies recovery flowsheets and cuts unit operations. Solvent extraction is capital and chemical intensive; direct crystallization reduces both if recovery rates are acceptable. The approach applies to the sulfuric acid leaching pathway common in industry.

Recovery yields and impurity levels relative to conventional solvent-extraction routes are not stated in the abstract, limiting assessment of whether the method trades unit simplicity for product purity or throughput.
