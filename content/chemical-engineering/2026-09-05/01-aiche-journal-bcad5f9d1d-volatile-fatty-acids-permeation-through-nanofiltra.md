This work presents a comprehensive experimental and modeling framework to describe and predict the volatile fatty acids (VFA) permeation in commercial polymeric nanofiltration (NF) and reverse osmosis (RO) membranes. The permeation behavior was evaluated using single- and multicomponent VFA solutions under varying feed concentration, pH, and transmembrane pressure.
