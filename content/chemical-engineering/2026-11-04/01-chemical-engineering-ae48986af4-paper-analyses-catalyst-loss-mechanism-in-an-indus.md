Chemical Engineering Journal has published an analysis of how catalyst is lost from the regenerator of an industrial catalytic cracking unit. The available source text gives only the title. No abstract, method or results were provided, so the mechanism the authors identify and the size of the unit cannot be reported here.

Catalyst loss from regenerators is a standing operational cost in catalytic cracking. It drains expensive inventory, raises particulate emissions and often signals cyclone wear or attrition upstream. A mechanism study on a working unit would let operators distinguish collection failure from particle breakage and target the right fix.
