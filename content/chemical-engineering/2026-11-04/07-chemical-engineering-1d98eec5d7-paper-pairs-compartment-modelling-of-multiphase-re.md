A paper in Chemical Engineering Science addresses compartment modelling of multiphase reactors using unsupervised clustering. The available source gives only the title. The reactor systems and the clustering algorithm are not stated, nor is any validation against experiment or CFD, so no results can be reported here. The title points to a methods paper joining reduced-order reactor models with machine learning.

Compartment models replace the full flow field with a small set of zones, which makes dynamic reactor simulation affordable where multiphase CFD is not. That matters for scale-up studies and control design. The hard part is deciding where to draw the zones, and that choice usually rests on modeller judgement. Unsupervised clustering offers a data-driven route to it. The full text is needed to judge how well it works here.
