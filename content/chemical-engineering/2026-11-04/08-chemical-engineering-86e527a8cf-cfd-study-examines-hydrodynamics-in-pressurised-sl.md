Chemical Engineering Science has published a computational fluid dynamics study of hydrodynamics in a pressurised slurry bubble column reactor. Only the title was available to this digest, so no methods, results or operating conditions can be reported. The stated scope is the flow behaviour of a gas, liquid and solid system under pressure, modelled with CFD.

Hydrodynamics govern gas holdup, mixing and mass transfer in slurry bubble columns, and pressure changes bubble size and flow regime. Those effects feed directly into reactor sizing, heat removal and scale-up. A CFD treatment of a pressurised column is therefore relevant to design and operations work, but the specific findings of this paper cannot be judged from the title alone.
