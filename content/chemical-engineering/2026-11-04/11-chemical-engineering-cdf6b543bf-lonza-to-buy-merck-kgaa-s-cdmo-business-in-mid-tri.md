Merck KGaA has signed a definitive agreement to sell the contract development and manufacturing organization business in its Life Science sector to Lonza Group of Basel. The price is a mid-triple-digit million-euro amount. Closing is expected in mid-2027, subject to regulatory approvals. The deal covers CDMO operations at Carlsbad, California, Madison and Verona, Wisconsin, bioconjugation work in St. Louis, Missouri, and Indianapolis, Indiana.

For process engineers, the deal shifts US capacity for complex modalities to a dedicated CDMO. Lonza, which employs about 17,000 people across three continents, says the acquisition adds broader capabilities and extra US manufacturing capacity in a key market. Merck keeps its consumables, technologies and services portfolio and will keep supplying pharmaceutical, biotechnology and CDMO customers, including Lonza itself.

Two boundaries matter. BioReliance Contract Testing Services is excluded and stays within Merck's Life Science portfolio. Until the deal closes, the CDMO business continues to operate inside Merck KGaA, and both firms say they will work together on program and supply continuity through the transition.
