A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal compares the activity of ferrite perovskite oxygen carriers for biogas chemical looping reforming. The work pairs experiments with density functional theory calculations to explain why the carriers differ in activity. Only the title is available at the time of writing, so the specific compositions, measured conversions and DFT outcomes cannot be reported here.

Oxygen carrier selection governs the performance of a chemical looping reforming loop, since the carrier supplies the lattice oxygen that converts the fuel and must survive repeated reduction and oxidation cycles. Ferrite perovskites are candidate carriers because their redox behaviour can be tuned through composition. A study that ties measured activity differences to DFT-level mechanisms offers a route to rational carrier screening rather than trial and error.
