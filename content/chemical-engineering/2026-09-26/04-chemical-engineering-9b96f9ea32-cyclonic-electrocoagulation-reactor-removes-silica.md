Researchers report a proof-of-concept cyclonic flow-through electrocoagulation reactor aimed at removing silica from water and limiting electrode passivation.

Electrode passivation by insulating silica layers is a known cause of efficiency loss in electrocoagulation. The cyclonic flow design uses centrifugal forces to keep particles in suspension and scour the electrode surface, which the authors frame as a way to sustain current and metal release.

The paper presents the concept and initial results rather than a scaled design. A practising engineer would note that the work is at proof-of-concept stage, and performance data on long-term operation, energy use, and residual aluminium or other metal concentrations are not detailed here.
