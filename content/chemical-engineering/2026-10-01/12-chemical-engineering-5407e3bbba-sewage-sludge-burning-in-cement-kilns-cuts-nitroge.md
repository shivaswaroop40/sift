Researchers tracked how nitrogen oxides (NO) are reduced when municipal sewage sludge is burned alongside fuel in industrial cement kilns. The mechanism involves in-situ chemical reactions within the kiln environment that convert NO to less harmful products.

For process engineers operating or designing kiln systems, understanding this reduction pathway matters because it offers a route to lower NOx emissions without requiring additional equipment like selective catalytic reduction units. This could improve compliance and reduce capital costs on existing installations.
