Researchers tested a reactor for hydrogenating liquid organic hydrogen carriers, compounds that store hydrogen chemically for transport and later release. The reactor maintained stable performance over multiple charge-discharge cycles without loss of activity.

For process design, this matters because liquid organic hydrogen carriers need reliable hydrogenation reactors for a hydrogen economy to work. Stability under cycling is essential data for scaling up from lab to commercial plants where the same equipment runs repeatedly.
