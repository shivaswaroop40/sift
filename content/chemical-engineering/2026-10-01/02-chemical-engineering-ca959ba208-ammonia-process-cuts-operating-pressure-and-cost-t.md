Ammobia Inc. has developed an ammonia synthesis process operating at 20–60 bars instead of the conventional 120–300 bars required by Haber-Bosch. The system combines synthesis and separation in one step using a proprietary adsorbent to continuously remove ammonia from the reactor as it forms, shifting the thermodynamic equilibrium.

Lower pressure allows use of standard equipment with thinner pressure vessels and smaller compressors, reducing capital cost per unit capacity. Pilot testing showed 65% conversion at 30 bars over 1,000 hours, against 15–30% for conventional processes. The company is now building a 10-ton/d demonstration unit and a 250-ton/d modular commercial plant.

For process design and operations, this matters because it breaks the economies-of-scale constraint that has locked ammonia production into world-scale plants. Regional, standardized facilities become economically viable, changing how capacity can be added and how ammonia supply chains are built.
