Researchers developed a fast Joule-heating process that extracts fluorine from persistent organofluorine waste and couples it with waste silver to produce a reusable fluorination reagent. The method avoids the typical loss of fluorine that occurs during conventional waste destruction.

For process engineers handling fluorinated compounds, this offers a circular route to recover a valuable reagent rather than destroying it. Fluorination reagents are expensive inputs, and persistent organofluorine streams—difficult to treat—become a feedstock instead of a disposal problem.
