Plug Power has launched GenEco Scale, an integrated PEM electrolyzer plant offering for projects of about 100 MW and above. It uses standardised 20-MW blocks with balance of plant included, in an outdoor-ready layout. Arcadia eFuels is the first customer, for the 280-MW hydrogen plant at Project ARC in Texas. Plug has completed the technical evaluation and FEED study.

Project ARC is designed to make about 23.2 million gal a year of e-SAF from renewable hydrogen. Arcadia already has a 280-MW electrolyzer supply agreement with Plug for Project ENDOR in Denmark.

The pitch is lower installed cost and less EPC work. Plug supplies a pre-engineered reference plant and acts as single OEM for the process plant, instead of EPCs integrating several equipment packages. Process skids and cooling equipment are stacked across levels, targeting under one acre per 100 MW. Plug says the design meets oil-and-gas process-safety standards and reduces on-site hydrogen inventory.

The source is a company announcement. It gives no cost figures, schedule, efficiency or stack durability data, so the savings are claims rather than demonstrated results. Plug cites more than 320 MW of PEM capacity shipped.
