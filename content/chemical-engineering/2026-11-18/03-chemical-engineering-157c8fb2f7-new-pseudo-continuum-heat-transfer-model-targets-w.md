A paper in Chemical Engineering Science presents a pseudo-continuum heat-transfer model for a wall-cooled packed-bed reactor with a low tube-to-particle diameter ratio. The title says the model is informed by PRS. The source gives only the title, so the expansion of that term is not stated.

Low tube-to-particle ratios are common in highly exothermic or endothermic tubular reactors, where wall heat removal controls hot-spot behaviour. Standard pseudo-continuum models often struggle there because wall effects and local bed structure matter. A better closure could improve temperature predictions and tube design.
