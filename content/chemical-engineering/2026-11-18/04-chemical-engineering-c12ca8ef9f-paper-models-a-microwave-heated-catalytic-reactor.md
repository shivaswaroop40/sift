A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal presents a coupled electromagnetic and thermal CFD model of a microwave-heated catalytic reactor for dry methane reforming. The title is the only text available. No results, geometry, operating conditions or validation data were supplied, so the findings cannot be stated here.

Dry methane reforming is strongly endothermic and needs high temperatures. Microwave heating is one proposed way to deliver that heat directly to the catalyst bed. Coupling the field solution to heat transfer is the usual route to predicting hot spots and temperature uniformity, which matter for conversion and catalyst life.

Whether the model is validated against experiment, and whether reaction kinetics are included, is not known from the source. Treat any claims as unverified until the full paper is read.
