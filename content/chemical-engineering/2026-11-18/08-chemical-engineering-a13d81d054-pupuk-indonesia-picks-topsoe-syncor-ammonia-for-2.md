Pupuk Indonesia has selected Topsoe's SynCOR Ammonia technology for a new ammonia-urea complex in West Papua. The plant will have an ammonia capacity of 2,500 metric tons per day. The licensee is Pupuk Indonesia's subsidiary, PT Pupuk Kalimantan Timur.

Topsoe will supply the technology licence, basic engineering design and proprietary equipment. A final investment decision is expected in 2027, with completion targeted for 2030. The ammonia will feed urea production for fertilizer supply to Eastern Indonesia.

The announcement says SynCOR Ammonia is designed for large-scale production, but it gives no energy, emissions or feedstock details. It does not say whether the plant is conventional or low-carbon. Pupuk Indonesia also frames the project as a step towards becoming a competitive petrochemical player.
