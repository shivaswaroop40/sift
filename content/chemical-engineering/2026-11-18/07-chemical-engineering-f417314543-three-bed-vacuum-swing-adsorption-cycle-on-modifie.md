A paper in Chemical Engineering Science describes an improved three-bed vacuum swing adsorption cycle. It uses a modified form of Al-FUM, an aluminium fumarate metal-organic framework, as the adsorbent. The stated aim is to produce high-quality biomethane and a biogenic CO2 stream from biogas.

The source text supplied is only the title. No abstract, recovery figures, purities, energy use or modelling details are available, so the paper's results cannot be reported here.

The topic matters because biogas upgrading needs both a pipeline-grade methane product and a usable CO2 stream. A cycle that targets both products suggests attention to CO2 purity as well as methane recovery. Whether the cycle achieves this, and at what energy cost, cannot be judged from the title.
