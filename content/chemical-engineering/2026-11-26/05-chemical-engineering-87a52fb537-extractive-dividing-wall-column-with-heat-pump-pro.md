A paper in Chemical Engineering Science describes an energy-saving design for dehydrating tert-butanol. It combines an extractive dividing-wall column with vapour recompression and intermediate reboiling. The source text gives only the title, so the design details, solvent and results are not available here.

Tert-butanol forms an azeotrope with water, so dehydration needs an entrainer or extractive solvent and is usually energy intensive. Combining the two extractive columns into one dividing-wall shell, and adding a heat pump and an intermediate reboiler, targets both capital and steam duty.

No savings figures, economic comparison or simulation basis are given in the text supplied. The size of any benefit over a conventional extractive sequence cannot be judged from this entry alone.
