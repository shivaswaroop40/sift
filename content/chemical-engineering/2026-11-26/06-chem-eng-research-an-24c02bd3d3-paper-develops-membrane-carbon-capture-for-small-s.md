A paper in Chemical Engineering Research and Design describes a carbon capture system for small-scale hydrogen production from natural gas. The system uses gas membrane separation to remove CO2. The source text gives only the title, so no performance figures, membrane type or process conditions are available.

Small-scale steam methane reforming units are often too modest for conventional amine absorption to be economic. Membranes are modular and have no solvent loop, so they are a plausible fit for such sites. That is the likely motivation, though the source does not state it.
