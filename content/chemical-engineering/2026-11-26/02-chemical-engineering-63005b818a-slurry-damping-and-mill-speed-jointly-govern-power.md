A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal reports that slurry damping and mill speed together control power transfer, ore breakage and liner wear in full-scale wet semi-autogenous grinding (SAG) mills. That is the full extent of the source text, which gives only the title.

The title implies that slurry in the charge cushions impacts, and that this interacts with rotational speed. Operators who adjust speed or slurry content to trade throughput against liner life may therefore be changing several outcomes at once.

The source gives no figures, mill dimensions, test method or model. It is unclear whether the work is based on simulation, plant measurement or both. Any claim about the size of the effects cannot be assessed from this text.
