CVR Energy has signed a definitive agreement to buy Marathon Petroleum's St. Paul Park, Minnesota, and Mandan, North Dakota, refineries plus the related marketing business for about $4.2 billion, plus net working capital. St. Paul Park runs 105,000 barrels per day and Mandan 72,000. The deal lifts CVR's crude throughput capacity from roughly 207,000 to 384,000 barrels per day. Closing is expected by the end of 2026, subject to Hart-Scott-Rodino clearance.

The acquired business generated about $1.2 billion of EBITDA in the trailing 12 months to 30 September 2026, adjusted to CVR's accounting policies. On a pro forma basis, CVR's nine-month EBITDA would have risen from about $900 million to about $2.0 billion, and earnings per share from $3.73 to $7.67, accretion of over 105%. CVR expects about $70 million of annual run-rate synergies, mainly in SG&A and commercial and operational areas. The refineries average a complexity of 7.8 and ran at 98% utilisation from 2023 to 2025, excluding turnarounds.

Financing combines $2.5 billion of committed secured debt, a $500 million equity commitment from CVR's controlling shareholder, an $800 million equity investment from Trafigura, and cash on hand. A Trafigura affiliate will also enter long-term commercial arrangements with CVR subsidiaries. The existing 7.500% notes due 2031 and 7.875% notes due 2034 become secured on an equal basis with the new debt, and the 5.750% notes due 2028 will be redeemed. The marketing business supplies up to 590 branded locations, and its high blending capacity reduces exposure to volatile RIN costs.
