Evonik has invested €52 million to expand its silicone platform at the Goldschmidtstrasse site in Essen, Germany. The project adds a new production plant and a tank farm, raising capacity for organomodified silicones. A 150-metre pipe bridge links four production units and 15 manufacturing assets to the new storage area, which holds five stainless-steel tanks, the tallest at 24 metres. The site grew from 32 to 35 hectares. Work proceeded while the plant stayed fully operational.

The additives made there go into polyurethane foams for insulation, mattresses and automotive seating, plus adhesives, coatings and personal care products. Evonik says the tank farm cuts the need to move intermediates and finished products between its sites in Germany, Italy, China and the United States, reducing bottlenecks and dependence on short-term deliveries. For formulators and buyers, that points to steadier supply across the network.

The project also signals where Evonik intends to concentrate future work. Essen Goldschmidtstrasse is designated a centre of excellence for customer-specific formulations, one of six major German sites now given a defined long-term profile. Around 80 Evonik employees and more than 230 contractors from 25 partner companies delivered the build.
