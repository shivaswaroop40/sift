Researchers report scaling up a thermally controlled absorber packing for CO2 capture from point sources. The process intensification packing technology uses temperature management within the column to shift the absorption equilibrium and reduce solvent regeneration energy. The work moves the concept from bench scale toward larger unit sizes.

For process engineers in carbon capture, the appeal is lower energy duty in the reboiler, which is the largest operating cost in amine scrubbing. A drop in regeneration heat directly improves the economics of post-combustion capture on flue gas from power and industrial emitters.
