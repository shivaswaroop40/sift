A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal reports a heterogeneous Ru/La2O3-CeO2 catalyst for hydrogenolysis of low-density polyethylene (LDPE) into valuable chemicals, claiming high activity and selectivity.

The work targets plastic waste valorisation through catalytic hydrogenolysis rather than incineration or mechanical recycling, a route relevant to chemical engineers designing chemical-recycling processes and new catalyst formulations.

Only the title is available from this source, so the specific conversion, product slate and reaction conditions are not stated and the claimed performance cannot be verified from the text provided.

Readers should consult the full paper for the catalyst preparation method, operating conditions, and quantitative selectivity data before drawing conclusions about process viability.
