The paper reports catalytic pyrolysis of polystyrene over acidic zeolite materials to produce BTEX aromatics (benzene, toluene, ethylbenzene and xylenes). The abstract indicates zeolite acidity is the key parameter steering the vapour upgrading step from a polymer waste stream towards a defined aromatic slate.

For a process engineer, the result matters because polystyrene is a large, fairly homogeneous waste stream, and BTEX are established drop-in feedstocks for styrene, para-xylene and solvent markets. The work positions zeolite catalysis as the selectivity lever rather than the pyrolysis reactor itself.

The text available is only the title, so yields, catalyst lifetime, deactivation by coking, and comparison against non-acidic or hierarchical zeolites cannot be reported here. Read the full paper for those numbers and the reaction conditions used.
