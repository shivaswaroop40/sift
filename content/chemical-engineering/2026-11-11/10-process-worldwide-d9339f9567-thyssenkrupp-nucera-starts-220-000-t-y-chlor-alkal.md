Thyssenkrupp Nucera has commissioned a chlor-alkali plant for Kutch Chemical at Padana, Gujarat. It uses five electrolysers based on the BM Generation 6+ technology. Capacity is up to 675 t/d of 100 % NaOH, or 220,000 t/y. Nucera supplied engineering, proprietary technology, equipment and start-up support. The electrolysers have started operating.

Each electrolyser can make up to 135 t/d of 100 % NaOH. Nucera says this is the highest-capacity single electrolyser installed in India. The plant is described as one of the largest single-train chlor-alkali sites in the country, which matters for anyone weighing unit size against plant footprint and spares strategy.

Kutch Chemical will use the caustic soda and chlorine in its own chlorination, nitration and dye intermediate operations, and sell the surplus in India. The relationship dates from 2017, with the site expanded in stages. The release gives no figures for energy consumption, membrane performance or cost, so the efficiency and reliability claims for Generation 6+ are not quantified.
