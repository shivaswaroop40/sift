A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal describes a method for fast parameter identification in elementary-step surface reaction mechanisms. The approach uses kinetically parameterized neural networks. The title is the only text available, so the details of the method are not known.

Fitting rate parameters for elementary-step mechanisms is a routine bottleneck in heterogeneous catalysis modelling. Stiff, coupled rate equations make regression slow and sensitive to starting guesses. A faster route to parameter estimates would matter to anyone building microkinetic models.

The source gives no data on speed-up, accuracy, test systems or validation against experiment. Any claim of performance cannot be assessed from what is provided, and the abstract or full paper would be needed to judge it.
