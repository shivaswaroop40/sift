A paper in Chemical Engineering Journal describes a plate-column combined micro-stirred reactor built for high-viscosity systems. The design pairs a plate column with micro-scale stirring to intensify mixing. The authors apply the reactor to the continuous synthesis of trinitrobenzyl chloride, TNBCl.

Viscous feeds are a known weak point for microreactors. Narrow channels rely on diffusion, so thick media mix slowly and foul more readily. Stirring inside a plate-column format is one way to keep the heat transfer and small inventory of flow processing while mixing viscous streams. Continuous operation also suits hazardous chemistry, because it holds less reactive material at any moment than a batch vessel.
