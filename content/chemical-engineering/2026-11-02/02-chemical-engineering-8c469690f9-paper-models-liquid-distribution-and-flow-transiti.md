Chemical Engineering Science has published a study of liquid distribution in a pilot-scale micro-packed bed reactor. The paper characterises how liquid spreads through the bed, examines the transition between flow modes, and proposes a model for the velocity profile. Only the title was available to this digest, so the specific findings, operating conditions and validation results cannot be reported here.

Liquid distribution governs wetting and contacting in packed beds, and maldistribution is a familiar cause of lost performance at scale. Work at pilot scale, rather than bench scale, speaks directly to that problem. A velocity profile model, if validated, would let designers predict distribution rather than measure it after the fact.
