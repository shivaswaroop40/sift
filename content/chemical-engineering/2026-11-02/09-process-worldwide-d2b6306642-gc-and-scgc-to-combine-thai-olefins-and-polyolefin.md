PTT Global Chemical (GC) and SCG Chemicals (SCGC) plan to combine their olefins and polyolefins businesses in Thailand. The joint venture would hold roughly 7.1 million tonnes per year of olefins capacity, placing it among the world's ten largest polyolefins producers and in a leading position in ASEAN. The companies expect synergies of about 150 to 200 million dollars per year, progressively realised within around three years.

The combination matters to anyone tracking Asian polymer supply. GC brings a flexible feedstock mix of naphtha, LPG, propane and ethane, plus a refinery business that secures feedstock supply. SCGC adds high value-added products and its i2P Center, which turns customer problems into market-ready products. Together they aim to serve automotive, solar, construction and specialty industrial markets from a stronger upstream base.

The plan builds on Map Ta Phut as Thailand's petrochemical base, with the stated aim of developing it into a regional manufacturing hub. The press release gives no deal value, ownership split or completion timeline, so the structure remains unclear. The synergy and ranking claims are the companies' own projections.
