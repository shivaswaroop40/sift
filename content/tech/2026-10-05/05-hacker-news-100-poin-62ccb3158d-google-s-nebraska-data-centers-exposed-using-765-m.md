Google's three Nebraska data centres redacted resource usage figures as trade secrets in mandatory reports to state regulators. A reporter revealed the hidden data by copying text from PDF files. Lincoln's Agate facility reported 52.65 megawatts peak electricity demand and 13.3 million gallons annual water use. The three sites combined expect $117.6 million in tax refunds.

The disclosure matters because data centre water and power consumption directly affects regional infrastructure and grid strain. Nebraska regulators mandated reporting precisely to assess these impacts. Google claimed the figures were confidential, yet the redaction mechanism failed trivially, making secrecy claims look questionable.

Six reporting data centres collectively used 765 million gallons of water last year. Papillion's Fireball facility used the most at 548 million gallons. Agate's facility footprint is 288,530 square feet, roughly five American football fields.
