Google has signed a 20-year power purchase agreement with Constellation Energy for 3,590 megawatts of electricity, including 890 megawatts from upgrading turbines, steam generators, and digital controls at eleven existing nuclear plants across three states. Constellation will invest $4.3 billion and aims to complete the first uprated reactor by 2028.

Hyperscalers face an acute power constraint: Google is spending $205 billion on datacentres and compute this year, but cannot operate them without electricity. Uprating existing plants bypasses the multi-year timelines and grid interconnection bottlenecks that greenfield nuclear construction faces.

Microsoft has similarly contracted Constellation to restart Three Mile Island Unit 1, expected online next year. Meta has committed to 6.6 gigawatts of nuclear and clean energy, though this figure depends on unproven small modular reactors reaching scale.
