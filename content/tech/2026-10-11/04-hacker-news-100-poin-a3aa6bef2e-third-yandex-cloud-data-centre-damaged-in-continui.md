Yandex Cloud reported damage to a third data centre facility on its status page, following earlier incidents at other sites. The company has not disclosed the extent of damage or service impact in public statements.

For teams running workloads on Yandex Cloud, this represents ongoing infrastructure risk in a region experiencing active conflict. Customers in Russia or Eastern Europe relying on Yandex for critical systems should assess their disaster recovery and failover readiness.

The pattern of multiple data centre hits suggests targeted or widespread strikes rather than isolated incidents, though Yandex has not detailed which services are affected or recovery timelines.
