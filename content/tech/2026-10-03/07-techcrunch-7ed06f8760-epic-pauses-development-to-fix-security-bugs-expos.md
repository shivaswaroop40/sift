Epic, which maintains records for 320 million patients across US hospitals, has paused most product development for six weeks. The pause follows deployment of an AI cybersecurity tool that discovered flaws allowing unauthorised access to patient data without logging intrusions. Epic's chief security officer confirmed some MyChart configurations could leak records.

The bugs matter because they affect widely deployed software across hundreds of healthcare providers. If exploited, attackers could access multiple systems simultaneously and steal sensitive health data from millions of people. Healthcare data commands high ransoms, as shown by the 2024 Change Healthcare breach affecting 192 million people.

The discovery highlights how AI tools can rapidly find vulnerabilities that might otherwise go undetected. The pause is unusual but reflects genuine concern that attackers now have similar capabilities to find and exploit flaws. Epic has not disclosed whether the bugs allow data alteration without detection.
