The Sovereign Tech Agency is investing almost €1.3m in KDE through 2027. At Akademy 2026 in Graz, contributors Nate Graham and Kevin Ottens explained how the deal was secured and what the money will fund.

For open source projects chasing institutional funding, the talk matters because STA is one of the few public bodies that writes cheques for desktop and infrastructure software. The talk gives a worked example of how a mature community prepared a proposal, scoped deliverables, and matched money to ongoing work rather than hiring new initiatives.

The plan is to improve KDE for everyone, not just enterprise customers. Specifics of which subsystems or maintenance tasks are paid for were not in the source.

The source text is truncated, so the exact scope of deliverables and the percentage that goes to maintainer pay are not stated.
