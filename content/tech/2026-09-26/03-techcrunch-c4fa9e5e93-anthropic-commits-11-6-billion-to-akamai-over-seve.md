Anthropic will pay Akamai $11.6 billion over seven years for cloud infrastructure, Akamai said on Thursday, its largest deal ever and more than six times the $1.8 billion arrangement Bloomberg reported in May. The commitment is conditional on Akamai meeting delivery and availability targets, and either party can exit under certain terms.

The deal leans on CPUs rather than GPUs, a less-hyped slice of AI infrastructure that has gained demand as agents take on tasks like code execution and web browsing. Akamai did not say what Anthropic will run on the capacity.

Revenue will not arrive this year. Akamai expects $150 million to $300 million in the second half of 2027, scaling to about $1.7 billion annually by the end of 2028. To deliver, Akamai will spend roughly $5.5 billion on buildout and add about $1.7 billion to this year's capital expenditure to pre-buy memory and components.

Akamai also issued Anthropic a warrant for nonvoting preferred stock convertible into 7.7 million common shares, or about 5% of the company, at $111.33 per share. About 2% vests on first payment, with each additional $3 billion in Anthropic spend unlocking roughly another 1%, potentially taking total commitment to about $20 billion. Akamai shares rose up to 17% after hours.
