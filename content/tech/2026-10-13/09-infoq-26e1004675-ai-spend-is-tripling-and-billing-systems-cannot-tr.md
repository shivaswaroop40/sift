Erik Peterson, CTO of CloudZero, reports that AI costs are eclipsing cloud bills across organisations, with spending roughly tripled and continuing upward. Token prices have fallen but total expenditure has risen sharply. Even companies built to track cloud costs struggle to audit AI billing accurately.

For engineering leaders and platform teams, this matters because AI infrastructure costs are now a material line item that existing billing and showback systems cannot measure or allocate properly. The economics have shifted faster than the tooling.

Peterson highlights tokenmaxxing, the practice of maximising token consumption as a vanity metric, as potentially costing between five and fifteen billion dollars industry-wide. Many managers tracked AI adoption by spending levels rather than actual outcomes, creating perverse incentives.
