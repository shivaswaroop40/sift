Ukrainian forces struck three Yandex datacenters across Russia between October 7 and 9. The Sasovo site suffered total shutdown. The Vladimir datacenter, which hosts Yandex Pay, went offline. Network connectivity to Yandex Cloud fell to 30 percent of normal levels.

Yandex operates Russia's largest cloud platform and payments service, comparable to Google Pay or AWS. Outages affect both individual users unable to access funds and enterprises relying on Yandex Cloud for hosting. Engineers are working to restore the most-used services first, prioritising Mail, Maps, Search, Taxi and Alice AI.

Yandex Pay users cannot access their money, though the company states funds are safe. The company has recommended customers use alternative hosting providers. Full restoration timelines remain unknown, with the team working around the clock.
