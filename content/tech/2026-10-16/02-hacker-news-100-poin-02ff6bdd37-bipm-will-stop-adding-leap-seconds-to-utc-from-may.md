The International Bureau of Weights and Measures has decided to end leap second adjustments to UTC effective 20 May 2027. UTC will become a continuous time scale, with the maximum drift from Earth rotation (UT1-UTC) capped at 3,600 seconds, or one hour.

Leap seconds have caused increasing problems for distributed systems and critical infrastructure. A negative leap second, never applied before, now has a 30 per cent probability by 2035, posing high risk to unprepared systems. Different mitigation strategies across providers create timing inconsistencies that violate SI standards.

The decision accelerates a 2022 commitment. The workshop noted that preparation costs for industry match those of millennium bug remediation. Delay risked users abandoning UTC for GNSS-based time scales, fracturing international standardization.
