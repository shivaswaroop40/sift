N-able has taken its cloud-hosted N-Central remote management service offline to fix vulnerabilities that a researcher, Ben Bales, published on GitHub on Sunday. The company had no advance notice. Bales calls the bugs Able2Shell. He describes them as a mix of flaws that can steal credentials, create new administrative accounts and give root access.

N-able also urged customers to take down their self-hosted N-Central deployments, because the public exploits could let attackers into customer networks. While the service is down, customers cannot use it to remotely access and manage devices on their networks.

Bales told Bleeping Computer he published the bugs because N-able allegedly failed to patch flaws he had reported in a different product. That is his account, and the article does not give N-able's side on it.

N-able says it has no evidence of successful exploitation. It did not say whether it can check, for example through server logs, or whether it has no way to check. Without that, the statement says little.
