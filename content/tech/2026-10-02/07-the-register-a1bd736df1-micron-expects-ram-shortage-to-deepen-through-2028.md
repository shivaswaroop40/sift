Micron's CEO said RAM supply will remain tight through 2028, with demand outpacing supply in both 2027 and 2028. The company has already sold most next year's production and will charge much higher prices. New factories coming online in 2028 will not immediately ease availability.

For anyone buying compute capacity or building infrastructure, this signals sustained pricing pressure on DRAM and HBM for the next two years. High-bandwidth memory for AI accelerators is especially constrained. Plan procurement and capital spend accordingly.

Micron's gross margins tell the real story. DRAM datacenter margins hit 90 percent in Q4, up from 41 percent a year prior. HBM margins jumped from 59 to 83 percent. The company is capitalising on scarcity, not racing to relieve it.
