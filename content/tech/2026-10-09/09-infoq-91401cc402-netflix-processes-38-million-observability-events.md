Netflix ingests 38 million telemetry events per second across 65 million concurrent streams and two billion daily requests. The company is replacing reactive alerting with an AI-driven operational ontology that unifies metrics, events, logs, and traces into queryable knowledge graphs. The system uses Claude and graph databases to automate issue detection, prioritisation, triaging, root-cause analysis, and self-healing workflows.

At this scale, correlating user experience with infrastructure performance becomes a data engineering problem rather than a tooling problem. Traditional monitoring flags issues after they occur; Netflix aims to detect anomalies across the entire stack within minutes, automatically route them to responsible teams, and predict failures before they impact users. The presentation focuses on moving from reactive incident response to proactive insights engines that operate on unified telemetry data.
