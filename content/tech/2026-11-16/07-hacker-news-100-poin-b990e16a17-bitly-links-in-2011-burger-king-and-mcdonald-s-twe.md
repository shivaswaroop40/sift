On 15 November 2026 someone re-registered the endings of old Bitly short links and pointed them at Wikipedia pages. Burger King's 2011 tweet link went to a list of Nazi concentration camps. A McDonald's 2011 link went to the September 11 attacks article. Neither company's X account was compromised. The links changed hands on Bitly's side.

Ransomnews checked 97 Bitly links in Trump's 2009 to 2013 tweets. Forty are original. Forty-nine were re-created later but still lead where the tweets suggest, 48 of them in a 17-minute run in May 2023. Seven lead elsewhere, four registered on 15 November. One more is blocked as malicious or spam. CNN reported Gates, Trudeau, Pence, the FBI and the Department of Defense were also affected. Ransomnews did not verify those.

Bitly's help pages say a bit.ly ending cannot be reused, even after the account is deleted. A bug allowing reuse was reportedly fixed in February 2025. Bitly said on X that bad actors repurposed old, inactive links. It has suspended accounts and is disabling the affected links. The 49 re-created links that look like the originals still work.

The method of dating is simple. Adding a plus sign to a Bitly link shows its creation time and destination. Ransomnews tested it on links with known histories and found it consistent. It suspects one actor behind the first two changes but says nothing it has seen proves that.
