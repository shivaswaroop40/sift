Nvidia has told manufacturing partners, including Dell and Super Micro, to pause server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia, according to a Wall Street Journal report relayed by TechCrunch. The pause has no stated end date. Nvidia is assessing whether its regional partners meet US export requirements.

The reported cause is remote access. Chinese companies have been using Nvidia chips in data centres in these countries, a route the US Commerce Department closed earlier this month. The report says it did so reportedly grudgingly.

The Institute for Progress estimates that remote data centres like these supply about 24% of China's AI computing power. That figure is an estimate from a think tank, not a measured number.
