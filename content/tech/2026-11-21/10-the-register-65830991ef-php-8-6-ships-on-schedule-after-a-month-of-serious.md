PHP 8.6 was released on Thursday on the language's usual yearly schedule. The release follows a month of scrutiny. Since October, a run of newly found vulnerabilities has let unauthenticated attackers crash PHP workers or execute arbitrary code on the server.

Users of PHP-based applications, including WordPress, Laravel and Symfony, had to update their PHP instances quickly. The headline suggests 8.6 makes the language stricter, but the source text is truncated before it explains what changes.
