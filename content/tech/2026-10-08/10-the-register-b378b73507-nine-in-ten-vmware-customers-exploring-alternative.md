A Unisphere Research survey of 269 VMware users found 90 percent cited higher licensing costs as reason to evaluate alternatives, and 54 percent pointed to the end of perpetual license support. Broadcom's May 2025 price increases were reported to reach eight to fifteen times previous levels.

For platform engineers running distributed systems, this matters because mass migration away from VMware reshapes infrastructure choices and vendor lock-in risks. Forty-eight percent of respondents have no plans to adopt Broadcom's preferred subscription platform, VMware Cloud Foundation.

Barriers to migration remain substantial: 40 percent cited operational complexity, 38 percent multi-vendor management challenges, and 37 percent each cited security and team skills gaps. Sixty percent are considering a multi-hypervisor strategy to reduce dependence.
