On 23 October, ISO declined to ratify the document Working Group 21 presented as C++26. ISO has approved seven C++ revisions since 1998. This is the first time it has rejected a draft from the working group, and the article says the standard's future is now in doubt.

The article's title ties the dispute to contracts, a proposed language feature. The source text provided is truncated, so it does not set out the objections, who raised them, or what happens next.
