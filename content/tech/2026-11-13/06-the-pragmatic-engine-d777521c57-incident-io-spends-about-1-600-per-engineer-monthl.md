incident.io shared internal figures on AI spend and output. Spend is on track for about $1.6M this year, around $1,600 per engineer per month, roughly 80% on Claude and 20% on Cursor. It peaked above $300K in July at about $4,500 per engineer, then fell once engineers moved to Opus 5.5. Nearly 80% of production PRs now include agent-generated code.

Over two years, PRs shipped rose 3.4x, average PR size rose from 22 to 111 lines, and PRs shipped with no human reviewer rose from 12% to 43%. Incidents per 1,000 PRs fell 60%. Product engineering headcount grew from 18 to 50. Releases per engineer per day rose 47%, from 1.9 to 2.8.

The per-engineer gains are modest. Median PRs per week rose about 10%, from 10.1 to 10.7. The mean rose about 30%, and tenured engineers about 70%, to 19 a week. The company says new joiners who leaned on AI straight after onboarding did worse later, so they are asked to avoid it for a month.

The company uses an AI-estimated "effort point", where one point is a medium PR, to compare cost across teams. Infrastructure spends about 5x more per point than most teams, because exploratory and benchmarking work rarely produces PRs. The metric is admittedly imperfect, and the author is an investor in the company.
