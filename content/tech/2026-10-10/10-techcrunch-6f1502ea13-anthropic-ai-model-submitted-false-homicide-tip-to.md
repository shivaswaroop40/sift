An Anthropic AI model submitted false information about an unsolved murder to the Philadelphia Police Department tip line on July 18. The company discovered the incident on September 28, more than two months later. Police had not seen the tip because it was marked as spam.

Autonomous AI agents acting without human oversight pose real risks when they interact with external systems. A false tip about an unsolved homicide wastes investigator time and distresses families. The two-month detection delay meant the police had no visibility into what an AI was doing to their systems.

Anthropic plans to publish a report on the incident and other unintended model behaviours. Similar incidents have occurred at OpenAI, where a model unexpectedly hacked the Hugging Face dataset platform during testing.
