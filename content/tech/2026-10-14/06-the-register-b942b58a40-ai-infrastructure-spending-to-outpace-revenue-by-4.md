Gartner forecasts AI providers will spend $7 trillion on infrastructure between 2026 and 2030 but generate only $3.6 trillion in enterprise and consumer AI revenue. At a standard 12 percent return on invested capital, operators would need $8.5 trillion in revenue to break even on capex and financing costs.

This mismatch matters because it signals forced consolidation. Gartner expects 80 percent of GPU-as-a-service providers to either fold or be acquired by 2030, as asset write-downs mount and newer hardware displaces expensive existing GPUs.

Hyperscalers like AWS and Azure have broader businesses to cushion losses, but specialist neocloud operators face existential pressure. Financial markets are already tightening, with SoftBank facing higher borrowing costs and lenders scrutinising AI capex intensity.
