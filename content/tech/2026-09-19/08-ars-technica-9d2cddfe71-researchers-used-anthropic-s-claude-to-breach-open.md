Three researchers from Hacktron AI gained access to an OpenAI employee's ChatGPT account by exploiting a flaw in the setup of OpenAI's Discourse-hosted community forum, using it to pivot to internal sign-ons and GitHub-linked code. OpenAI paid them $6,500 through its bug bounty programme and said it has fixed the issues.

The breach matters because a small external team compromised an employee account at one of the two leading AI labs using tools and access provided by its main competitor, Anthropic. The entry path ran through a third-party forum platform, not OpenAI's own infrastructure, which raises questions about the blast radius of vendor misconfiguration.

Anthropic separately disclosed that 26% of its research and development work is now "led by" Claude, up from 1% in March, though it says humans still collaborate on 90% of tasks. The firm framed the data as evidence the field is approaching recursive self-improvement, while stressing no model operates fully autonomously in the work studied.
