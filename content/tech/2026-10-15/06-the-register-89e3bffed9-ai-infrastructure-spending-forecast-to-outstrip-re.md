Gartner estimates AI datacenter operators will spend $7 trillion between 2026 and 2030 but generate only $3.6 trillion in revenue, leaving a shortfall of $4.4 trillion against the $8.5 trillion needed for a 12 percent return on invested capital. The firm expects 80 percent of neocloud GPU-as-a-service providers to disappear as standalone companies by 2030.

The mismatch forces asset write-downs as newer GPU generations displace older hardware and operators cannot recoup their capital investment. Hyperscalers with diversified revenue streams will survive better than specialist neocloud operators dependent on AI services alone.

This conclusion aligns with warnings from McKinsey, Jefferies, and Bain that AI companies face commoditization, price undercutting from open source and Chinese models, and pressure to develop new use cases. Both the IMF and Bank for International Settlements have flagged risks of an AI bubble collapsing and spreading financial contagion.
