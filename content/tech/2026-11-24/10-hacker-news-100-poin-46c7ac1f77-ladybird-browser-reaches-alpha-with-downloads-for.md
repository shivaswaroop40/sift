The Ladybird project has released an alpha build of its browser, available to download for macOS and Linux. Andreas Kling says the engine is the project's own, built from the ground up and not a fork. The browser shell around it is described as modest but functional.

The alpha is a rolling channel with regular automatic updates. Until now, anyone wanting to try Ladybird had to build it from source. Users can report broken sites from inside the browser and review crash reports before choosing whether to send them.

The post is candid about limits. Some sites will not work and crashes are expected. The team makes no promises about security and invites people to attack it. It gives no figures on compatibility, performance or test pass rates.
